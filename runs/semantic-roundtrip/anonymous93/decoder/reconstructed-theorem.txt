RECONSTRUCTED THEOREM — SOURCE-BLIND RAW SEMANTICS

Parameters and hypotheses.
E is any finite-dimensional real inner-product space, with its normed additive commutative group structure and a measurable structure satisfying BorelSpace. Its dimension may be zero. V:E→R is twice continuously differentiable. α,β are nonnegative reals, α>0 and α≤β. For every x,v∈E the second Frechet derivative obeys
 α||v||² ≤ D²V(x)[v,v] ≤ β||v||².
η is a real number satisfying η>0 and βη≤1. The quantified Hessian bounds are assumptions on all x,v. No assumption on third or higher derivatives occurs. All constructions below depend on this fixed E,V,η; α,β restrict admissible inputs but do not occur in the explicit trajectory formulas.

Definitions.
Write z=(x,v)∈E×E and g=∇V. For any y,r∈E, let c=y−ηg(r) and define, for every real t,
 Φ(y,r,t,(x,v))=(c+cos(t)(x−c)+sqrt(η)sin(t)v,
                  −sin(t)/sqrt(η)(x−c)+cos(t)v).
Define δ=g(x)−g(r), S(r,(x,v))=(x,v−[2<v,δ>/||δ||²]δ), and λ(r,(x,v))=sqrt(η)max(0,<v,δ>). Real division is total, so when δ=0 the coefficient is zero and S leaves z unchanged. The displayed λ includes sqrt(η) exactly. Time in Φ is its displayed real argument without an additional hidden rescaling.
For t∈R≥0, Λ(y,r,z,t)=∫_0^t λ(r,Φ(y,r,s,z)) ds, a real interval Bochner integral. For e∈R≥0 let τ(y,r,z,e)∈WithTop(R≥0) be the infimum of t≥0 with Λ(y,r,z,t)−e≥0; if no such time exists it is infinity. This is a threshold-entry infimum, not a supremum or a strict-threshold crossing.
Let Rec=(R≥0×(E×E))⊕Unit. Denote its live elements by Live(a,z) and its unique dead element by Dead. next(y,r,e,Dead)=Dead. For Live(a,z), next returns Dead if τ(y,r,z,e)=infinity; otherwise it returns Live(a+τ,S(r,Φ(y,r,τ,z))). The formal finite extraction uses untopD 0 only in this finite branch. Define R_0=Live(0,z0) and R_(k+1)=next(y,r,e_k,R_k) for any nonnegative threshold sequence e. These are finite recursions, defined on every input sequence.
The probability space is Ω=N→R with P=⊗_(k∈N)Exp(rate=1). The thresholds used by the recursion are ε_k(w)=max(w_k,0), as nonnegative reals. The raw Ω includes all real sequences, including negative entries, zeros, and atypical sequences. H_n(w)=(R_j(y,r,z0,ε(w)))_(j=0,...,n) is the full finite tagged history in Hist_n=Fin(n+1)→Rec.

Finite prefixes and densities.
For n∈N and T∈R≥0 let Δ_(n,T)={t∈R^n: the tuple (0,t_0,...,t_(n−1)) is strictly increasing and every t_i<T}. Prefix_(n,T) is this subtype, and m_(n,T) is the comap of n-dimensional product Lebesgue measure along its inclusion. This measure is not normalized. Put u_0=0 and u_(i+1)=t_i. Define z_0(t)=z0 and z_(i+1)(t)=S(r,Φ(y,r,u_(i+1)−u_i,z_i(t))) for 0≤i<n. Then K_n(t)_j=Live(max(u_j,0),z_j(t)); on this prefix domain u_j≥0 so max has no effect.
Define
 qReal_n(t)=∏_(i=0)^(n−1) [λ(r,Φ(y,r,u_(i+1)−u_i,z_i(t))) exp(−Λ(y,r,z_i(t),max(u_(i+1)−u_i,0)))],
 q_n(t)=ENNReal.ofReal(qReal_n(t)).
All differences inside these factors are positive on Prefix_(n,T). The event rate factor is evaluated immediately before the reflection, whereas z_(i+1) is the post-reflection state.
For a record, readTime(Live(a,z))=a as a real and readTime(Dead)=0; liveTime(Live(a,z))=a as a nonnegative real and liveTime(Dead)=0. Let A_(n,T) consist of histories whose every entry is live, whose readTime tuple is strictly increasing, and whose entries at indices 1,...,n have times strictly below T. A alone does not require that the first record has time zero or the correct initial state; H_n and K_n supply that initialization.
Let t_j(w) be the live time of R_j as a WithTop nonnegative real, or infinity when R_j is dead. Define
 I_(T,n)={w:t_n(w)≤T<t_(n+1)(w)}.
N_T(w) is the least n with w∈I_(T,n), if one exists, and is zero otherwise. It is a total natural-valued definition; its raw default does not certify that zero events occurred.
For h∈Hist_n put d_(n,T)(h)=T−liveTime(h_n), using truncated nonnegative subtraction. survival(Live(a,z),u,e) means u<τ(y,r,z,max(e,0)); survival(Dead,u,e) is false. weight(Live(a,z),u)=ENNReal.ofReal(exp(−Λ(y,r,z,u))); weight(Dead,u)=0. Define
 C_(n,T)={w:H_n(w)∈A_(n,T) and survival(R_n(w),d_(n,T)(H_n(w)),w_n)}.
The final survival uses the unused coordinate w_n after n transitions driven by w_0,...,w_(n−1).
Let Path_T be the full function space [0,T]_(R≥0)→E×E with its coordinate-product sigma-algebra. For any h∈Hist_n, define Ψ_n(h)(s) by starting an accumulator at z0 and scanning j=0,...,n in increasing order. At a live record Live(a,z) with a≤s, replace the accumulator by Φ(y,r,s−a,z), using truncated nonnegative subtraction; otherwise retain it. Thus the last eligible index wins, including on malformed or unordered histories. Dead records do nothing. Define Γ_n(t)=Ψ_n(K_n(t)) and
 ρ_n(t)=q_n(t) weight(K_n(t)_n,d_(n,T)(K_n(t))).
On valid prefixes this is the product of the n pre-event rate and survival factors, followed by exp(−Λ(y,r,z_n(t),T−u_n)). It is an extended-nonnegative density for an unnormalized event-count component.

Conclusion and exact quantifier order.
There exists ONE function Z:E→E→(E×E)→R≥0→Ω→(E×E), chosen after E,V,α,β,η and their hypotheses, with all of the following properties simultaneously.
(1) Z is jointly measurable in (y,r,z0,s,w) with the displayed product measurable structures.
(2) For EVERY y,r,z0,w,s,n and a∈R≥0×(E×E), if w∈I_(s,n) and R_n(w)=Live(a.time,a.state), then Z(y,r,z0,s,w)=Φ(y,r,s−a.time,a.state), where subtraction is nonnegative truncated subtraction. These are raw, pointwise implications, not almost-everywhere claims.
(3) For EVERY y,r,z0,w,s, if there is no n with w∈I_(s,n), then Z(y,r,z0,s,w)=z0. This explicitly totalizes unbracketed times.
(4) For EVERY fixed y,r,z0, Z(y,r,z0,0,w)=z0 for P-almost every w. The statement does not assert this initialization for every raw w.
(5) For EVERY n∈N and T∈R≥0, Ψ_n, Γ_n and ρ_n are jointly measurable in (y,r,z0,h), (y,r,z0,t) and (y,r,z0,t), respectively. The output sigma-algebra of Ψ_n and Γ_n is the coordinate-product sigma-algebra on Path_T.
For each such n,T and EVERY fixed y,r,z0, set H=H_n and ZT(w)(s)=Z(y,r,z0,s,w). Then ALL of (6)–(14) hold:
(6) H, ZT and N_T are measurable, and C_(n,T) is measurable.
(7) For EVERY t∈Prefix_(n,T), s∈[0,T] and j∈{0,...,n}, if u_j≤s and s<u_k for every k>j, then Γ_n(t)(s)=Φ(y,r,s−u_j,z_j(t)), with the real difference s−u_j. This includes the event instant s=u_j and uses the post-event state there.
(8) For EVERY raw w∈C_(n,T), Ψ_n(H(w))=ZT(w) as full functions on [0,T]. Thus this equality holds for all path times simultaneously on that event.
(9) For P-almost every w, one single conjunction holds: every positive-index t_j(w) differs from T; for EVERY k∈N, N_T(w)=k iff w∈C_(k,T); both {j>0:t_j(w)<T} and {j>0:t_j(w)≤T} are finite; and N_T(w) equals the natural cardinality of each of those two sets. T,y,r,z0 are fixed outside this AE quantifier. All positive j and all k are quantified inside it.
(10) The events C_(i,T) and C_(j,T) are disjoint as raw sets whenever i≠j.
(11) P(⋃_(k∈N) C_(k,T))=1.
(12) The following equality is of unnormalized measures on Hist_n×Path_T:
 pushforward_(w↦(H(w),ZT(w))) [P restricted to {w:N_T(w)=n}]
 = pushforward_(t↦(K_n(t),Γ_n(t))) [m_(n,T) with density ρ_n].
(13) For EVERY measurable F:Hist_n×Path_T→[0,infinity],
 ∫_{N_T=n} F(H(w),ZT(w)) dP(w)
 =∫_{Prefix_(n,T)} F(K_n(t),Γ_n(t)) ρ_n(t) dm_(n,T)(t).
These are extended nonnegative Lebesgue integrals and require no finite integrability assumption.
(14) ∫ρ_n dm_(n,T)=P{w:N_T(w)=n}, and P{w:N_T(w)=n}≤1.

Scope and totalization boundaries.
The existence of Z and its measurability is simultaneous in all parameters and times. The fixed-parameter/fixed-horizon AE clauses do not assert one null set uniform over uncountably many initial states, parameters or horizons. In particular, the no-event-at-T property cannot be converted into a claim of no events at any real time. Countable intersections can combine fixed-horizon assertions, but such an argument is not written as a separate conclusion here. The equality in (8), unlike separate coordinate-wise AE equalities, is a raw equality of whole functions for each w in C. The path sigma-algebra is the product generated by evaluations, with no supplied topology, regularity subspace, or measurability of arbitrary uncountable-time events. No general diagonal-evaluation measurability follows just from that product structure; joint measurability of this specific Z is separately stated.
Finite waits are elapsed waiting times; t_j are accumulated times. An infinite wait produces a dead subsequent record, remains dead at all subsequent indices, and does not mean the physical path stops: at any finite time bracketed by the preceding finite event and infinity, (2) continues the deterministic flow from that last live state. No raw non-explosion, strict raw event-time increase, or raw count interpretation is asserted. Zero or negative raw exponential coordinates are clipped to zero and can cause immediate, coincident events. Missing brackets invoke the explicit Z=z0 and N=0 defaults. The density identity includes final survival up to T and is not a normalized conditional distribution given N_T=n.

Degenerate cases.
Dimension zero is allowed: E and E×E each have a unique point. Gradients, displacements, rates and cumulative hazards vanish, reflection and flow preserve the unique state, and the Hessian inequalities are vacuous in v. The scalar assumptions α>0, α≤β and 0<η with βη≤1 still remain. For any state with identically zero future hazard, τ(e)=infinity for e>0 and τ(0)=0. In dimension zero, under P the thresholds are positive simultaneously at all countably many indices almost surely, so there are no positive-index finite events and N_T=0 almost surely for every finite T; raw zero thresholds may instead create zero-time records.
At n=0, Prefix_(0,T) is a singleton for every T, including T=0; strict order preservation on the one-point (0) tuple and all constraints on Fin 0 are vacuous. Its zero-dimensional product-volume mass is one. K_0 is the sole initial record, qReal_0=q_0=1, Γ_0(s)=Φ(y,r,s,z0), and ρ_0=exp(−Λ(y,r,z0,T)) embedded in ENNReal. Thus the n=0 component is the deterministic no-event path weighted by its survival probability. C_(0,T) means the first wait is strictly greater than T.
At T=0 and n>0 the prefix subtype is empty, since its first event time would have to be both positive and below zero. Its density integral is zero; the mass identity therefore gives P{N_0=n}=0 for n>0. At n=0,T=0 the density is one because Λ(0)=0, hence P{N_0=0}=1. The AE endpoint clause excludes all positive-index event times equal to zero. Raw inputs with zero thresholds are still possible and are not covered by that exclusion.
If an event-count component has probability zero, (12)–(14) still give zero finite measures and zero integrals; no division by that probability occurs and no conditional probability is defined.
The theorem contains no stationarity/invariance assertion, mixing or accuracy bound, moment or expected-event-count bound, random initial law, acceptance/rejection algorithm, or source-provenance claim.
