    A self outer product has operator norm equal to the squared vector norm

    AutoSamplingTheory.TechnicalLemmas.Probability.EmpiricalCovariance.norm_rankOne_self_eq_sq · theorem · Teaching coverage

    Read this first

    Mathematical statement

    For every vector x in a real inner-product space, the continuous rank-one operator y ↦ ⟪x,y⟫x has operator norm exactly ‖x‖².

    \[\|x\otimes x\|_{\mathrm{op}}=\|x\|^2\]

      Mathlib computes the norm of u⊗v as ‖u‖‖v‖.

    All objects and hypotheses

    • E is a real inner-product space.
    • x is arbitrary; finite dimensionality and completeness are not assumed.

    Mathematical proof

    1. Use the rank-one norm identity

    Mathlib computes the norm of u⊗v as ‖u‖‖v‖.

    \[\|x\otimes x\|=\|x\|\,\|x\|\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    Simplify InnerProductSpace.rankOne with its norm theorem.

    2. Rewrite the product as a square

    The identical norm factors are exactly ‖x‖².

    \[\|x\|\,\|x\|=\|x\|^2\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    simp [pow_two] closes the equality.
