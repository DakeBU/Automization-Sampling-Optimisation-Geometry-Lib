    The actual SPHMC product reference has controlled phase energy

    Mathematical statement

    The product of the smoothed position law and independent standard-Gaussian momentum is a probability law with exact coordinate moments and an explicit combined phase-energy bound.

    \[\mathbb E_{\Pi_\sigma}[\|X-x_\star\|^2+\|P\|^2]\le M+(\sigma^2+1)d.\]

    Proof roadmap

    1. 1Construct the phase law

      Use the actual product measure, not two unrelated marginal assumptions.

    2. 2Integrate the position coordinate

      The second coordinate has total mass one, so the product integral is the smoothing identity.

    3. 3Integrate the momentum coordinate

      The first marginal has total mass one and the momentum is standard Gaussian.

    4. 4Add the two energies

      Product integrability legitimizes splitting the integral before applying the supplied base bound.

    Mathematical proof

    1. Construct the phase law

    Use the actual product measure, not two unrelated marginal assumptions.

    \[\Pi_\sigma=(\mu*\mathsf N(0,\sigma^2I))\otimes\mathsf N(0,I).\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    piSigma.prod (stdGaussian E), with an explicit probability instance for the smoothing pushforward.

    2. Integrate the position coordinate

    The second coordinate has total mass one, so the product integral is the smoothing identity.

    \[\mathbb E_{\Pi_\sigma}\|X-x_\star\|^2=\mathbb E_\mu\|X-x_\star\|^2+\sigma^2d.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    Integrable.comp_fst, integral_prod and probReal_univ.

    3. Integrate the momentum coordinate

    The first marginal has total mass one and the momentum is standard Gaussian.

    \[\mathbb E_{\Pi_\sigma}\|P\|^2=d.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    Integrable.comp_snd and the standard-Gaussian moment theorem.

    4. Add the two energies

    Product integrability legitimizes splitting the integral before applying the supplied base bound.

    \[(M+\sigma^2d)+d=M+(\sigma^2+1)d.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    integral_add followed by linarith.

    theorem phaseReference_second_moment
    (mu : Measure E) [IsProbabilityMeasure mu] (xstar : E) (sigma M : ℝ)
    (hpos : Integrable (fun x : E => ‖x - xstar‖ ^ 2) mu)
    (hposBound : (∫ x : E, ‖x - xstar‖ ^ 2 ∂mu) ≤ M) :
    let piSigma := gaussianSmoothing mu sigma
    let PiSigma := piSigma.prod (stdGaussian E)
    IsProbabilityMeasure PiSigma ∧
      Integrable (fun z : E × E => ‖z.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2) PiSigma ∧
      Integrable (fun z : E × E => ‖z.2‖ ^ 2) PiSigma ∧
      (∫ z : E × E, ‖z.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 ∂PiSigma) =
        (∫ x : E, ‖x - xstar‖ ^ 2 ∂mu) +
          sigma ^ 2 * (Module.finrank ℝ E : ℝ) ∧
      (∫ z : E × E, ‖z.2‖ ^ 2 ∂PiSigma) = Module.finrank ℝ E ∧
      Integrable
        (fun z : E × E => ‖z.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 + ‖z.2‖ ^ 2) PiSigma ∧
      (∫ z : E × E, ‖z.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 + ‖z.2‖ ^ 2 ∂PiSigma) ≤
        M + (sigma ^ 2 + 1) * (Module.finrank ℝ E : ℝ)

    theorem phaseReference_second_moment
    (mu : Measure E) [IsProbabilityMeasure mu] (xstar : E) (sigma M : ℝ)
    (hpos : Integrable (fun x : E => ‖x - xstar‖ ^ 2) mu)
    (hposBound : (∫ x : E, ‖x - xstar‖ ^ 2 ∂mu) ≤ M) :
    let piSigma := gaussianSmoothing mu sigma
    let PiSigma := piSigma.prod (stdGaussian E)
    IsProbabilityMeasure PiSigma ∧
      Integrable (fun z : E × E => ‖z.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2) PiSigma ∧
      Integrable (fun z : E × E => ‖z.2‖ ^ 2) PiSigma ∧
      (∫ z : E × E, ‖z.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 ∂PiSigma) =
        (∫ x : E, ‖x - xstar‖ ^ 2 ∂mu) +
          sigma ^ 2 * (Module.finrank ℝ E : ℝ) ∧
      (∫ z : E × E, ‖z.2‖ ^ 2 ∂PiSigma) = Module.finrank ℝ E ∧
      Integrable
        (fun z : E × E => ‖z.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 + ‖z.2‖ ^ 2) PiSigma ∧
      (∫ z : E × E, ‖z.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 + ‖z.2‖ ^ 2 ∂PiSigma) ≤
        M + (sigma ^ 2 + 1) * (Module.finrank ℝ E : ℝ) := by
  classical
  dsimp only
  let piSigma := gaussianSmoothing mu sigma
  have hposition :
      Integrable (fun y : E => ‖y - xstar‖ ^ 2) piSigma ∧
        (∫ y : E, ‖y - xstar‖ ^ 2 ∂piSigma) =
          (∫ x : E, ‖x - xstar‖ ^ 2 ∂mu) +
            sigma ^ 2 * (Module.finrank ℝ E : ℝ) := by
    simpa only [piSigma] using
      gaussianSmoothing_position_second_moment mu xstar sigma hpos
  obtain ⟨hgauss, hgaussIntegral⟩ :=
    integrable_norm_sq_and_integral_stdGaussian (E := E)
  letI : IsProbabilityMeasure piSigma := by
    dsimp [piSigma, gaussianSmoothing,
      AutoSamplingTheory.TechnicalLemmas.Measure.CommonNoiseContraction.addNoise]
    exact Measure.isProbabilityMeasure_map
      (by fun_prop : AEMeasurable (fun p : E × E => p.1 + p.2)
        (mu.prod (scaledStdGaussian (E := E) sigma)))
  have hX := hposition.1.comp_fst (stdGaussian E)
  have hP := hgauss.comp_snd piSigma
  have hXI :
      (∫ z : E × E, ‖z.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 ∂piSigma.prod (stdGaussian E)) =
        (∫ x : E, ‖x - xstar‖ ^ 2 ∂mu) +
          sigma ^ 2 * (Module.finrank ℝ E : ℝ) := by
    rw [integral_prod _ hX]
    simp only [integral_const, probReal_univ, one_smul, hposition.2]
  have hPI :
      (∫ z : E × E, ‖z.2‖ ^ 2 ∂piSigma.prod (stdGaussian E)) =
        Module.finrank ℝ E := by
    rw [integral_prod _ hP]
    simp only [hgaussIntegral, integral_const, probReal_univ, one_smul]
  refine ⟨inferInstance, hX, hP, hXI, hPI, hX.add hP, ?_⟩
  rw [integral_add hX hP, hXI, hPI]
  linarith
