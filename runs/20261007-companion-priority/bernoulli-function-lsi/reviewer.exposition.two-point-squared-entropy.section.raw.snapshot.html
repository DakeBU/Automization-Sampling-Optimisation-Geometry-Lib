Mathematical statement

For every signed real a,b, including zero, the homogeneous two-point entropy of their squares is at most half their squared difference.

\[\frac{a^2\log a^2+b^2\log b^2}{2}-\frac{a^2+b^2}{2}\log\frac{a^2+b^2}{2}\le\frac{(a-b)^2}{2}.\]

Proof roadmap

  1. 1Positive squares: the upper Rothaus direction

    For positive A,B, the actual scalar calculus proves the positive defect 2-2sqrt(1-t²)-phi(t) by two mean-value arguments, then rescales m=(A+B)/2, t=(A-B)/(A+B). All logarithm/sqrt derivative domains are proved inside the helper closure.

  2. 2Pass to signed inputs

    Apply the positive inequality to A=a²,B=b², then use sqrt(a²)=|a| and the reverse absolute-value triangle inequality. This is the upper entropy bound, not the unrelated lower-bound theorem.

  3. 3Include zero values

    If one square vanishes, expand log(A/2)=log A-log2. The remaining entropy is A log2/2≤A/2 because log2≤1; if both vanish, both sides are zero.

Mathematical proof

1. Positive squares: the upper Rothaus direction

For positive A,B, the actual scalar calculus proves the positive defect 2-2sqrt(1-t²)-phi(t) by two mean-value arguments, then rescales m=(A+B)/2, t=(A-B)/(A+B). All logarithm/sqrt derivative domains are proved inside the helper closure.

\[\frac{A\log A+B\log B}{2}-m\log m\le\frac{(\sqrt A-\sqrt B)^2}{2},\quad A,B>0.\]

2. Pass to signed inputs

Apply the positive inequality to A=a²,B=b², then use sqrt(a²)=|a| and the reverse absolute-value triangle inequality. This is the upper entropy bound, not the unrelated lower-bound theorem.

\[(|a|-|b|)^2\le(a-b)^2.\]

3. Include zero values

If one square vanishes, expand log(A/2)=log A-log2. The remaining entropy is A log2/2≤A/2 because log2≤1; if both vanish, both sides are zero.

\[\operatorname{Ent}_2(A,0)=\tfrac A2\log2\le\tfrac A2.\]

theorem two_point_squared_entropy_le_half_sq_sub (a b : ℝ) :
    (a ^ 2 * Real.log (a ^ 2) + b ^ 2 * Real.log (b ^ 2)) / 2 -
      ((a ^ 2 + b ^ 2) / 2) * Real.log ((a ^ 2 + b ^ 2) / 2) ≤
    (a - b) ^ 2 / 2 := by
  have hlog2 : Real.log 2 ≤ 1 := by
    have h := Real.log_le_sub_one_of_pos (by norm_num : (0 : ℝ) < 2)
    norm_num at h
    exact h
  by_cases ha : a = 0
  · subst a
    by_cases hb : b = 0
    · simp [hb]
    · have hb2 : b ^ 2 ≠ 0 := pow_ne_zero 2 hb
      have hlog := Real.log_div hb2 (by norm_num : (2 : ℝ) ≠ 0)
      simp only [zero_pow (by norm_num : (2 : ℕ) ≠ 0), zero_mul, zero_add,
        zero_sub, neg_sq]
      rw [hlog]
      nlinarith [sq_nonneg b, mul_le_mul_of_nonneg_left hlog2 (sq_nonneg b)]
  · by_cases hb : b = 0
    · subst b
      have ha2 : a ^ 2 ≠ 0 := pow_ne_zero 2 ha
      have hlog := Real.log_div ha2 (by norm_num : (2 : ℝ) ≠ 0)
      simp only [zero_pow (by norm_num : (2 : ℕ) ≠ 0), zero_mul, add_zero, sub_zero]
      rw [hlog]
      nlinarith [sq_nonneg a, mul_le_mul_of_nonneg_left hlog2 (sq_nonneg a)]
    · have h := rothaus_lemma (sq_pos_of_ne_zero ha) (sq_pos_of_ne_zero hb)
      rw [Real.sqrt_sq_eq_abs, Real.sqrt_sq_eq_abs] at h

        rw [sq_le_sq]
        exact abs_abs_sub_abs_le_abs_sub a b
      linarith
