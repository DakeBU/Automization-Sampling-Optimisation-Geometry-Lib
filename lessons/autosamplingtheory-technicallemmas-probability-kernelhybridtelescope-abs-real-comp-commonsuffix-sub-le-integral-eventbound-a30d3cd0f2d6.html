    Samplinglib mathematical lesson

    Later adaptive computation cannot amplify one replaced call

    AutoSamplingTheory.TechnicalLemmas.Probability.KernelHybridTelescope.abs_real_comp_commonSuffix_sub_le_integral_eventBound · theorem · Teaching coverage

    Read this first

    Mathematical statement

    Under the preceding hypotheses, let S be any Markov kernel from the one-call output space B to a final output space C. Applying the same downstream kernel S after K and after L preserves the mean error bound: every measurable final event T differs by at most ∫ε dμ.

    \[\left|\bigl(\mu(KS)\bigr)(T)-\bigl(\mu(LS)\bigr)(T)\right|\le\int_A\varepsilon\,d\mu.\]

    Proof roadmap

    1. 1Control the laws immediately after the replaced slot

      The first theorem gives an eventwise discrepancy bound between μK and μL equal to the ideal-history mean of the local budget.

    2. 2Apply total-variation data processing to the common suffix

      A common Markov kernel cannot increase an eventwise total-variation bound. Treat every later adaptive action, including its final readout, as S. This is the mathematical reason adjacent hybrids only pay for the slot at which they differ.

    Full assumptions and notation

    All objects and hypotheses

    • The input law, two replacement kernels and conditional error budget satisfy the first theorem.
    • S is the same Markov kernel in both hybrids. In an adaptive proof, the history state must contain enough information that all later calls really are represented by this common suffix.
    • Kernel associativity is used as equality of composed measures; no informal reassociation of conditional distributions is assumed.

    Mathematical proof

    1. Control the laws immediately after the replaced slot

    The first theorem gives an eventwise discrepancy bound between μK and μL equal to the ideal-history mean of the local budget.

    \[\forall U,\quad |(\mu K)(U)-(\mu L)(U)|\le\int_A\varepsilon\,d\mu.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    abs_real_comp_sub_le_integral_eventBound is called with exactly the same μ,K,L,ε and almost-everywhere conditional estimate.

    2. Apply total-variation data processing to the common suffix

    A common Markov kernel cannot increase an eventwise total-variation bound. Treat every later adaptive action, including its final readout, as S. This is the mathematical reason adjacent hybrids only pay for the slot at which they differ.

    \[\sup_T|((\mu K)S)(T)-((\mu L)S)(T)|\le\sup_U|(\mu K)(U)-(\mu L)(U)|.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    KernelTotalVariation.abs_real_comp_sub_le supplies factor-one contraction. Measure.comp_assoc rewrites the result into the source-facing composition order.
