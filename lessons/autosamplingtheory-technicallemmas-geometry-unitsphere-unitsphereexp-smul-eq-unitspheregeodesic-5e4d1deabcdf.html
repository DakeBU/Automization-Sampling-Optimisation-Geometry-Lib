    Forward exponential steps equal the signed geodesic

    AutoSamplingTheory.TechnicalLemmas.Geometry.UnitSphere.unitSphereExp_smul_eq_unitSphereGeodesic · theorem · Teaching coverage

    Read this first

    Mathematical statement

    For nonnegative time, exponentiating the scaled increment tz equals the signed geodesic at t.

    \[t\ge0\Longrightarrow\operatorname{Exp}_w(tz)=\gamma_{w,z}(t)\]

    Proof roadmap

    1. 1Handle degenerate cases

      When t=0 or z=0, both sides reduce to w.

    2. 2Rewrite the scaled norm

      Nonnegative t gives ‖tz‖=t‖z‖.

    3. 3Expand sinc and cancel t

      For nonzero t‖z‖, sinc becomes sine divided by its argument.

    Full assumptions and notation

    All objects and hypotheses

    • t is nonnegative.
    • Zero time and zero velocity are allowed.
    • w and z are arbitrary ambient vectors.

    Mathematical proof

    1. Handle degenerate cases

    When t=0 or z=0, both sides reduce to w.

    \[t=0\text{ or }z=0\Rightarrow\operatorname{Exp}_w(tz)=w\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    Lean discharges both branches by simplification.

    2. Rewrite the scaled norm

    Nonnegative t gives ‖tz‖=t‖z‖.

    \[\|tz\|=t\|z\|\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    norm_smul and abs_of_nonneg prove the equality.

    3. Expand sinc and cancel t

    For nonzero t‖z‖, sinc becomes sine divided by its argument.

    \[\operatorname{sinc}(t\|z\|)(tz)=\frac{\sin(t\|z\|)}{\|z\|}z\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    Real.sinc_of_ne_zero and field simplification finish.

    theorem unitSphereExp_smul_eq_unitSphereGeodesic
    (w z : E) {t : ℝ} (ht : 0 ≤ t) :
    unitSphereExp w (t • z) = unitSphereGeodesic w z t := by
  by_cases hz : z = 0
  · subst z
    simp [unitSphereExp, unitSphereGeodesic]
  by_cases ht0 : t = 0
  · subst t
    simp [unitSphereExp, unitSphereGeodesic]
  have hr : ‖z‖ ≠ 0 := norm_ne_zero_iff.mpr hz
  have htne : t ≠ 0 := ht0
  have hnorm : ‖t • z‖ = t * ‖z‖ := by
    rw [norm_smul, Real.norm_eq_abs, abs_of_nonneg ht]
  rw [unitSphereExp, unitSphereGeodesic, if_neg hr, hnorm,
    Real.sinc_of_ne_zero (mul_ne_zero htne hr)]
  congr 1
  rw [smul_smul]
  congr 1
  field_simp [htne, hr]
