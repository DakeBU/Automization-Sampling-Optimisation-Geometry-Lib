    Backward interpolation turns growth into contraction

    AutoSamplingTheory.TechnicalLemmas.FunctionalInequalities.SemigroupDecay.backward_interpolation_contraction_of_growth · theorem · Teaching coverage

    Read this first

    Mathematical statement

    Let Φ be a continuous scalar interpolation with an exact right derivative. If Φ'(u)≥rΦ(u) at every time, then reading the interpolation from s to t gives Φ(s)≤Φ(t)e^{-r(t-s)} for every s≤t.

    \[\Phi'(u)\ge r\Phi(u)\Longrightarrow \Phi(s)\le \Phi(t)e^{-r(t-s)}\]

    Proof roadmap

    1. 1Reverse the inequality by negating the curve

      For g=-Φ, the lower growth bound becomes the upper differential inequality g'≤rg required by one-sided Grönwall.

    2. 2Apply one-sided Grönwall between the two endpoints

      The comparison theorem gives -Φ(t)≤-Φ(s)e^{r(t-s)}, equivalently Φ(s)e^{r(t-s)}≤Φ(t).

    3. 3Move the exponential to the terminal side

      Multiply by the positive inverse exponential and use e^{rΔ}e^{-rΔ}=1.

    Full assumptions and notation

    All objects and hypotheses

    • Φ and its supplied derivative are real-valued on the whole real line.
    • Φ is continuous.
    • At every u, the right derivative within [u,∞) is exactly Φ'(u).
    • The lower differential inequality rΦ(u)≤Φ'(u) holds at every real u.
    • The endpoints satisfy s≤t.
    • No sign condition on Φ or r is required.

    Mathematical proof

    1. Reverse the inequality by negating the curve

    For g=-Φ, the lower growth bound becomes the upper differential inequality g'≤rg required by one-sided Grönwall.

    \[g=-\Phi,\qquad g'=-\Phi'\le-r\Phi=rg\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    Negate hgrowth; HasDerivWithinAt.neg supplies the exact right derivative of g.

    2. Apply one-sided Grönwall between the two endpoints

    The comparison theorem gives -Φ(t)≤-Φ(s)e^{r(t-s)}, equivalently Φ(s)e^{r(t-s)}≤Φ(t).

    \[\Phi(s)e^{r(t-s)}\le\Phi(t)\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    Use le_gronwallBound_of_liminf_deriv_right_le, then gronwallBound_ε0.

    3. Move the exponential to the terminal side

    Multiply by the positive inverse exponential and use e^{rΔ}e^{-rΔ}=1.

    \[\Phi(s)\le\Phi(t)e^{-r(t-s)}\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    Use Real.exp_nonneg, Real.exp_add, and exact ring normalization of the exponents.

    Lean proof · backward_interpolation_contraction_of_growth

    The proof applies Mathlib's one-sided Grönwall theorem to the negated interpolation and then cancels the positive exponential exactly.

    Braces mark parameters Lean can infer; square brackets request structures such as a measurable space or probability measure. Named hypotheses are mathematical premises, not facts established by this declaration. Section parameters are described in the mathematical hypotheses above; the module link retains their exact source context.

    theorem backward_interpolation_contraction_of_growth
    {interpolation interpolation' : ℝ → ℝ} {rate s t : ℝ}
    (hcontinuous : Continuous interpolation)
    (hderiv : ∀ u : ℝ,
      HasDerivWithinAt interpolation (interpolation' u) (Ici u) u)
    (hgrowth : ∀ u : ℝ,
      rate * interpolation u ≤ interpolation' u)
    (hst : s ≤ t) :
    interpolation s ≤
      interpolation t * Real.exp (-rate * (t - s)) := by
  have hbound : ∀ x ∈ Ico s t,
      -interpolation' x ≤ rate * (-interpolation x) + 0 := by
    intro x _hx
    have hneg := neg_le_neg (hgrowth x)
    simpa [neg_mul] using hneg
  have hgronwall :=
    le_gronwallBound_of_liminf_deriv_right_le
      (f := fun x => -interpolation x)
      (f' := fun x => -interpolation' x)
      (δ := -interpolation s)
      (K := rate)
      (ε := 0)
      (a := s)
      (b := t)
      hcontinuous.neg.continuousOn
      (fun x _hx r hr => by
        simpa [slope] using ((hderiv x).neg.liminf_right_slope_le hr))
      le_rfl
      hbound
      t
      ⟨hst, le_rfl⟩
  rw [gronwallBound_ε0] at hgronwall
  have hexponential_growth :
      interpolation s * Real.exp (rate * (t - s)) ≤ interpolation t := by
    linarith
  have hscaled := mul_le_mul_of_nonneg_right hexponential_growth
    (Real.exp_nonneg (-rate * (t - s)))
  have hinverse :
      Real.exp (rate * (t - s)) * Real.exp (-rate * (t - s)) = 1 := by
    rw [← Real.exp_add]
    have hzero : rate * (t - s) + -rate * (t - s) = 0 := by ring
    rw [hzero, Real.exp_zero]
  calc
    interpolation s =
        (interpolation s * Real.exp (rate * (t - s))) *
          Real.exp (-rate * (t - s)) := by
      rw [mul_assoc, hinverse, mul_one]
    _ ≤ interpolation t * Real.exp (-rate * (t - s)) := hscaled

end
