Mathematical derivation without extra hypotheses
Work in the real Hilbert space HP0, which need not be finite-dimensional. Abbreviate A=A0, G=GammaP0, Iinv=Inv, Q=A Iinv. The old clauses give selfadjoint A and Iinv, positive hence selfadjoint G, commuting A with G and Iinv, A^2+G^2=identity, and the SAME two-sided inverse Iinv G=G Iinv=identity. Thus Q is selfadjoint, commutes with A and G, and QG=GQ=A. These properties are consequences of retained old clauses, not extra caller hypotheses.

For arbitrary u,v in HP0 put u'=Au-Gv and v'=Gu+Av. Define k=<Au,Gv>. Selfadjointness, commutation and real-inner-product symmetry give
  k=<Gu,Av>=<Q G^2 u,v>.
The square identity gives ||Aw||^2+||Gw||^2=||w||^2 for every w. Expanding real Hilbert norms yields
  (||u'||^2-||v'||^2)/2
    = (||Au||^2-||Gu||^2+||Gv||^2-||Av||^2)/2 - 2k.
Expanding the cross term, using QG=A and moving the selfadjoint factors through the inner product, gives
  <Q u',v'>
    = ||Au||^2-||Av||^2 + <Q A^2 u,v> - k.
Subtract the second equality from the first, use k=<Q G^2 u,v> and A^2+G^2=identity, and obtain
  C(u',v') = -||u||^2/2 + ||v||^2/2 - <Q u,v>.
Since C(u,v)=||u||^2/2-||v||^2/2-<Q u,v>, subtraction gives exactly
  C(u',v')-C(u,v) = -||u||^2+||v||^2.
Substitute the retained actual rotation identities

  gV=GammaP0 fP+A0 fV,
to obtain the proposed formula for C(gP,gV)-C(fP,fV), with precisely its displayed sign and coefficient. No diagonalization, eigenbasis, finite-rank premise, new inverse, new spectral gap, additional source regularity or onto property of V0 is used.
