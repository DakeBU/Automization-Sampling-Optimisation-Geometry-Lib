Anonymous theorem reconstruction

Ambient data and hypotheses.
Let E be any finite-dimensional real inner-product space with its normed additive group structure and Borel measurable structure. Dimension zero is allowed. Let V:E -> R be twice continuously Frechet differentiable. Let alpha,beta be nonnegative real numbers, regarded also as ordinary real scalars, with alpha>0 and alpha<=beta. For every x,v in E, assume
  alpha ||v||^2 <= D(DV)(x)[v][v] <= beta ||v||^2.
Let eta be a real number with eta>0 and beta eta<=1. The gradient below is the real Hilbert-space gradient of V. All following displayed constructions are definitions from these data, not additional assumptions or freely supplied interfaces.

Deterministic phase-space constructions.
For y,r in E, put c=y-eta grad V(r). For z=(x,v) in E x E and a real time u define
  Phi(y,r,u,(x,v)) = (c+cos(u)(x-c)+sqrt(eta)sin(u)v,
                      -sin(u)/sqrt(eta)(x-c)+cos(u)v).
Set g_r(x)=grad V(x)-grad V(r) and define
  S_r(x,v) = (x, v-[2 <v,g_r(x)> / ||g_r(x)||^2] g_r(x)),
  rate_r(x,v) = sqrt(eta) max(0,<v,g_r(x)>).
Real division by zero is zero. Thus S_r(x,v)=(x,v) when g_r(x)=0; there is no requirement that this gradient difference be nonzero. For h>=0 define the real cumulative rate
  Lambda(y,r,z,h) = integral from 0 to h of rate_r(Phi(y,r,u,z)) du,
where the integral is the real interval integral with respect to real Lebesgue measure.

Finite ordered times and event states.
For an integer n>=0 and a finite nonnegative real horizon T, let D(n,T) be the subset of R^n consisting of t=(t_1,...,t_n) with
  0<t_1<...<t_n<T.
The Lean definition expresses this by strict monotonicity after prepending zero and by the upper bound for every coordinate. For n=0 all conditions are vacuous, so D(0,T) is a singleton, including T=0. Prefix(n,T) is the subtype of R^n specified by D(n,T). Set t_0=0. Given an initial phase state z_0 and t in Prefix(n,T), recursively define, for j=1,...,n,
  delta_j=t_j-t_(j-1),
  b_j=Phi(y,r,delta_j,z_(j-1)),
  z_j=S_r(b_j).
Here b_j is the state immediately before the prescribed event and z_j is the state after its reflection. The delta_j are strictly positive. zAt is the sequence z_0,...,z_n. K is the history with n+1 live records (t_j,z_j), j=0,...,n. In the literal definition times are converted by Real.toNNReal, which takes positive part and hence leaves these valid nonnegative times unchanged.

Histories, path evaluation, and terminal survival.
The full record type Rec is the disjoint union of ([0,infinity) x (E x E)) and a one-point cemetery. Hist(n) is the set of arrays of n+1 such records. liveTime is the stored nonnegative time on a live record and zero on the cemetery. d(n,T,h) is the truncated nonnegative difference T-liveTime(h_n). For a duration u>=0, weight(y,r,record,u) is exp(-Lambda(y,r,z,u)), embedded into the extended nonnegative reals, for a live record whose stored phase state is z; for the cemetery it is zero. Its stored time does not otherwise enter weight.

Path(T) is the space of all functions from the closed nonnegative interval [0,T] into E x E. For a general history h, Psi(y,r,z_0,n,T,h)(s) is computed by starting from z_0 and visiting records in ascending index order j=0,...,n. A live record (a,z) with a<=s replaces the current value by Phi(y,r,s-a,z); a live record with a>s or a cemetery record leaves it unchanged. The subtraction used here is nonnegative truncated subtraction, equal to ordinary subtraction when a<=s. Consequently it is the last eligible record by index, not necessarily the largest record time for an arbitrary unsorted history, that determines the final value. Gamma(y,r,z_0,n,T,t) is Psi evaluated on the particular ordered, fully live history K. Equivalently, on this valid history Gamma(s)=Phi(y,r,s-t_j,z_j), where j is the largest index with t_j<=s. This includes the after-reflection state at an event time, and includes both endpoints 0 and T. In particular its terminal value is Phi(y,r,T-t_n,z_n), with t_n=0 and z_n=z_0 when n=0. The actual theorem only evaluates this path at T; it does not quantify over path-space tests or assert a path-space identity. The packet's contextual convention about a clamped real-time extension adds no such extension or claim to this displayed statement.

Prefix and complete finite-history densities.
For each valid prefix define the nonnegative real number
  qReal(y,r,z_0,n,T,t)
    = product over j=1,...,n of
        [rate_r(b_j) exp(-Lambda(y,r,z_(j-1),delta_j))].
q is qReal embedded in the extended nonnegative reals by ENNReal.ofReal. Empty products equal one. Define
  density(y,r,z_0,n,T,t)
    = q(y,r,z_0,n,T,t)
      * exp(-Lambda(y,r,z_n,T-t_n)),
with the exponential embedded in the extended nonnegative reals. This is exactly the literal rho/density definition using K, d and weight; the live ordered histories make its final duration T-t_n. The terminal survival factor remains present for n=0. Every potential event factor uses the rate at the state b_j before reflection, not at z_j. These definitions use real exponentials and extended-nonnegative multiplication; they contain no normalization constant.

Energy and integration measures.
Define R(x,v)=(x,-v),
  U_y(x,v)=V(x)+||x-y||^2/(2 eta)+||v||^2/2,
  w_y(x,v)=exp(-U_y(x,v)).
Let ell be the product of Lebesgue volume on the position E and Lebesgue volume on the velocity E, in that order. Let m(n,T) be Measure.comap of Lebesgue volume on Fin(n)->R along the inclusion of Prefix(n,T) into that finite-coordinate real vector space. In ordinary terms this is the induced Lebesgue measure on the ordered open simplex of event times, with no n! factor or probability normalization. For n=0 it is the mass-one measure on the sole empty time vector. The final integration measure is ell.prod(m(n,T)): the phase-state variable (position,velocity) is the first product component, and the ordered time vector is the second. If written as iterated nonnegative integrals, this has outer phase-state integration and inner prefix-time integration.

Exact quantified assertion.
For every integer n>=0 and every finite T>=0, there exists a map revTimes:Prefix(n,T)->Prefix(n,T) such that, in one-based coordinates,
  revTimes(t)_j = T-t_(n+1-j),  j=1,...,n.
Equivalently the original zero-based coordinate i is sent to T-t_(n-1-i). The map is chosen after n,T and before y,r, so the same map works for every y,r in E. Its coordinate formula specifies its value completely.

For every y,r in E, on Q=(E x E) x Prefix(n,T), write a point as p=(z_0,t) and define
  A(p)=Gamma(y,r,z_0,n,T,t)(T),
  Theta(p)=(R(A(p)),revTimes(t)),
  W(p)=ENNReal.ofReal(w_y(z_0)) * density(y,r,z_0,n,T,t).
Then for every measurable F:Q->[0,infinity],
  integral_Q W(p) F(Theta(p)) d(ell.prod(m(n,T)))(p)
    = integral_Q W(p) F(p) d(ell.prod(m(n,T)))(p).
These are nonnegative Lebesgue integrals with values in the extended nonnegative reals, not real integrals. Thus the assertion includes cases where either side is infinite; it asserts equality of the extended values and requires neither integrability nor boundedness nor finiteness of F. Standard extended-nonnegative conventions apply, including 0 times infinity equals 0.

Scope and degenerate cases.
There is no probability normalization, sum over event counts, random event-count variable, stationary-process construction, non-explosion assertion, path-space reversal assertion, or complexity assertion in this theorem. It establishes the displayed weighted integral identity separately for each fixed n and T. It does not separately state measurability, bijectivity, an involution law, or ordinary unweighted measure preservation of Theta; those must not be substituted for its literal conclusion. revTimes has the stated coordinate property, but the conclusion is precisely its existence together with the integral identity.

When n=0, Prefix is a singleton, K contains just (0,z_0), q=1, the density is exp(-Lambda(y,r,z_0,T)), and A=Phi(y,r,T,z_0). When T=0 and n>0, the strict simplex is empty and both integrals are zero; revTimes is the unique empty map. When T=0 and n=0, the sole path value is z_0, the density is one, and Theta flips the initial velocity. Dimension zero is permitted; in that case all rates vanish, all positive-count densities vanish, and no positive-dimensional assumption may be inserted. A zero event rate at any prescribed event likewise makes its full density zero. All horizons, coordinates, energies, and parameters are finite real or nonnegative-real objects; only F, density/weights as typed, and integral values inhabit extended nonnegative reals. The constructed weights are pointwise finite, though this assertion does not supply a finiteness conclusion for all the displayed integrals. All parameters alpha,beta,eta,V,E are shared throughout, and no extra constants or uniform estimates are asserted.
