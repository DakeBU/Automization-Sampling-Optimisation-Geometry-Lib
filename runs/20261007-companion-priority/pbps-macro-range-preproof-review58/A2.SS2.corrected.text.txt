B.2 Estimates for the reflection and half-turn operators We begin with the reflection operator \mathsf{U} . Let f\in\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}} , so that f(x,y)=f(y) . Although f initially depends only on the auxiliary coordinate y , reflection gives (\mathsf{U}f)(x,y)=f(2x-y) , which generally depends on both coordinates. Its macroscopic and microscopic components are \mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}f and \mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}f , respectively. The following probabilistic representation identifies the squared norm of the microscopic component created by reflection with an expected conditional variance, thereby enabling quantitative estimate by the conditional Poincaré estimates used in Appendix C . Concretely, under \pi_{\eta} , write Y_{+}\coloneqq X+\sqrt{\eta}\,Z\,,\hskip 20.00003ptY_{-}\coloneqq X-\sqrt{\eta}\,Z\,,\hskip 20.00003ptZ\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I)\,,\hskip 20.00003ptX\sim\mu\,. (B.8) The pair (Y_{+},Y_{-}) is exchangeable. We henceforth identify \mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}} with L^{2}(\pi_{\eta}^{Y}) and write f(y) for a macroscopic observable. Under this identification, (\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}f)(y)=\mathbb{E}[f(Y_{-})\mid Y_{+}=y]\,,\hskip 20.00003pt\|\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}f\|^{2}=\mathbb{E}\Var(f(Y_{-})\mid Y_{+})\,. (B.9) To quantify the microscopic component \mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}f created when reflection acts on f\in\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}} , define \mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}\coloneqq(I-\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}^{2})^{1/2}\hskip 10.00002pt\text{on }\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}}\,. (B.10) By ( B.5 ), \mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}=I-\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}^{2} , and hence \mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}=\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{2}\,,\hskip 20.00003pt\|\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}f\|=\|\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}f\|\,,\hskip 10.00002ptf\in\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}}\,. (B.11) Thus a lower bound for \mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}} quantifies how much of a macroscopic observable reflection transfers into the microscopic subspace. Lemma B.1 (Uniform macroscopic coercivity) . Assume \beta\eta\leq 1 , and set \rho_{\rm mac}\coloneqq\frac{1-\alpha\eta}{1+\alpha\eta}\,,\hskip 20.00003pt\gamma_{\rm gap}\coloneqq\sqrt{1-\rho_{\rm mac}^{2}}=\frac{2\sqrt{\alpha\eta}}{1+\alpha\eta}\,. (B.12) Note that \gamma_{\rm gap}\geq\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\alpha\eta} . Then the following hold. (1) For every f\in L^{2}(\pi_{\eta}^{Y}) , \mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}f\in H^{1}(\pi_{\eta}^{Y}) and 4\eta\,\|\nabla\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}f\|^{2}\leq\|f\|^{2}-\|\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}f\|^{2}=\|\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}f\|^{2}=\|\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}f\|^{2}\,. (B.13) (2) Consider the mean-zero macroscopic subspace \mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0}\coloneqq\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}}\cap L_{0}^{2}(\pi_{\eta}) . Then, it holds that \|\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\|\leq\rho_{\rm mac}\hskip 10.00002pt\text{and}\hskip 10.00002pt\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\succeq-\frac{1}{2}\,I\hskip 20.00003pt\text{on }\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0}\,. (B.14) Consequently, \mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}=(I-\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}^{2})^{1/2}\succeq\gamma_{\rm gap}I\hskip 20.00003pt\text{on }\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0}\,. (B.15) We prove Lemma B.1 in Appendix C.1 . The identity ( B.9 ) expresses \|\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}f\| in terms of the conditional variance of f(Y_{-}) given Y_{+} . Differentiating the corresponding conditional expectation shows that this variance controls how quickly \mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}f changes with y . The Poincaré inequality for \pi_{\eta}^{Y} then bounds the norm of a centered f in terms of \|\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}f\| , giving the stated estimate. Since \mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}} is boundedly invertible on \mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0} , ( B.11 ) gives the polar decomposition \mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}=\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}\,,\hskip 20.00003pt\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}\coloneqq\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}\,:\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0}\longrightarrow\mathfrak{H}_{\perp}\,,\hskip 20.00003pt\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}=I_{\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0}}\,. (B.16) Here \mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}} measures the size of the microscopic component created by reflection, while the isometry \mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}} records its direction in \mathfrak{H}_{\perp} . The next proposition says that the half-turn acts nearly as -I on the corresponding microscopic directions \mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}f . Proposition B.2 (Global half-turn bound) . There exist universal constants c,C>0 such that, if \beta\eta\leq c , then \bigl\|(I+\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp})\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}v\bigr\|^{2}\leq C\log(1/\gamma_{\rm gap})\,\|\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}v\|^{2}\,,\hskip 20.00003ptv\in\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0}\,. (B.17) We prove Proposition B.2 in Sections C.2 and C.3 . Together, Lemmas B.1 and B.2 provide the estimates used in the modified L^{2} argument below.
