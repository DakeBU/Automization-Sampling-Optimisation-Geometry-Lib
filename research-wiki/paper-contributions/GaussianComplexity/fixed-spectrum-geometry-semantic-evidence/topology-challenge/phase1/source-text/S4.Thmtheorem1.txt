Theorem 4.1 ( Extension to general $\kappa$ ). For every fixed $\delta\in(0,1)$ , there exists $d_{0}(\delta)$ such
that, for every integer $d\geq d_{0}(\delta)$ , $2\leq\kappa\leq d^{1-\delta}$ , and $0<\varepsilon\leq 1/10$ , $Q_{G}(d,\kappa,\varepsilon)\geq\frac{\delta}{256}\sqrt{\kappa}\log d.$
