B.3 Modified L2L^{2} contraction and proof of the main theorem

We now prove Theorem 3.5 using a discrete modified L2L^{2} argument. For f∈L02​(πη)f\in L_{0}^{2}(\pi_{\eta}), write

f𝖯≔𝖯​f,f⟂≔𝖯⟂​f.f_{\mathsf{P}}\coloneqq\mathsf{P}f\,,\hskip 20.00003ptf_{\perp}\coloneqq\mathsf{P}^{\perp}f\,.

Here f𝖯f_{\mathsf{P}} and f⟂f_{\perp} are, respectively, the macroscopic and microscopic parts of ff.

Fix 0<ρ≤1/20<\rho\leq 1/2. The ordinary L2L^{2} energy does not by itself give the required one-step contraction. Indeed, since 𝖴\mathsf{U} is unitary and 𝖧⟂⁣⟂\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp} is a contraction,

‖𝖪​f‖2−‖f‖2\displaystyle\|\mathsf{K}f\|^{2}-\|f\|^{2} =(1−ρ)2​‖𝖧⟂⁣⟂​f⟂‖2−‖f⟂‖2\displaystyle=(1-\rho)^{2}\,\|\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}f_{\perp}\|^{2}-\|f_{\perp}\|^{2}
≤−ρ⁡(2−ρ)​‖f⟂‖2≤−ρ​‖f⟂‖2.\displaystyle\leq-\rho\,(2-\rho)\,\|f_{\perp}\|^{2}\leq-\rho\,\|f_{\perp}\|^{2}\,. (B.18)

This estimate controls f⟂f_{\perp} but gives nothing when f=f𝖯f=f_{\mathsf{P}}. Reflection moves part of f𝖯f_{\mathsf{P}} into the microscopic subspace, but this transfer is not captured by the L2L^{2} norm, which is a standard difficulty in hypocoercivity. The modified L2L^{2} method addresses this difficulty by adding a corrector to the Lyapunov function. The corrector records the transfer from f𝖯f_{\mathsf{P}} to f⟂f_{\perp}, allowing the contraction in (B.18) to also control f𝖯f_{\mathsf{P}}. This idea goes back to Dolbeault, Mouhot, and Schmeiser [14]; here we follow the refinement of Fan, Li, and Lu [16].

To construct the corrector, we first isolate the part of f⟂f_{\perp} that couples to f𝖯f_{\mathsf{P}} under reflection. Define

f𝖵≔𝖵⟂𝖯∗​f⟂.f_{\mathsf{V}}\coloneqq\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}f_{\perp}\,.

By (B.16), 𝖴⟂𝖯∗​f⟂=Γ𝖯​f𝖵\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}f_{\perp}=\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{V}}, while the component of f⟂f_{\perp} orthogonal to ran⁡(𝖵⟂𝖯)\operatorname{ran}(\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}) is annihilated by 𝖴⟂𝖯∗\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}. Since 𝖵⟂𝖯\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}} is an isometry, 𝖵⟂𝖯​f𝖵=𝖵⟂𝖯​𝖵⟂𝖯∗​f⟂\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{V}}=\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}f_{\perp} is the orthogonal projection of f⟂f_{\perp} onto ran⁡(𝖵⟂𝖯)\operatorname{ran}(\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}), and hence ‖f𝖵‖≤‖f⟂‖\|f_{\mathsf{V}}\|\leq\|f_{\perp}\|. We work with f𝖵f_{\mathsf{V}}, rather than with this projection itself, because f𝖵f_{\mathsf{V}} and f𝖯f_{\mathsf{P}} both belong to ℌ𝖯,0\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0} and can therefore be paired in the corrector.

With this notation, let ω>0\omega>0 be a weight to be chosen below and define the modified Lyapunov function

ℒω​(f)≔‖f‖2+ω​𝒞​(f𝖯,f𝖵),\mathscr{L}_{\omega}(f)\coloneqq\|f\|^{2}+\omega\mathscr{C}(f_{\mathsf{P}},f_{\mathsf{V}})\,, (B.19)

where the corrector is

𝒞⁡(f𝖯,f𝖵)≔12​(‖f𝖯‖2−‖f𝖵‖2)−⟨𝖴𝖯𝖯​Γ𝖯−1​f𝖯,f𝖵⟩.\mathscr{C}(f_{\mathsf{P}},f_{\mathsf{V}})\coloneqq\frac{1}{2}\,\bigl(\|f_{\mathsf{P}}\|^{2}-\|f_{\mathsf{V}}\|^{2}\bigr)-\bigl\langle\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}f_{\mathsf{P}},f_{\mathsf{V}}\bigr\rangle\,. (B.20)

Here Γ𝖯−1\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1} is applied only on ℌ𝖯,0\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0}, where Γ𝖯⪰γgap​I\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}\succeq\gamma_{\rm gap}I, so the corrector is well-defined.
