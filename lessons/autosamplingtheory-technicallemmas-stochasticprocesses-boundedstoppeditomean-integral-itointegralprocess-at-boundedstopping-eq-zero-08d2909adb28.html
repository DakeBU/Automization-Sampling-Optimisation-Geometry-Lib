    A completed Ito process has mean zero at a bounded stopping time

    AutoSamplingTheory.TechnicalLemmas.StochasticProcesses.BoundedStoppedItoMean.integral_itoIntegralProcess_at_boundedStopping_eq_zero · theorem · Teaching coverage

    Read this first

    Mathematical statement

    Under the same global L2, Brownian, usual-filtration, stopping-time, and pointwise horizon-bound assumptions, the integral of the completed Ito process evaluated at tau is zero.

    \[\mathbb E\left[\int_0^\tau\eta_s\,dB_s\right]=0.\]

    Proof roadmap

    1. 1Use the stopped-integrand martingale

      The completed Ito process of the stopped integrand is a martingale and starts exactly at zero, including on the patched exceptional set.

    2. 2Compare deterministic times zero and T

      The martingale set-integral identity on the whole sample space makes the expectation at T equal the expectation at zero, hence zero.

    3. 3Transfer to the bounded stopping time

      The terminal representative agrees almost everywhere both with J_T and with the original completed process I evaluated at tau. Integral congruence transfers the zero expectation.

    Full assumptions and notation

    All objects and hypotheses

    • eta is a Samplinglib ProgressiveL2Integrand on a positive NNReal horizon T.
    • B is Brownian with respect to the filtration and measure, and the filtration satisfies the usual conditions.
    • tau is an NNReal-valued stopping time with tau(omega)≤T for every omega.
    • The theorem concerns the library's actual completed continuous Ito process, not an arbitrary process supplied together with a martingale premise.

    Mathematical proof

    1. Use the stopped-integrand martingale

    The completed Ito process of the stopped integrand is a martingale and starts exactly at zero, including on the patched exceptional set.

    \[J_t=\int_0^t\eta_s\mathbf 1_{s\le\tau}\,dB_s,\qquad J_0=0.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    Use itoIntegralProcess_martingale and itoIntegralProcess_at_zero.

    2. Compare deterministic times zero and T

    The martingale set-integral identity on the whole sample space makes the expectation at T equal the expectation at zero, hence zero.

    \[\mathbb E[J_T]=\mathbb E[J_0]=0.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    Use Martingale.setIntegral_eq with the universal measurable set.

    3. Transfer to the bounded stopping time

    The terminal representative agrees almost everywhere both with J_T and with the original completed process I evaluated at tau. Integral congruence transfers the zero expectation.

    \[J_T=\int_0^T\eta_s\mathbf 1_{s\le\tau}\,dB_s=I_\tau\quad\text{a.s.}\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    Use terminalRepresentative_ae_eq_actual, itoIntegralTerminal_stop_ae, and integral_congr_ae.

    theorem integral_itoIntegralProcess_at_boundedStopping_eq_zero
    [IsFiniteMeasure mu]
    (eta : ProgressiveL2Integrand filtration mu T) (hT : 0 < T)
    (tau : Omega → ℝ≥0)
    (htau : IsChewiStoppingTime filtration
      (fun omega => (tau omega : WithTop ℝ≥0)))
    (htauT : ∀ omega, tau omega ≤ T)
    (hB : IsBrownianMotionWithFiltration B filtration mu)
    (hUsual : SatisfiesUsualConditions filtration mu) :
    ∫ omega, itoIntegralProcess eta hT hB hUsual (tau omega) omega ∂mu = 0 := by
  let _ : IsProbabilityMeasure mu := hB.isProbabilityMeasure
  let stoppedEta := stop eta (fun omega => (tau omega : WithTop ℝ≥0)) htau
  have hmartingale :
      Martingale (itoIntegralProcess stoppedEta hT hB hUsual) filtration mu :=
    itoIntegralProcess_martingale stoppedEta hT hB hUsual
  have hterminalMean :
      ∫ omega, terminalRepresentative stoppedEta hT hB omega ∂mu = 0 := by
    have hmean := hmartingale.setIntegral_eq (show (0 : ℝ≥0) ≤ T from bot_le)
      (MeasurableSet.univ : MeasurableSet[filtration 0] Set.univ)
    have hprocessMean :
        ∫ omega, itoIntegralProcess stoppedEta hT hB hUsual T omega ∂mu = 0 := by
      simpa only [Measure.restrict_univ, itoIntegralProcess_at_zero,
        Pi.zero_apply, integral_zero] using hmean.symm
    exact integral_congr_ae
      (terminalRepresentative_ae_eq_actual stoppedEta hT hB hUsual) |>.trans hprocessMean
  calc
    ∫ omega, itoIntegralProcess eta hT hB hUsual (tau omega) omega ∂mu =
        ∫ omega, terminalRepresentative stoppedEta hT hB omega ∂mu := by
          apply integral_congr_ae
          exact (itoIntegralTerminal_stop_ae eta hT tau htau htauT hB hUsual).symm
    _ = 0 := hterminalMean

end BoundedStoppedItoMean
