For a finite coordinate type I, E=EuclideanSpace(R,I), any function f:E->R and any w in E, the total Frechet derivative satisfies D(f composed with negation)(w)[h]=-Df(-w)[h] for every h in E, and the existing extrinsic spherical expression satisfies L_S(f composed with negation)(w)=L_S f(-w). The operator is literally Delta f(w)-D(Df)(w)[w][w]-(card(I)-1)_+ Df(w)[w], using natural-number subtraction before casting to R. No unit or C2 premise is needed for this algebraic total-operator identity. At unit points where f is C2, its usual classical generator interpretation is applicable; this statement does not itself construct or identify an intrinsic diffusion.
