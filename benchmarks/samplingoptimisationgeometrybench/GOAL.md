## Admission order

1. Establish a versioned curriculum, track-specific L1–L5 reasoning levels, explicit gaps, public-development split and graph truth boundaries.
2. Expand small subject-balanced batches from existing original/permission-cleared library results. Natural-language questions need not pretend to have an equivalent Lean task. A support lemma is not an aligned formal view.
3. Independently decode and compare each proposed pair; bind accepted review to its exact content/source/target. Compilation, semantic review, mathematical source fidelity and publication are distinct. Draft questions remain draft until then.
4. Add trustworthy structured graph tasks, then declaration/source-proof/hypergraph tasks only where the evidence actually supports them. Module imports never certify theorem entailment. Export only audited, license-eligible slices, never the full mixed upstream graph.
5. Add auditable response/run records, human or validated semantic grading for prose, and deterministic structural grading where possible. Untrusted Lean needs a separately isolated executor and fixed-target checks before any model evaluation.
6. Calibrate with recorded model/human runs and reserve genuinely new private families before publishing answers. No public item or variant becomes an unseen test merely by changing a split field.
7. Integrate stable batches, CI and the hierarchical reader; pull before shared integration and push at bounded, tested checkpoints. Do not mark the whole Goal complete after one batch.
