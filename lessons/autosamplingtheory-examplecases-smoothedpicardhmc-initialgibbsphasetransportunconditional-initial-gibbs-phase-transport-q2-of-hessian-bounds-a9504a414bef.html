    The SPHMC q=2 initialization bound with no supplied critical point

    AutoSamplingTheory.ExampleCases.SmoothedPicardHMC.InitialGibbsPhaseTransportUnconditional.initial_gibbs_phase_transport_q2_of_hessian_bounds · theorem · Teaching coverage

    Read this first

    Mathematical statement

    For the actual normalized Gibbs law, its Gaussian smoothing and standard-Gaussian momentum, the q=2 squared M-kappa transport initialization cost is at most 5 kappa d, with the minimizer derived rather than assumed.

    \[W_{2,M_\kappa}^{2}(\delta_{x_{\rm ref}}\otimes\gamma,(\pi*\mathsf N(0,\eta I))\otimes\gamma)\le5\kappa d,\qquad\kappa=1/\alpha.\]

    Proof roadmap

    1. 1Convert Hessian curvature to strong convexity

    2. 2Construct the source minimizer

    3. 3Apply the verified numerical transport chain

    Full assumptions and notation

    All objects and hypotheses

    • E is a finite-dimensional complete real inner-product Borel space.
    • U is C2 with alpha I <= Hessian U <= I and 0 < alpha <= 1.
    • The reference point has squared gradient norm at most alpha times dimension.
    • The smoothing variance eta lies in [0,1].

    Mathematical proof

    1. Convert Hessian curvature to strong convexity

    The genuine C2 Hessian lower bound implies whole-space strong convexity with the same alpha.

    \[D^2U(x)\succeq\alpha I\Longrightarrow U\text{ is }\alpha\text{-strongly convex}.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    HessianStrongConvexity.strongConvexOn_univ_of_fderiv2_lower performs the affine-line argument.

    2. Construct the source minimizer

    Positive strong convexity and differentiability supply a global minimizer p with zero gradient; p is not added as a theorem hypothesis.

    \[\exists p:\ U(p)=\min U,\quad\nabla U(p)=0.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    StrongConvexMinimizer.exists_isMinOn_and_gradient_eq_zero discharges the hidden source step.

    3. Apply the verified numerical transport chain

    The constructed critical point is fed to the already verified Gibbs moment, smoothing and identical-momentum coupling theorem.

    \[\nabla U(p)=0\Longrightarrow W_{2,M_\kappa}^{2}(\mu_0,\Pi_\eta)\le5\kappa d.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    InitialGibbsPhaseTransport.initial_gibbs_phase_transport_q2 supplies the actual normalized measures and constant.
