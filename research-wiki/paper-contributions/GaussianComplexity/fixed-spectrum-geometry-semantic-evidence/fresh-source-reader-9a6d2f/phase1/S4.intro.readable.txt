4 Lower Bound via Hidden Eigenbasis

[[S4.p1]]

[[S4.p1.1]] The preprint [22] proved an $\Omega(\log d)$ query lower bound for centered Gaussian sampling with $I_{d}\preceq A\preceq 2I_{d}$ at total variation accuracy $\varepsilon=\frac{1}{10}$ . We subsequently examine the limitations of this analysis for establishing accuracy-dependent lower bounds in Section 4.4.

[[S4.Thmtheorem1]]

Theorem 4.1 (Extension to general $\kappa$ ).

[[S4.Thmtheorem1.p1]]

[[S4.Thmtheorem1.p1.1]] For every fixed $\delta\in(0,1)$ , there exists $d_{0}(\delta)$ such that, for every integer $d\geq d_{0}(\delta)$ , $2\leq\kappa\leq d^{1-\delta}$ , and $0<\varepsilon\leq 1/10$ ,

[[S4.Ex9]]

$Q_{G}(d,\kappa,\varepsilon)\geq\frac{\delta}{256}\sqrt{\kappa}\log d.$

[[S4.p2]]

[[S4.p2.1]] Compared with Theorem 3.1, this bound also covers $2\leq\kappa<4$ and $\frac{1}{16}<\varepsilon\leq\frac{1}{10}$ , and it holds even when the spectrum of $A$ is known to the algorithm. On the other hand, it does not grow as $\varepsilon\to 0$ and it requires $\kappa\leq d^{1-\delta}$ .
