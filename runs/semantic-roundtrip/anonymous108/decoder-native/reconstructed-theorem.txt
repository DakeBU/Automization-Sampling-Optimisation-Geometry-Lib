ANONYMOUS MATHEMATICAL RECONSTRUCTION

OBJECTS
Write X=E x E, z=(x,v), g=gradient V, and H_V(x)[v,v]=(D(DV)(x)[v])[v]. All the following are literal definitions, not additional hypotheses. Scalar multiplication, norm and inner product are those of E. NN denotes the nonnegative reals, ENN the extended nonnegative reals, NNtop the nonnegative reals with a top element, and r_+=max(r,0).

For parameters y,b in E (b denotes xRef), set c(y,b)=y-eta*g(b). For every real t,
Phi(y,b,t,(x,v))=(c+cos(t)*(x-c)+sqrt(eta)*sin(t)*v, -sin(t)/sqrt(eta)*(x-c)+cos(t)*v).
Define D_b(x)=g(x)-g(b),
S(b,(x,v))=(x,v-[2*<v,D_b(x)>/||D_b(x)||^2]*D_b(x)),
and rate(b,(x,v))=sqrt(eta)*max(0,<v,D_b(x)>).
Division by zero is defined as zero, so the displayed S is also defined when D_b(x)=0 and then leaves the velocity unchanged.
For t in NN, Lambda(y,b,z,t)=integral from 0 to t of rate(b,Phi(y,b,s,z)) ds, using the real Lebesgue interval integral. For e in NN,
tau(y,b,z,e)=inf {t in NN : Lambda(y,b,z,t)-e >= 0}, with the infimum taken in NNtop and inf(empty)=top. This is the hittingAfter construction from time zero for the displayed function, with target [0,infinity). The parameter tuple for that function is ((y,b),z,e).

Let Rec=(NN x X) disjoint-union Unit. Denote a live record by Live(a,z) and the single stopped record by Dead. For e in NN, next(y,b,e,Dead)=Dead. Given Live(a,z), if tau(y,b,z,e)=top then next returns Dead; otherwise, with u=untopD(0,tau(y,b,z,e)), it returns Live(a+u,S(b,Phi(y,b,u,z))). The untopD operation returns the finite value and defaults to zero at top. Define record(y,b,z0,eSequence,0)=Live(0,z0) and record(...,k+1)=next(y,b,eSequence(k),record(...,k)).

The noise space W is the set of sequences w:N->R with its coordinate-product measurable structure. P is the countable product over k in N of the unit-rate exponential probability measure on R. epsilon(w)(k)=(w(k))_+, viewed as NN. Hist(n) is the function space Fin(n+1)->Rec. The random history history(y,b,z0,n,w) has j-th value record(y,b,z0,epsilon(w),j.val). readTime(Live(a,z))=a as a real, and readTime(Dead)=0.

For n in N and T in NN, Delta(n,T) consists of vectors t:Fin(n)->R for which prepending zero gives a strictly increasing sequence and every t(i)<T. Prefix(n,T) is the subtype of such vectors. Its measure m(n,T) is the comap of Lebesgue volume on R^Fin(n) along the subtype inclusion; equivalently, the induced subtype measure under the approved context. Write t_0=0 and t_{i+1}=t(i) for 0<=i<n, so for n>0 this means 0<t_1<...<t_n<T. At n=0 the inequalities are vacuous. Define zAt_0=z0 and zAt_{i+1}=S(b,Phi(y,b,t_{i+1}-t_i,zAt_i)). This specifies zAt(y,b,z0,n,T,t):Fin(n+1)->X by finite induction. K(y,b,z0,n,T,t)(j)=Live((t_j)_+,zAt_j).
qReal(y,b,z0,n,T,t) is the product over 0<=i<n of
rate(b,Phi(y,b,t_{i+1}-t_i,zAt_i))*exp(-Lambda(y,b,zAt_i,(t_{i+1}-t_i)_+)).
The empty product is 1. q is ENNReal.ofReal(qReal), namely the positive part embedded in ENN.
The set A(n,T) of histories consists exactly of h such that every h(j) is a live record, the real sequence j->readTime(h(j)) is strictly increasing, and readTime(h(i+1))<T for every 0<=i<n. This definition alone does not prescribe the initial time or states.

eventTime(y,b,z0,w,j) is the time of record(...,j), embedded in NNtop when live, and top when dead. I(y,b,z0,T,n) is the set of w for which eventTime(...,n)<=T<eventTime(...,n+1). N(y,b,z0,T,w) is the least n satisfying w in I(...,T,n), when such n exists, and zero otherwise. liveTime(Live(a,z))=a in NN and liveTime(Dead)=0. For h in Hist(n), d(n,T,h)=T-liveTime(h(n)), with subtraction truncated at zero.
For r in Rec, u in NN and e in R, survival(y,b,Live(a,z),u,e) means u<tau(y,b,z,e_+) in NNtop; survival(...,Dead,u,e) is False. weight(y,b,Live(a,z),u)=ENNReal.ofReal(exp(-Lambda(y,b,z,u))), while weight(...,Dead,u)=0. C(y,b,z0,n,T) is the set of w such that history(...,n,w) lies in A(n,T) and survival(y,b,record(...,n),d(n,T,history(...,n,w)),w(n)) holds.

Path(T) is the function space {s in NN:s<=T}->X. Psi(y,b,z0,n,T,h)(s) is obtained by starting with z0 and visiting h(0),...,h(n) in ascending order. At a live record Live(a,z) with a<=s replace the current value by Phi(y,b,s-a,z), with truncated NN subtraction s-a converted to R; otherwise retain the current value. Dead records retain the current value. Thus the last eligible live record visited supplies the final value, or z0 if none is eligible. Gamma(y,b,z0,n,T,t)=Psi(y,b,z0,n,T,K(...,t)). rho(y,b,z0,n,T,t)=q(...,t)*weight(y,b,K(...,t)(n),d(n,T,K(...,t))).

Momentum reversal is R(x,v)=(x,-v). V_y(x)=V(x)+||x-y||^2/(2*eta). The measure nu(y) on X is the product of volume tilted by -V_y on E and the centered identity-covariance Gaussian on E. In this packet, tilt by h means withDensity(ENNReal.ofReal(exp(h(x))/integral exp(h) dvolume)); hence the first marginal has this literal normalized exponential density with h=-V_y, and division retains the stated real-zero-division convention. No independent normalizing constant is a premise. Products use position then velocity.

The required process Z has type E->E->X->NN->W->X. For fixed y,b,T its expectation operator on real functions is op(G)(z)=integral_W G(Z(y,b,z,T,w)) dP(w), and its endpoint map is (z,w)->Z(y,b,z,T,w). For a raw G and a proof hG of MemLp(G,2,nu(y)), hG.toLp(G) is the corresponding class in the real Hilbert space Lp(R,2,nu(y)). A continuous real linear map on this space has its operator norm and real Hilbert adjoint. A measure-preserving R induces the real linear isometry J by pullback of Lp classes.

CONCLUSION
There exists a process Z satisfying all of the following.
(1) Z is jointly measurable in y,b,z,s,w.
(2) For every y,b,z0,w,s,n,a, if w belongs to I(y,b,z0,s,n) and record(y,b,z0,epsilon(w),n)=Live(a.time,a.state), then Z(y,b,z0,s,w)=Phi(y,b,s-a.time,a.state), with truncated NN subtraction before conversion to a real.
(3) For every y,b,z0,w,s, if there is no n with w in I(y,b,z0,s,n), then Z(y,b,z0,s,w)=z0.
(4) For every y,b,z0, Z(y,b,z0,0,w)=z0 for P-almost every w.
(5) For every y,b,T, the endpoint map (z,w)->Z(y,b,z,T,w) is measure preserving from nu(y) product P to nu(y): it is measurable and its measure pushforward equals nu(y). For all measurable real F,G and nonnegative real constants C_F,C_G with |F(z)|<=C_F and |G(z)|<=C_G at every z, both functions z->F(z)*op(G)(z) and z->G(R(z))*op(F composed with R)(z) are integrable under nu(y), and
integral F(z)*op(G)(z) dnu(y)(z) = integral G(R(z))*op(F composed with R)(z) dnu(y)(z).
Here op uses this same Z, fixed y,b,T and P.
(6) For every measurable real G, the map (y,b,z,T)->integral G(Z(y,b,z,T,w)) dP(w) is jointly measurable. For every C>=0 with |G(z)|<=C for all z, and every y,b,z,T, the function w->G(Z(y,b,z,T,w)) is integrable under P and the absolute value of its integral is <=C.
(7) For every y,b there exists a family A_T of continuous real linear maps on Lp(R,2,nu(y)) with A_0 equal to the identity. For every T, ||A_T||<=1 and, for every raw G and every hG:MemLp(G,2,nu(y)), put opG(z)=integral G(Z(y,b,z,T,w)) dP(w). Then for nu(y)-almost every z the noise integrand is P-integrable; there exists hOp:MemLp(opG,2,nu(y)) with A_T(hG.toLp(G))=hOp.toLp(opG); and
integral |opG(z)|^2 dnu(y)(z) <= integral |G(z)|^2 dnu(y)(z).
There also exists hR:MeasurePreserving(R,nu(y),nu(y)). For the canonical linear isometry J induced by pullback through R and hR, every f in Lp satisfies J(f)(z)=f(R(z)) for nu(y)-almost every z, every f satisfies J(J(f))=f as Lp classes, and every T satisfies
A_T.adjoint = J composed with A_T composed with J
as continuous real linear maps. The precise composition in the packet is J.toContinuousLinearMap.comp(A_T.comp(J.toContinuousLinearMap)).

SCOPES
This is an existence theorem for a globally measurable, explicitly constrained process and its expectation operators. The live-interval and fallback identities hold pointwise for every noise sequence, including sequences outside the support of P. Initialization is asserted only P-almost everywhere, separately for each fixed y,b,z0; the statement does not require one exceptional set uniform across those parameters. Likewise, for each fixed y,b,T,G,hG, noise-integrability in the Lp clause holds for nu(y)-almost every starting z; no uniform exceptional set over times, functions or parameters is specified. The pullback representative formula for J is almost everywhere for each Lp class f; the identities A_0=id, J^2=id, and A_T.adjoint=J A_T J are exact equalities of maps or equivalence classes, not pointwise equalities of arbitrary representatives. Uniform bounds on bounded tests are pointwise. MemLp is square integrability with the appropriate strong measurability up to almost-everywhere equivalence; it need not give pointwise measurability of the raw representative, so the Lp clause is not limited to the earlier pointwise-measurable test class.
The stopping interval uses a weak inequality at the current event and a strict inequality at the next: t_n<=T<t_{n+1}. The deterministic prefix simplex instead uses strictly positive, strictly increasing events strictly before T. A(n,T) requires live records and strict times without specifying an initial zero time; K does specify zero at its first record. NN subtraction is truncated, and real positive parts are used exactly at the explicitly defined conversions. Top is a legitimate infinite hitting/event time; no assumption excludes it. On trajectories with no finite bracketing interval, the process has the prescribed initial-state fallback. There is no separate conclusion that every trajectory or almost every trajectory has finitely many events, and the existence of N as a total natural-valued function includes its zero fallback.
The packet defines m,K,q,C,Psi,Gamma,rho and related auxiliary objects but does not assert a density formula, path-law decomposition, change-of-variables formula or equality of measures involving them. Their presence does not add a conclusion. The measure nu(y) is fixed by the literal tilted-volume product construction, not an arbitrary invariant measure supplied as a premise. Endpoint preservation is asserted for this measure. The time-indexed family A carries no stated time regularity, semigroup composition law, positivity condition or generator identification. The adjoint identity is reversal-conjugated symmetry, not a claim that each A_T is self-adjoint. No uniqueness of Z or of the witness family is expressly asserted. The packet's compiled flag is reported input metadata only; this reconstruction performs no independent Lean validation and no source comparison or acceptance.
