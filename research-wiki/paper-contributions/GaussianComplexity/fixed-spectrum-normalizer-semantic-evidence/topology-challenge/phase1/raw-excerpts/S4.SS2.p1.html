This subsection states the four lemmas used in the proof of Theorem 4.1. Lemma 4.3 places the output of any qq-query algorithm in a block Krylov space generated by independent Gaussian vectors, Lemma 4.4 bounds a quadratic form with cosine weights uniformly over this space, Lemma 4.5 evaluates the same quadratic form under the target, and Lemma 4.6 verifies that the hard family is admissible. All proofs are deferred to Appendix C.
