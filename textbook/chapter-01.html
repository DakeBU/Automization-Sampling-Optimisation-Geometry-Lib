    The Langevin Diffusion in Continuous Time

    Build the analytic language that turns Langevin dynamics into quantitative convergence estimates.

    Begin with 1.1 Open this chapter in the canonical August 9 source ↗
    Formal topologyOpen this chapter's Lean branches↗

    Chapter route

    This chapter develops Markov semigroup, infinitesimal generator, Dirichlet form, Poincaré and log-Sobolev inequalities. Its main destination is to connect the definitions below to the results that later chapters consume.

    Core definitions

    • A Markov semigroup evolves observables by conditional expectation.
    • The infinitesimal generator is a time-zero semigroup derivative on its stated domain.
    • The overdamped Langevin SDE has drift minus the potential gradient and isotropic Brownian noise.
    • The Wasserstein gradient-flow viewpoint identifies the Fokker-Planck evolution with entropy dissipation.

    Main results

    • Itô's formula yields the formal Langevin generator.
    • The weighted integration-by-parts calculation predicts the Gibbs stationary density.
    • Poincaré and log-Sobolev inequalities turn dissipation identities into exponential convergence.
    • Strong convexity supplies the condition-number-dependent convergence scale summarized at the end of the chapter.

    Why is this chapter route valid?

    Analytic contracts

    • The weighted integration-by-parts identity first needs compactly supported test functions or a justified cutoff limit.
    • Pointwise exhaustion of a cutoff does not imply convergence of its gradient term under an integral.
    • A totalized Fréchet derivative equal to zero outside a support set does not assert differentiability there.
    • The score-weighted source field must be integrable before dominated convergence can be applied.
    • Formal symmetry, a closed generator on a stated domain, stationarity, and invariance are separate claims.

    Open boundaries

    • Concrete Langevin Markov semigroup construction (external/upstream dependency)
    • Semigroup-stable generator-domain extension beyond the compiled C_c^2 core
    • Full invariant Gibbs law and measure-determining extension

    Canonical LSI to finite KL--Fisher bridge

    Sinho Chewi; ASTIS supplies the RN/KL and generator-domain formal details. No author endorsement implied.

    current public PDF · Definition 1.2.25 and the standard canonical-density specialization · faithful paraphrase

    Complete source statement (Samplinglib restatement)

    An established density-form generator LSI, applied to the admissible canonical RN density and combined with the canonical Dirichlet--Fisher identity, gives finite Mathlib KL and KL ≤ (C/2) FI.

    • Canonical density entropy is integrable and normalized.
    • The canonical score is genuinely differentiable almost everywhere with integrable squared norm.
    • Generator integration-by-parts, symmetry and carré-du-champ/score identities hold for this pair.
    • For the Lee--Shen--Tian curvature rate lambda, substitute C=lambda^{-1}.

    finite canonical KL and Fisher control

    \[\mathrm{KL}(\mu\|\pi)<\infty,\quad \mathrm{KL}(\mu\|\pi)\le\frac{C}{2}\mathrm{FI}(\mu\|\pi)\]

    A canonical generator LSI gives finite KL and a Fisher bound

    AutoSamplingTheory.TechnicalLemmas.FunctionalInequalities.CanonicalLogSobolev.finite_klDiv_and_toReal_le_half_mul_information · theorem · Teaching coverage

    Read this first

    Mathematical statement

    Let mu be a probability law and pi a reference law on finite-dimensional Euclidean space. Assume pi satisfies the density-form generator log-Sobolev inequality with constant C. If the canonical density dmu/dpi is admissible, its canonical log-ratio has a finite smooth score, and the generator integration-by-parts and carré-du-champ identities identify its Dirichlet form with Fisher information, then KL(mu||pi) is finite and KL(mu||pi) ≤ (C/2) FI(mu||pi).

    \[\mathrm{KL}(\mu\|\pi)<\infty,\qquad \mathrm{KL}(\mu\|\pi)\le \frac{C}{2}\,\mathrm{FI}(\mu\|\pi).\]

    Proof roadmap

    1. 1Extract genuine KL finiteness

      Entropy admissibility gives integrability of rho log rho under pi. Mathlib transports this to integrability of the canonical log-likelihood ratio under mu, which together with absolute continuity proves that the ENNReal KL divergence is not infinity.

    2. 2Apply LSI to the canonical density

      The supplied LSI is specialized to rho=dmu/dpi. Its entropy side is a genuine finite integral because admissibility was stated before applying the inequality.

    3. 3Identify entropy with canonical KL

      The Radon--Nikodym change-of-measure identity rewrites the density entropy as the integral of Mathlib's canonical log-ratio under mu, hence as klDiv.toReal.

    4. 4Identify Dirichlet energy with Fisher information

      The already verified integration-by-parts and carré-du-champ bridge turns the generator Dirichlet form into the canonical squared-score integral.

    Full assumptions and notation

    All objects and hypotheses

    • mu is a probability measure; hLSI supplies that pi is a probability measure and C>0.
    • The canonical Radon--Nikodym density is nonnegative, normalized and integrable, with integrable rho log rho and rho L(log rho).
    • mu is absolutely continuous with respect to pi; the canonical log-ratio is differentiable mu-almost everywhere and its squared score is integrable.
    • The density/log-density pair lies in the generator integration-by-parts domain and its carré-du-champ is rho times the squared score.
    • Chewi uses C. SALD and Lee--Shen--Tian use the reciprocal rate c_LSI=lambda=C^{-1}.

    Mathematical proof

    1. Extract genuine KL finiteness

    Entropy admissibility gives integrability of rho log rho under pi. Mathlib transports this to integrability of the canonical log-likelihood ratio under mu, which together with absolute continuity proves that the ENNReal KL divergence is not infinity.

    \[\rho\log\rho\in L^1(\pi)\Longrightarrow \log(d\mu/d\pi)\in L^1(\mu)\Longrightarrow \mathrm{KL}(\mu\|\pi)<\infty.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    integrable_rnDeriv_mul_log_iff; InformationTheory.klDiv_ne_top

    2. Apply LSI to the canonical density

    The supplied LSI is specialized to rho=dmu/dpi. Its entropy side is a genuine finite integral because admissibility was stated before applying the inequality.

    \[\operatorname{Ent}_{\pi}(\rho)\le \frac C2\,\mathcal E_{\pi}(\rho,\log\rho).\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    Generator.SatisfiesLogSobolev; Generator.LogSobolevAdmissible

    3. Identify entropy with canonical KL

    The Radon--Nikodym change-of-measure identity rewrites the density entropy as the integral of Mathlib's canonical log-ratio under mu, hence as klDiv.toReal.

    \[\int \rho\log\rho\,d\pi=\int\log(d\mu/d\pi)\,d\mu=\mathrm{KL}(\mu\|\pi).\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    integral_rnDeriv_mul_log; RNLogRatio.toReal_klDiv_eq_integral_logRatio_of_probability

    4. Identify Dirichlet energy with Fisher information

    The already verified integration-by-parts and carré-du-champ bridge turns the generator Dirichlet form into the canonical squared-score integral.

    \[\mathcal E_{\pi}(\rho,\log\rho)=\int\|\nabla\log(d\mu/d\pi)\|^2\,d\mu=\mathrm{FI}(\mu\|\pi).\]

    theorem finite_klDiv_and_toReal_le_half_mul_information
    (mu pi : Measure (EuclideanSpace ℝ ι)) [IsProbabilityMeasure mu]
    [SigmaFinite mu] [Measure.HaveLebesgueDecomposition mu pi]
    (generator :
      (EuclideanSpace ℝ ι → ℝ) →ₗ[ℝ] (EuclideanSpace ℝ ι → ℝ))
    {C : ℝ}
    (hLSI : Generator.SatisfiesLogSobolev pi generator C)
    (hAdmissible : Generator.LogSobolevAdmissible pi generator
      (InformationTheory.RNLogRatio.density mu pi))
    (hScore :
      InformationTheory.CanonicalRelativeFisher.SmoothFiniteScoreDomain mu pi)
    (hPair :
      InformationTheory.CanonicalDirichletFisher.DirichletPairDomain
        mu pi generator)
    (hGamma :
      InformationTheory.CanonicalDirichletFisher.HasCanonicalFisherGamma
        mu pi generator) :
    _root_.InformationTheory.klDiv mu pi ≠ ∞ ∧
      (_root_.InformationTheory.klDiv mu pi).toReal ≤
        (C / 2) *
          InformationTheory.CanonicalRelativeFisher.information mu pi hScore := by
  let _ : IsProbabilityMeasure pi := hLSI.1
  have hLogRatioIntegrable : Integrable (MeasureTheory.llr mu pi) mu := by
    rw [← MeasureTheory.integrable_rnDeriv_mul_log_iff
      hScore.absolutelyContinuous]
    simpa [InformationTheory.RNLogRatio.density] using
      hAdmissible.2.2.2.1
  have hFinite : _root_.InformationTheory.klDiv mu pi ≠ ∞ :=
    _root_.InformationTheory.klDiv_ne_top
      hScore.absolutelyContinuous hLogRatioIntegrable
  refine ⟨hFinite, ?_⟩
  have hApplied := hLSI.2.2
    (InformationTheory.RNLogRatio.density mu pi) hAdmissible
  have hEntropy :
      Generator.densityEntropy pi
          (InformationTheory.RNLogRatio.density mu pi) =
        (_root_.InformationTheory.klDiv mu pi).toReal := by
    unfold Generator.densityEntropy
    change
      (∫ x, (mu.rnDeriv pi x).toReal *
        Real.log (mu.rnDeriv pi x).toReal ∂pi) =
        (_root_.InformationTheory.klDiv mu pi).toReal
    rw [MeasureTheory.integral_rnDeriv_mul_log
      hScore.absolutelyContinuous]
    simpa [InformationTheory.RNLogRatio.logRatio] using
      (InformationTheory.RNLogRatio.toReal_klDiv_eq_integral_logRatio_of_probability
        mu pi hScore.absolutelyContinuous).symm
  have hDirichlet :
      Generator.dirichletForm pi generator
          (InformationTheory.RNLogRatio.density mu pi)
          (fun x => Real.log
            (InformationTheory.RNLogRatio.density mu pi x)) =
        InformationTheory.CanonicalRelativeFisher.information mu pi hScore := by
    rw [show
      (fun x => Real.log (InformationTheory.RNLogRatio.density mu pi x)) =
        InformationTheory.RNLogRatio.logRatio mu pi by
      funext x
      exact (InformationTheory.RNLogRatio.logRatio_apply mu pi x).symm]
    exact
      InformationTheory.CanonicalDirichletFisher.dirichletForm_density_logRatio_eq_information
        mu pi generator hScore hPair hGamma
  rw [hEntropy, hDirichlet] at hApplied
  exact hApplied

    Scope and omitted-condition boundaries
    • This theorem does not prove that any concrete Gibbs measure satisfies LSI.
    • It does not prove Bakry--Emery, Talagrand T2, Wasserstein contraction or an SPHMC/PBPS main theorem.
    • It does not derive canonical score differentiability from almost-everywhere agreement with an explicit representative.
    • KL finiteness comes from hAdmissible and is returned explicitly; toReal of infinity is never accepted as evidence.
