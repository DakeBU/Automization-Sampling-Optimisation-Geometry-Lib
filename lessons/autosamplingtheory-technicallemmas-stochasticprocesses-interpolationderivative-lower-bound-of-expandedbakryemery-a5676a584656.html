    Curvature controls the interpolation derivative expression

    AutoSamplingTheory.TechnicalLemmas.StochasticProcesses.interpolationDerivative_lower_bound_of_expandedBakryEmery · theorem · Teaching coverage

    Read this first

    Mathematical statement

    Let L be a real-linear generator and suppose it satisfies CD(α,∞): αΓ(f)≤Γ₂(f). Expanding Γ₂ gives the pointwise lower bound LΓ(f)−2Γ(f,Lf)≥2αΓ(f).

    \[\Gamma_2(f)\ge\alpha\Gamma(f)\Longrightarrow L\Gamma(f)-2\Gamma(f,Lf)\ge2\alpha\Gamma(f)\]

    Proof roadmap

    1. 1Specialize the expanded curvature premise

      At the chosen observable and point, the globally quantified hypothesis gives the exact one-half normalized inequality.

    2. 2Combine the repeated mixed terms

      The two identical subtractions are exactly twice the mixed carré-du-champ term.

    3. 3Clear the factor one half

      Multiply the specialized inequality by two and normalize the real arithmetic.

    Full assumptions and notation

    All objects and hypotheses

    • The state space X is arbitrary.
    • L is a real-linear map from real-valued observables on X to real-valued observables on X.
    • The curvature parameter α is strictly positive.
    • For every observable g and point y, the exact expanded inequality αΓ(g,g)(y)≤(1/2)(LΓ(g,g)(y)−Γ(g,Lg)(y)−Γ(g,Lg)(y)) holds.
    • No semigroup or differentiability assumption is present.

    Mathematical proof

    1. Specialize the expanded curvature premise

    At the chosen observable and point, the globally quantified hypothesis gives the exact one-half normalized inequality.

    \[\alpha\Gamma(f)(x)\le\tfrac12(L\Gamma(f)(x)-\Gamma(f,Lf)(x)-\Gamma(f,Lf)(x))\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    Specialize hcurvature to f and x.

    2. Combine the repeated mixed terms

    The two identical subtractions are exactly twice the mixed carré-du-champ term.

    \[\Gamma(f,Lf)+\Gamma(f,Lf)=2\Gamma(f,Lf)\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    Real-ring normalization identifies the repeated terms.

    3. Clear the factor one half

    Multiply the specialized inequality by two and normalize the real arithmetic.

    \[2\alpha\Gamma(f)\le L\Gamma(f)-2\Gamma(f,Lf)\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    nlinarith closes the scalar real inequality.
