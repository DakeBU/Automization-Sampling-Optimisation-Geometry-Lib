

Take 
[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m1] c_{0}\leq 1/16
. Then

[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m2] \gamma_{\rm gap}\leq 2\sqrt{\alpha\eta}\leq 1/2
, and consequently

[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m3] \Lambda_{\rho}\geq\log 2
.
We compare the actual update with the idealized operator analyzed above.
Let 
[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m4] g\coloneqq\mathsf{K}_{\rm id}f
,

[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m5] g_{\mathsf{P}}\coloneqq\mathsf{P}g
, and

[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m6] g_{\mathsf{V}}\coloneqq\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\mathsf{P}^{\perp}g
.
By the definitions of 
[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m7] \mathsf{K}
 and 
[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m8] \mathsf{K}_{\rm id}
,

[MATH A2.Ex23.m1] \displaystyle\mathsf{K}f-\mathsf{K}_{\rm id}f

[MATH A2.Ex23.m2] \displaystyle=\mathsf{U}\bigl[I+(1-\rho)\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}\bigr]f_{\perp}

[MATH A2.Ex24.m1] \displaystyle=\mathsf{U}\bigl[\rho f_{\perp}+(1-\rho)(I+\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp})f_{\perp}\bigr]\,.

Using 
[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m9] \mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}=\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}
 and

[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m10] \mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\mathsf{U}_{\perp\perp}=-\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}
, the two components of
this difference are

[MATH A2.Ex25.m1] \displaystyle\mathsf{P}(\mathsf{K}f-\mathsf{K}_{\rm id}f)

[MATH A2.Ex25.m2] \displaystyle=\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\bigl[I+(1-\rho)\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}\bigr]f_{\perp}\,,

[MATH A2.Ex26.m1] \displaystyle\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\mathsf{P}^{\perp}(\mathsf{K}f-\mathsf{K}_{\rm id}f)

[MATH A2.Ex26.m2] \displaystyle=-\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\bigl[I+(1-\rho)\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}\bigr]f_{\perp}\,.

The same vector therefore determines both components of the error. Denote
it by

[MATH A2.Ex27.m1] r_{\rho}\coloneqq\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\bigl[I+(1-\rho)\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}\bigr]f_{\perp}=\rho f_{\mathsf{V}}+(1-\rho)r\,,\hskip 20.00003ptr\coloneqq\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}(I+\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp})f_{\perp}\,.

Both 
[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m11] r
 and 
[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m12] r_{\rho}
 belong to 
[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m13] \mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0}
. In

[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m14] r_{\rho}
, the first
term comes from refreshment, while the second measures the departure of
the half turn from 
[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m15] -I_{\mathfrak{H}_{\perp}}
.
Consequently,

[MATH A2.E27.m1] (\mathsf{K}f)_{\mathsf{P}}=g_{\mathsf{P}}+\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}r_{\rho}\,,\hskip 20.00003pt(\mathsf{K}f)_{\mathsf{V}}=g_{\mathsf{V}}-\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}r_{\rho}\,.

(B.27)

Substituting (B.27) into the definition
(B.20) of the corrector 
[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m16] \mathscr{C}
 and expanding
gives

[MATH A2.Ex28.m1] \displaystyle\mathscr{C}\bigl((\mathsf{K}f)_{\mathsf{P}},(\mathsf{K}f)_{\mathsf{V}}\bigr)-\mathscr{C}(g_{\mathsf{P}},g_{\mathsf{V}})

[MATH A2.Ex29.m1] \displaystyle\hskip 20.00003pt=\bigl\langle g_{\mathsf{P}},\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}r_{\rho}\bigr\rangle+\bigl\langle g_{\mathsf{V}},\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}r_{\rho}\bigr\rangle+\frac{1}{2}\,\bigl(\|\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}r_{\rho}\|^{2}-\|\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}r_{\rho}\|^{2}\bigr)

[MATH A2.Ex30.m1] \displaystyle\hskip 20.00003pt\hskip 20.00003pt+{}\bigl\langle\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}g_{\mathsf{P}},\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}r_{\rho}\bigr\rangle-\bigl\langle\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}r_{\rho},g_{\mathsf{V}}\bigr\rangle+\|\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}r_{\rho}\|^{2}

[MATH A2.Ex31.m1] \displaystyle\hskip 20.00003pt=\bigl\langle g_{\mathsf{P}},\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}r_{\rho}\bigr\rangle+\bigl\langle\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}g_{\mathsf{P}},\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}r_{\rho}\bigr\rangle+\frac{1}{2}\,\bigl(\|\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}r_{\rho}\|^{2}+\|\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}r_{\rho}\|^{2}\bigr)\,.

In the last equality, the two terms containing 
[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m17] g_{\mathsf{V}}
 cancel.
For the terms linear in 
[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m18] g_{\mathsf{P}}
, the commutation of

[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m19] \mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}
 with 
[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m20] \mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}
 and the identity

[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m21] \mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}^{2}+\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{2}=I
 give

[MATH A2.Ex32.m1] \displaystyle\bigl\langle g_{\mathsf{P}},\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}r_{\rho}\bigr\rangle+\bigl\langle\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}g_{\mathsf{P}},\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}r_{\rho}\bigr\rangle

[MATH A2.Ex33.m1] \displaystyle\hskip 20.00003pt=\bigl\langle g_{\mathsf{P}},(\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}+\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}^{2})r_{\rho}\bigr\rangle=\bigl\langle g_{\mathsf{P}},\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}r_{\rho}\bigr\rangle\,.

The same identity reduces the quadratic terms to

[MATH A2.Ex34.m1] \frac{1}{2}\,\bigl(\|\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}r_{\rho}\|^{2}+\|\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}r_{\rho}\|^{2}\bigr)=\frac{1}{2}\,\|r_{\rho}\|^{2}\,.

Consequently, combined with (B.21),

[MATH A2.Ex35.m1] \displaystyle\mathscr{C}\bigl((\mathsf{K}f)_{\mathsf{P}},(\mathsf{K}f)_{\mathsf{V}}\bigr)-\mathscr{C}(f_{\mathsf{P}},f_{\mathsf{V}})

[MATH A2.Ex36.m1] \displaystyle\hskip 10.00002pt=\bigl[\mathscr{C}\bigl((\mathsf{K}f)_{\mathsf{P}},(\mathsf{K}f)_{\mathsf{V}}\bigr)-\mathscr{C}(g_{\mathsf{P}},g_{\mathsf{V}})\bigr]+\bigl[\mathscr{C}(g_{\mathsf{P}},g_{\mathsf{V}})-\mathscr{C}(f_{\mathsf{P}},f_{\mathsf{V}})\bigr]

[MATH A2.E28.m1] \displaystyle\hskip 10.00002pt=-\|f_{\mathsf{P}}\|^{2}+\|f_{\mathsf{V}}\|^{2}+\bigl\langle g_{\mathsf{P}},\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}r_{\rho}\bigr\rangle+\frac{1}{2}\,\|r_{\rho}\|^{2}\,.

(B.28)

Thus, it remains to estimate 
[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m22] r_{\rho}
. Let

[MATH A2.Ex37.m1] \mathsf{L}\coloneqq(I+\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp})\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}\,.

Since 
[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m23] r=\mathsf{L}^{*}f_{\perp}
, applying
(B.17) to 
[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m24] \mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}v
 and then taking
adjoints gives

[MATH A2.E29.m1] \displaystyle\|\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}r\|^{2}=\|\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}(I+\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp})f_{\perp}\|^{2}

[MATH A2.E29.m2] \displaystyle\leq C\log(1/\gamma_{\rm gap})\,\|f_{\perp}\|^{2}\,.

(B.29)

Together with 
[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m25] \mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}\succeq\gamma_{\rm gap}I
 and

[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m26] \|f_{\mathsf{V}}\|\leq\|f_{\perp}\|
, this gives

[MATH A2.E30.m1] \displaystyle\|\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}r_{\rho}\|^{2}

[MATH A2.E30.m2] \displaystyle\leq 2\rho^{2}\,\|\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}f_{\mathsf{V}}\|^{2}+2(1-\rho)^{2}\,\|\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}r\|^{2}\leq C\Lambda_{\rho}\,\|f_{\perp}\|^{2}\,.

(B.30)

Moreover, since 
[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m27] \mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}
 and 
[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m28] \mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}
 are contractions, 
[MATH A2.SS3.p8.m29] \|r_{\rho}\|\leq\rho\,\|f_{\mathsf{V}}\|+(1-\rho)\,\|r\|\leq 2\,\|f_{\perp}\|
.

The rotation formula for 
[MATH A2.SS3.p9.m1] \mathsf{K}_{\rm id}
 gives 
[MATH A2.SS3.p9.m2] \|g_{\mathsf{P}}\|^{2}\leq\|g_{\mathsf{P}}\|^{2}+\|g_{\mathsf{V}}\|^{2}=\|f_{\mathsf{P}}\|^{2}+\|f_{\mathsf{V}}\|^{2}
.
Hence, Young’s inequality and
(B.30) give

[MATH A2.Ex38.m1] \displaystyle\bigl|\bigl\langle g_{\mathsf{P}},\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}r_{\rho}\bigr\rangle\bigr|

[MATH A2.Ex38.m2] \displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{4}\,\|g_{\mathsf{P}}\|^{2}+C\,\|\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}r_{\rho}\|^{2}

[MATH A2.Ex39.m1] \displaystyle\leq\frac{1}{4}\,\|f_{\mathsf{P}}\|^{2}+\frac{1}{4}\,\|f_{\mathsf{V}}\|^{2}+C\Lambda_{\rho}\,\|f_{\perp}\|^{2}\,.

Inserting these estimates into
(B.28), and using

[MATH A2.SS3.p9.m3] \|f_{\mathsf{V}}\|\leq\|f_{\perp}\|
 and

[MATH A2.SS3.p9.m4] \Lambda_{\rho}\geq\log 2
, gives

[MATH A2.E31X.m2] \displaystyle\mathscr{C}\bigl((\mathsf{K}f)_{\mathsf{P}},(\mathsf{K}f)_{\mathsf{V}}\bigr)-\mathscr{C}(f_{\mathsf{P}},f_{\mathsf{V}})\leq-\frac{1}{2}\,\|f_{\mathsf{P}}\|^{2}+C\Lambda_{\rho}\,\|f_{\perp}\|^{2}\,.

(B.31)

Finally, (B.18) and
(B.31) give

[MATH A2.Ex40.m1] \displaystyle\mathscr{L}_{\omega}(\mathsf{K}f)-\mathscr{L}_{\omega}(f)

[MATH A2.Ex40.m2] \displaystyle\leq-\rho\,\|f_{\perp}\|^{2}+\frac{c_{\rm hyp}\rho}{\Lambda_{\rho}}\,\Bigl(-\frac{1}{2}\,\|f_{\mathsf{P}}\|^{2}+C\Lambda_{\rho}\,\|f_{\perp}\|^{2}\Bigr)

[MATH A2.Ex41.m1] \displaystyle=-\frac{c_{\rm hyp}\rho}{2\Lambda_{\rho}}\,\|f_{\mathsf{P}}\|^{2}-(1-Cc_{\rm hyp})\,\rho\,\|f_{\perp}\|^{2}\,.

Choose 
[MATH A2.SS3.p10.m1] c_{\rm hyp}\leq 1
 sufficiently small so that

[MATH A2.SS3.p10.m2] Cc_{\rm hyp}\leq 1/2
. Since 
[MATH A2.SS3.p10.m3] \Lambda_{\rho}\geq\log 2
, the last display is
then bounded above by

[MATH A2.Ex42.m1] -\frac{c\rho}{\Lambda_{\rho}}\,\bigl(\|f_{\mathsf{P}}\|^{2}+\|f_{\perp}\|^{2}\bigr)=-\frac{c\rho}{\Lambda_{\rho}}\,\|f\|^{2}\,.

It remains to compare the last norm with the Lyapunov function. The
elementary inequality 
[MATH A2.SS3.p10.m4] x/(a+x^{2})\leq 1/(2\sqrt{a})
, for 
[MATH A2.SS3.p10.m5] a>0
 and

[MATH A2.SS3.p10.m6] x\geq 0
, gives

[MATH A2.Ex43.m1] \frac{\omega}{\gamma_{\rm gap}}=c_{\rm hyp}\,\frac{\rho/\gamma_{\rm gap}}{\log(1/\gamma_{\rm gap})+(\rho/\gamma_{\rm gap})^{2}}\leq\frac{c_{\rm hyp}}{2\sqrt{\log(1/\gamma_{\rm gap})}}\leq\frac{c_{\rm hyp}}{2\sqrt{\log 2}}\leq 1\,.

Thus 
[MATH A2.SS3.p10.m7] \omega\leq\gamma_{\rm gap}
, and the upper bound in
Lemma B.3 gives

[MATH A2.Ex44.m1] \|f\|^{2}\geq\frac{2}{3}\,\mathscr{L}_{\omega}(f)\,.

Consequently,

[MATH A2.Ex45.m1] \mathscr{L}_{\omega}(\mathsf{K}f)\leq\Bigl(1-\frac{2c\rho}{3\Lambda_{\rho}}\Bigr)\,\mathscr{L}_{\omega}(f)\,,

which is (B.26) after renaming the universal
constant.
∎
