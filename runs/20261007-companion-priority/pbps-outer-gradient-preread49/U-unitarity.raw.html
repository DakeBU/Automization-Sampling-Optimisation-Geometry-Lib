Here 𝖯⟂≔I−𝖯\mathsf{P}^{\perp}\coloneqq I-\mathsf{P}, so ℌ=ℌ𝖯⊕ℌ⟂\mathfrak{H}=\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}}\oplus\mathfrak{H}_{\perp} and f=𝖯​f+𝖯⟂​ff=\mathsf{P}f+\mathsf{P}^{\perp}f. In the common terminology of hypocoercivity [14, 21], ran⁡(𝖯)\operatorname{ran}(\mathsf{P}) is the macroscopic subspace and its orthogonal complement is the microscopic subspace. Thus functions in ℌ𝖯\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}} depend only on the coordinate yy, whereas functions in ℌ⟂\mathfrak{H}_{\perp} have zero mean under πηX|Y=y\pi_{\eta}^{X\mid Y=y} for πηY\pi_{\eta}^{Y}-almost every yy.

Relative to the decomposition ℌ=ℌ𝖯⊕ℌ⟂\mathfrak{H}=\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}}\oplus\mathfrak{H}_{\perp}, we write an operator 𝖳\mathsf{T} on ℌ\mathfrak{H} in block form as

𝖳=(𝖳𝖯𝖯𝖳𝖯⟂𝖳⟂𝖯𝖳⟂⁣⟂),𝖳𝖯𝖯≔𝖯𝖳𝖯,𝖳𝖯⟂≔𝖯𝖳𝖯⟂,𝖳⟂𝖯≔𝖯⟂​𝖳𝖯,𝖳⟂⁣⟂≔𝖯⟂​𝖳𝖯⟂.\mathsf{T}=\begin{pmatrix}\mathsf{T}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}&\mathsf{T}_{\mathsf{P}\perp}\\ \mathsf{T}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}&\mathsf{T}_{\perp\perp}\end{pmatrix}\,,\hskip 20.00003pt\begin{aligned} \mathsf{T}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}&\coloneqq\mathsf{P}\mathsf{T}\mathsf{P}\,,&\mathsf{T}_{\mathsf{P}\perp}&\coloneqq\mathsf{P}\mathsf{T}\mathsf{P}^{\perp}\,,\\ \mathsf{T}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}&\coloneqq\mathsf{P}^{\perp}\mathsf{T}\mathsf{P}\,,&\mathsf{T}_{\perp\perp}&\coloneqq\mathsf{P}^{\perp}\mathsf{T}\mathsf{P}^{\perp}\,.\end{aligned} (B.3)

The first subscript specifies the target subspace and the second the source subspace; thus 𝖳a​b:ℌb→ℌa\mathsf{T}_{ab}:\mathfrak{H}_{b}\to\mathfrak{H}_{a} for a,b∈{𝖯,⟂}a,b\in\{\mathsf{P},\perp\}.

We first apply this block notation to the reflection map 𝖴\mathsf{U} from (2.14). We use the same symbol for its pullback on observables:

(𝖴​f)​(x,y)≔f⁡(𝖴⁡(x,y))=f⁡(x,2​x−y).(\mathsf{U}f)(x,y)\coloneqq f(\mathsf{U}(x,y))=f(x,2x-y)\,. (B.4)

This pullback is self-adjoint and unitary, and hence has block matrix

𝖴=(𝖴𝖯𝖯𝖴⟂𝖯∗𝖴⟂𝖯𝖴⟂⁣⟂).\mathsf{U}=\begin{pmatrix}\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}&\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\\ \mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}&\mathsf{U}_{\perp\perp}\end{pmatrix}\,.

Here 𝖴𝖯𝖯=𝖯𝖴𝖯\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}=\mathsf{P}\mathsf{U}\mathsf{P} maps ℌ𝖯\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}} to itself, whereas 𝖴⟂𝖯=𝖯⟂​𝖴𝖯\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}=\mathsf{P}^{\perp}\mathsf{U}\mathsf{P} maps ℌ𝖯\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}} to ℌ⟂\mathfrak{H}_{\perp}. Since 𝖴\mathsf{U} is self-adjoint and unitary, 𝖴𝖯𝖯\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}} is a self-adjoint contraction on ℌ𝖯\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}}. Writing 𝖴2=I\mathsf{U}^{2}=I in blocks gives

𝖴⟂𝖯∗​𝖴⟂𝖯=I−𝖴𝖯𝖯2,𝖴⟂𝖯∗​𝖴⟂⁣⟂=−𝖴𝖯𝖯​𝖴⟂𝖯∗.\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}=I-\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}^{2}\,,\hskip 20.00003pt\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\mathsf{U}_{\perp\perp}=-\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\,. (B.5)
