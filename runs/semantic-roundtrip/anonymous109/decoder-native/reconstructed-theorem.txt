Anonymous theorem reconstruction

Assumptions and conventions.
Let E be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, with its normed additive commutative group structure and a measurable structure equal to its Borel structure. Zero-dimensional E is allowed. Let V:E -> R be twice continuously Frechet differentiable. Let alpha,beta be nonnegative real numbers and eta a real number. Assume alpha>0, alpha<=beta, eta>0, beta*eta<=1, and, for every x,v in E,
  alpha*||v||^2 <= (D(DV)(x)[v])[v] <= beta*||v||^2.
Here D is the real Frechet derivative and grad V is the real Hilbert gradient. These are all the hypotheses.

Write X=E x E, with a point z=(x,p), R_+ for the nonnegative reals, and R_+ with an added top element for the extended time domain. All real division uses division-by-zero equal to zero. Write a_+=max(a,0) for Real.toNNReal, retaining its nonnegative-real type where used. Subtraction of two R_+ values is truncated at zero. An extended-nonnegative embedding of a real a means ENNReal.ofReal(a), hence the embedding of its positive part. Real interval integrals below use Lebesgue measure.

Literal definitions, for all indicated parameters.
For y,r in E, define c(y,r)=y-eta*grad V(r), and, for real t and z=(x,p),
  Phi(y,r,t,z) = ( c(y,r)+cos(t)*(x-c(y,r))+sqrt(eta)*sin(t)*p,
                  (-sin(t)/sqrt(eta))*(x-c(y,r))+cos(t)*p ).
Scalar multiplication is that of E. Define b(r,x)=grad V(x)-grad V(r),
  S(r,(x,p))=(x,p-(2*<p,b(r,x)>/||b(r,x)||^2)*b(r,x)),
  rate(r,(x,p))=sqrt(eta)*max(0,<p,b(r,x)>).
In particular the literal S formula uses zero for its quotient when b(r,x)=0. For t in R_+, set
  Lambda(y,r,z,t)=integral from 0 to t of rate(r,Phi(y,r,s,z)) ds.
For e in R_+, tau(y,r,z,e) is the infimum in the extended nonnegative time domain of all t in R_+ for which Lambda(y,r,z,t)-e >= 0. This is the displayed hittingAfter construction starting at 0, with tuple input ((y,r),z,e). If this set is empty its infimum is top.

A record lies in Rec=(R_+ x X) disjoint-union Unit. Denote a live record by live(a,z) and the unique other record by stopped. Define next(y,r,e,stopped)=stopped. On live(a,z), next is stopped if tau(y,r,z,e)=top; otherwise, writing u for that finite tau, it is
  live(a+u, S(r,Phi(y,r,u,z))).
The literal extraction used in both occurrences is untopD 0: finite tau is returned, and top would yield 0. Define record(y,r,z0,e,n) recursively by record(...,0)=live(0,z0) and
  record(...,k+1)=next(y,r,e(k),record(...,k)).
The threshold input e is a function N -> R_+.

Let P be the countable product on W=(N -> R) of unit-rate exponential probability measures on R, with coordinate-product measurable structure. For w in W, set epsilon(w)(k)=(w(k))_+. For each n in N let Hist(n)=(Fin(n+1) -> Rec). Define
  history(y,r,z0,n,w)(j)=record(y,r,z0,epsilon(w),j.val).
Fin(n) has indices 0,...,n-1. Let readTime(live(a,z))=a as a real number and readTime(stopped)=0.

For n in N and T in R_+, let Delta(n,T) be the set of real vectors t indexed by Fin(n) for which prepending 0 produces a strictly increasing vector and every t(i)<T. Equivalently, for n>0 this is 0<t(0)<...<t(n-1)<T; the definition also applies literally for n=0. Prefix(n,T) is the subtype of such vectors. Let m(n,T) be the comap of Lebesgue volume on (Fin(n) -> R) along the subtype inclusion t -> t.val, namely the induced subtype measure.
For t in Prefix(n,T), put u=(0,t(0),...,t(n-1)), indexed by Fin(n+1), and delta_i=t(i)-u(i) for i in Fin(n). Define the state sequence zAt(y,r,z0,n,T,t) by
  zAt(0)=z0,
  zAt(i+1)=S(r,Phi(y,r,delta_i,zAt(i))).
Here i as an index into Fin(n+1) in the right-hand side is i.castSucc; i+1 is i.succ. Define K(y,r,z0,n,T,t) in Hist(n) by
  K(t)(j)=live((u(j))_+, zAt(j)).
Define
  qReal(y,r,z0,n,T,t)
    = product over i in Fin(n) of
        [rate(r,Phi(y,r,delta_i,zAt(i)))
         * exp(-Lambda(y,r,zAt(i),(delta_i)_+))],
  q(y,r,z0,n,T,t)=ENNReal.ofReal(qReal(y,r,z0,n,T,t)).
The n=0 product is 1.

Let Aset(n,T) denote the set named A in the initial literal definitions: histories h for which every h(j) is live, the real function j -> readTime(h(j)) is strictly increasing, and readTime(h(i.succ))<T for every i in Fin(n). This definition alone does not additionally require the first time to be zero or require the states to follow Phi and S.
Set eventTime(y,r,z0,w,j) equal to the live record time of record(y,r,z0,epsilon(w),j), embedded in extended nonnegative time, or top if that record is stopped. Define
  I(y,r,z0,T,n)={w: eventTime(...,n)<=T<eventTime(...,n+1)}.
Define N(y,r,z0,T,w) to be the least natural n with w in I(y,r,z0,T,n), if such n exists, and 0 otherwise.
Let liveTime(live(a,z))=a in R_+ and liveTime(stopped)=0. Define
  d(n,T,h)=T-liveTime(h(n))
using truncated subtraction; h(n) is the final index of Fin(n+1).
Define survival(y,r,live(a,z),u,e) to mean u<tau(y,r,z,e_+) in extended nonnegative time, where e is real and u is nonnegative. Survival for a stopped record is false. Define
  weight(y,r,live(a,z),u)=ENNReal.ofReal(exp(-Lambda(y,r,z,u))),
  weight(y,r,stopped,u)=0.
Set C(y,r,z0,n,T) to be the w such that history(y,r,z0,n,w) belongs to Aset(n,T) and
  survival(y,r,record(y,r,z0,epsilon(w),n),
           d(n,T,history(y,r,z0,n,w)),w(n))
holds.

Let Path(T) be the set of all functions from {s in R_+:s<=T} to X. Define Psi(y,r,z0,n,T,h)(s) by the following ascending fold through j=0,...,n, initialized with accumulator z0. For a live h(j)=live(a,z), replace the accumulator by Phi(y,r,s-a,z) if a<=s, using truncated nonnegative subtraction cast to a real time; if a>s leave it unchanged. A stopped record also leaves it unchanged. The final accumulator is Psi(...)(s). Define
  Gamma(y,r,z0,n,T,t)=Psi(y,r,z0,n,T,K(y,r,z0,n,T,t)),
  rho(y,r,z0,n,T,t)=q(y,r,z0,n,T,t)
     * weight(y,r,K(y,r,z0,n,T,t)(n),d(n,T,K(y,r,z0,n,T,t))).
The product defining rho is multiplication of extended nonnegative numbers.

Finally define momentum reversal R(x,p)=(x,-p) and
  Vy(y,x)=V(x)+||x-y||^2/(2*eta),
  mu_y=volume.tilted(x -> -Vy(y,x)),
  nu_y=mu_y product gamma_E,
where gamma_E is the centered identity-covariance Gaussian on E. The literal tilted measure is volume with density
  x -> ENNReal.ofReal(exp(-Vy(y,x)) / integral exp(-Vy(y,a)) dvolume(a)).
This is the supplied literal definition with its denominator and real-division convention.

Conclusion: one process witness and all its properties.
There exists one function
  Z:E -> E -> X -> R_+ -> W -> X
satisfying all the following conjuncts simultaneously.

1. Joint measurability.
The map (((y,r),z0),(s,w)) -> Z(y,r,z0,s,w) is measurable on the displayed product parameter space. This states measurability jointly in y,r,z0,s,w.

2. Pointwise interpolation on every existing interval.
For every y,r in E, z0 in X, w in W, s in R_+, n in N, and a=(a_time,a_state) in R_+ x X, if w belongs to I(y,r,z0,s,n) and record(y,r,z0,epsilon(w),n)=live(a_time,a_state), then
  Z(y,r,z0,s,w)=Phi(y,r,(s-a_time),a_state),
where subtraction is truncated in R_+ and then cast to R. This quantification is pointwise, not almost everywhere.

3. Pointwise fallback.
For every y,r,z0,w,s, if there is no n with w in I(y,r,z0,s,n), then Z(y,r,z0,s,w)=z0.

4. Almost-everywhere initialization.
For every fixed y,r,z0, Z(y,r,z0,0,w)=z0 for P-almost every w. The exceptional set may depend on y,r,z0.

5. Invariance and a bounded-function reversal identity.
For every y,r in E and T in R_+, define
  op_T G(z)=integral G(Z(y,r,z,T,w)) dP(w),
  endpoint(z,w)=Z(y,r,z,T,w).
The endpoint map is measure preserving from nu_y product P to nu_y, meaning it is measurable and its pushforward measure equals nu_y.
Furthermore, for every real-valued F,G on X and real constants C_F,C_G, if F and G are measurable, C_F,C_G>=0, |F(z)|<=C_F for every z, and |G(z)|<=C_G for every z, then both
  z -> F(z)*op_T G(z),
  z -> G(R(z))*op_T(F composed with R)(z)
are integrable with respect to nu_y, and
  integral F(z)*op_T G(z) dnu_y(z)
    = integral G(R(z))*op_T(F composed with R)(z) dnu_y(z).
All these operators use the same process Z from the outer existential.

6. Measurability of raw expectations and the uniform bounded-function bound.
For every measurable real-valued G on X, the map
  (((y,r),z),T) -> integral G(Z(y,r,z,T,w)) dP(w)
is jointly measurable in y,r,z,T. This measurability conclusion is stated before, and without, the subsequent boundedness hypothesis.
For each real C>=0 satisfying |G(z)|<=C for every z, for every y,r,z,T, the function w -> G(Z(y,r,z,T,w)) is P-integrable and
  |integral G(Z(y,r,z,T,w)) dP(w)|<=C.

7. An L2 family, reversal, momentum averaging, and compressed position operators.
For every fixed y,r in E there exists a family Aop indexed by T in R_+ of continuous real linear maps on H=L2(nu_y;R). Aop is the locally bound A in this conclusion; it is distinct from Aset above. It satisfies Aop(0)=identity_H. For every T in R_+,
  ||Aop(T)||<=1,
and for every raw function G:X -> R and every witness hG of MemLp G 2 nu_y, put
  opG(z)=integral G(Z(y,r,z,T,w)) dP(w).
Then all of the following hold:
  (a) for nu_y-almost every z, w -> G(Z(y,r,z,T,w)) is P-integrable;
  (b) there exists hOp witnessing MemLp opG 2 nu_y such that
        Aop(T)(hG.toLp G)=hOp.toLp opG;
  (c) integral |opG(z)|^2 dnu_y(z) <= integral |G(z)|^2 dnu_y(z).
MemLp has its supplied strongly-measurable-modulo-almost-everywhere, square-integrable meaning, and toLp is the resulting equivalence class. This claim applies to all such G and hG, not merely bounded measurable representatives.

There exists a witness hR that R is measure preserving from nu_y to itself. With that same hR, define the real linear isometry J:H -> H to be the canonical L2 pullback by R. Then
  (a) for every f in H, the representative of J(f) equals z -> f(R(z)) nu_y-almost everywhere;
  (b) J(J(f))=f for every f in H;
  (c) for every T in R_+, Aop(T)^*=J composed with Aop(T) composed with J,
where composition in (c) uses the continuous linear maps underlying J and the star denotes the real Hilbert adjoint.

Using the same mu_y defined above, there also exists a witness hfst that the projection fst:X -> E is measure preserving from nu_y to mu_y. With this witness define the canonical pullback real linear isometry
  B:L2(mu_y;R) -> H,   B=composition by fst,
and let M:H -> L2(mu_y;R) be the Hilbert adjoint of the continuous linear map underlying B. Then all of the following hold for these same B,M,J,Aop:
  (a) for every f in L2(mu_y;R), the representative of B(f) equals (x,p) -> f(x) nu_y-almost everywhere;
  (b) ||B||<=1 and ||M||<=1;
  (c) M composed with B=identity on L2(mu_y;R);
  (d) for every raw G:X -> R and witness hG of MemLp G 2 nu_y, define
        gbar(x)=integral G(x,p) dgamma_E(p).
      For mu_y-almost every x, p -> G(x,p) is gamma_E-integrable, and there exists hbar witnessing MemLp gbar 2 mu_y such that
        M(hG.toLp G)=hbar.toLp gbar;
  (e) J(B(f))=B(f) for every f in L2(mu_y;R);
  (f) M composed with J=M.

Define, from these same maps,
  Kpos(T)=M composed with Aop(T) composed with B
as a continuous real linear map on L2(mu_y;R). Then Kpos(0)=identity. For every T in R_+, Kpos(T)^*=Kpos(T) and ||Kpos(T)||<=1. For every raw f:E -> R and every witness hf of MemLp f 2 mu_y, put
  kTf(x)=integral [integral f(fst(Z(y,r,(x,p),T,w))) dP(w)] dgamma_E(p).
For mu_y-almost every x, for gamma_E-almost every p, the function
  w -> f(fst(Z(y,r,(x,p),T,w)))
is P-integrable. Separately, for mu_y-almost every x, the function
  p -> integral f(fst(Z(y,r,(x,p),T,w))) dP(w)
is gamma_E-integrable. Finally there exists hk witnessing MemLp kTf 2 mu_y such that
  Kpos(T)(hf.toLp f)=hk.toLp kTf.

Scope and boundary.
Z is chosen once before all parameterwise conclusions. Aop is chosen for each y,r, before its assertions for all T; hR,hfst and the resulting J,B,M in that same existential block are shared by all its assertions for all T. Each stated almost-everywhere condition retains exactly its displayed measure and nesting. The measures, auxiliary histories, densities, prefix paths, counting function and events above are literal definitions; their presence does not itself assert additional identities or regularity. No separate assertion about m,q,rho,Gamma,C,N or nonexplosion is a conjunct of this proposition. The families Aop and Kpos have the stated operator identities and bounds, with no additional semigroup law or continuity in T asserted. This reconstruction states only the supplied proposition and gives no proof.
