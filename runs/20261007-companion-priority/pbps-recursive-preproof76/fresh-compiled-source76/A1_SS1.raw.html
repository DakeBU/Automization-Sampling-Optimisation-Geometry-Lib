A.1 The fixed-reference phase space process

Proof of the well-posedness assertion in Proposition 3.1.

Fix y,x~∈ℝdy,\widetilde{x}\in\mathbb{R}^{d}. In the notation of (3.4), the bounce rate (3.9) reads

λx~​(x,p)=η​[p⊤​hx~​(x)]+.\lambda_{\widetilde{x}}(x,p)=\sqrt{\eta}\,[p^{\top}h_{\widetilde{x}}(x)]_{+}\,.

The deterministic harmonic flow, namely the solution (4.5) of (3.8), and the bounce map are

Φty,x~​(x,p)\displaystyle\Phi_{t}^{y,\widetilde{x}}(x,p) ≔(cy,x~+(x−cy,x~)cost+ηpsint,−η−1/2(x−cy,x~)sint+pcost),\displaystyle\coloneqq\bigl(c_{y,\widetilde{x}}+(x-c_{y,\widetilde{x}})\cos t+\sqrt{\eta}\,p\sin t,\;-\eta^{-1/2}(x-c_{y,\widetilde{x}})\sin t+p\cos t\bigr)\,,

The bounce map is an involution. Although there is a possible discontinuity at hx~​(x)=0h_{\widetilde{x}}(x)=0, this creates no ambiguity because the event rate vanishes there.

when Sn+1<∞S_{n+1}<\infty. If the set is empty, set Tn+1=Sn+1=∞T_{n+1}=S_{n+1}=\infty and stop the recursion. Between jumps, set ζTn+t=Φty,x~​(ζTn)\zeta_{T_{n}+t}=\Phi_{t}^{y,\widetilde{x}}(\zeta_{T_{n}}) for 0≤t<Sn+10\leq t<S_{n+1}.

To prove non-explosion, define the shifted harmonic energy

The flow and each bounce preserve this energy. Hence, with ℰ≔Hy,x~​(x0,p0)\mathcal{E}\coloneqq H_{y,\widetilde{x}}(x_{0},p_{0}), every state produced by the recursion satisfies

‖p‖≤2​ℰ,‖x−cy,x~‖≤2​η​ℰ.\|p\|\leq\sqrt{2\mathcal{E}}\,,\hskip 20.00003pt\|x-c_{y,\widetilde{x}}\|\leq\sqrt{2\eta\mathcal{E}}\,.

Because ∇V\nabla V is β\beta-Lipschitz, λx~​(x,p)≤λ¯ℰ​(y,x~)\lambda_{\widetilde{x}}(x,p)\leq\bar{\lambda}_{\mathcal{E}}(y,\widetilde{x}) throughout the path, where

Hence,

∫0uλx~​(Φsy,x~​(ζTn))​𝑑s≤λ¯ℰ​(y,x~)​u.\int_{0}^{u}\lambda_{\widetilde{x}}(\Phi_{s}^{y,\widetilde{x}}(\zeta_{T_{n}}))\,\mathrm{d}s\leq\bar{\lambda}_{\mathcal{E}}(y,\widetilde{x})\,u\,.

If λ¯ℰ​(y,x~)=0\bar{\lambda}_{\mathcal{E}}(y,\widetilde{x})=0, the event rate vanishes and there are no jumps. Otherwise, (A.1) implies, for every finite waiting time,

Sn+1≥En+1λ¯ℰ​(y,x~).S_{n+1}\geq\frac{E_{n+1}}{\bar{\lambda}_{\mathcal{E}}(y,\widetilde{x})}\,.

If the recursion does not terminate, it follows that

almost surely by the strong law of large numbers. Thus, the jump times cannot accumulate, and the recursive construction defines a unique process for all t≥0t\geq 0. Finally, the memoryless property of the exponential clocks gives the time-homogeneous Markov property. This is precisely the integrated hazard construction of Davis [12, Section 2]. ∎

Proposition A.1 (Path reversal, invariance, and the L2L^{2} semigroup).

Let (𝖳ty,x~)t≥0(\mathsf{T}_{t}^{y,\widetilde{x}})_{t\geq 0} be the Markov transition operators of the process constructed in Proposition 3.1. For every t≥0t\geq 0,

(𝖳ty,x~)∗=𝖱𝖳ty,x~​𝖱on ​L2​(νη,y).(\mathsf{T}_{t}^{y,\widetilde{x}})^{*}=\mathsf{R}\mathsf{T}_{t}^{y,\widetilde{x}}\mathsf{R}\hskip 10.00002pt\text{on }L^{2}(\nu_{\eta,y})\,. (A.3)

Consequently:

  1. (i)

    νη,y\nu_{\eta,y} is invariant;

  2. (ii)

    (𝖳ty,x~)t≥0(\mathsf{T}_{t}^{y,\widetilde{x}})_{t\geq 0} is a strongly continuous contraction semigroup on L2​(νη,y)L^{2}(\nu_{\eta,y});

    if Πp:L2​(νη,y)→L2​(πηX|Y=y)\mathsf{\Pi}_{p}:L^{2}(\nu_{\eta,y})\to L^{2}(\pi_{\eta}^{X\mid Y=y}) denotes Gaussian momentum averaging, then Πp​𝖳ty,x~​Πp∗\mathsf{\Pi}_{p}\mathsf{T}_{t}^{y,\widetilde{x}}\mathsf{\Pi}_{p}^{*} is a self-adjoint Markov contraction on L2​(πηX|Y=y)L^{2}(\pi_{\eta}^{X\mid Y=y}).

Idea of the proof.

The identity (A.3) is a momentum-reversed form of detailed balance. To see the mechanism formally, write 𝖫y,x~=𝒜+𝒥\mathsf{L}_{y,\widetilde{x}}=\mathcal{A}+\mathcal{J}, where

𝒜​f​(x,p)\displaystyle\mathcal{A}f(x,p) ≔η​p⊤​∇xf​(x,p)−(x−cy,x~)⊤η​∇pf​(x,p),\displaystyle\coloneqq\sqrt{\eta}\,p^{\top}\nabla_{x}f(x,p)-\frac{(x-c_{y,\widetilde{x}})^{\top}}{\sqrt{\eta}}\nabla_{p}f(x,p),

Momentum reversal reverses the harmonic flow, so 𝖱​𝒜​𝖱=−𝒜\mathsf{R}\mathcal{A}\mathsf{R}=-\mathcal{A}. Moreover,

Formal integration by parts gives

The bounce map 𝖲x~\mathsf{S}_{\widetilde{x}} is a νη,y\nu_{\eta,y}-preserving involution, commutes with 𝖱\mathsf{R}, and satisfies λx~∘𝖲x~=λx~∘𝖱\lambda_{\widetilde{x}}\circ\mathsf{S}_{\widetilde{x}}=\lambda_{\widetilde{x}}\circ\mathsf{R}. A formal change of variables therefore gives

Combining the two identities yields

This generator calculation is only heuristic as stated, as the standard arguments are not immediately applicable because 𝖲x~\mathsf{S}_{\widetilde{x}} may be discontinuous where hx~​(x)=0h_{\widetilde{x}}(x)=0.
