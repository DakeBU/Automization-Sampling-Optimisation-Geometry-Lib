4.2 Properties of the Hard Family

This subsection states the four lemmas used in the proof of Theorem 4.1. Lemma 4.3 places the output of any qq-query algorithm in a block Krylov space generated by independent Gaussian vectors, Lemma 4.4 bounds a quadratic form with cosine weights uniformly over this space, Lemma 4.5 evaluates the same quadratic form under the target, and Lemma 4.6 verifies that the hard family is admissible. All proofs are deferred to Appendix C.

Krylov representation of the output.

We first control the information revealed by adaptive queries via a standard Krylov argument. The deferred-rotation argument of [22, Lemma 9.2] uses the rotational symmetry of the undisclosed eigenbasis to represent the algorithm’s output in a block Krylov space generated by independent Gaussian vectors.

For vectors g1,…,gq+1∈ℝdg_{1},\ldots,g_{q+1}\in\mathbb{R}^{d} and Λ=diag⁡(λ1,…,λd)\Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_{1},\ldots,\lambda_{d}), define

𝒦q(g1,…,gq+1)=span{Λjga:1≤a≤q+1, 0≤j≤q}.\mathcal{K}_{q}(g_{1},\ldots,g_{q+1})=\operatorname{span}\{\Lambda^{j}g_{a}:1\leq a\leq q+1,\ 0\leq j\leq q\}.
Lemma 4.3 (Krylov representation).

Fix an integer q≥0q\geq 0 with 2​q+1<d2q+1<d and let XX be the output. There exist independent vectors g1,…,gq+1∼N⁡(0,Id)g_{1},\ldots,g_{q+1}\sim N(0,I_{d}) such that

O⊤​X∈𝒦q​(g1,…,gq+1)almost surely.O^{\top}X\in\mathcal{K}_{q}(g_{1},\ldots,g_{q+1})\qquad\text{almost surely}.

The additional starting vector accounts for the output, which may lie outside the span of the query points and oracle replies. Equivalently, the lemma gives a representation O⊤​X=∑a=1q+1pa​(Λ)​gaO^{\top}X=\sum_{a=1}^{q+1}p_{a}(\Lambda)g_{a}, where each pap_{a} has degree at most qq. The polynomial coefficients may depend on the entire extended experiment. We therefore control the output through an estimate that holds simultaneously for every vector in 𝒦q\mathcal{K}_{q}. The proof of Lemma 4.3 is deferred to Appendix C.1.

A uniform bound on the Krylov space.

We next control a high-frequency cosine statistic uniformly over the space in Lemma 4.3. For a query budget qq, set

n=2​q+1,Dn=diag⁡(cos⁡(n​θ1),…,cos⁡(n​θd)).n=2q+1,\qquad D_{n}=\operatorname{diag}\bigl(\cos(n\theta_{1}),\ldots,\cos(n\theta_{d})\bigr).

The choice of nn places this statistic above all frequencies arising from products of two degree-qq cosine polynomials.

Lemma 4.4 (Uniform bound on the Krylov space).

There is an absolute constant C>0C>0 such that the following holds. Let q≥0q\geq 0 satisfy 2​q+1<d2q+1<d, let g1,…,gq+1g_{1},\ldots,g_{q+1} be independent N⁡(0,Id)N(0,I_{d}) vectors, and let 𝒦q=𝒦q​(g1,…,gq+1)\mathcal{K}_{q}=\mathcal{K}_{q}(g_{1},\ldots,g_{q+1}). Define

τd,q=C⁡[(q+1)​log⁡dd+(q+1)​(q+1+log⁡d)​log⁡dd].\tau_{d,q}=C\left[(q+1)\sqrt{\frac{\log d}{d}}+\frac{(q+1)(q+1+\log d)\log d}{d}\right].
