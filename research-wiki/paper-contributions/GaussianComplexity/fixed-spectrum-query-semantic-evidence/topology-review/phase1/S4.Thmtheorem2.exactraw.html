Remark 4.2 (Comparison with κ=2\kappa=2 case).

At κ=2\kappa=2, the spectrum in Eq. (11) recovers the original construction in [22]. More generally, if A2A_{2} denotes the original precision matrix with the same rotation OO, then A=(κ−1)​A2−(κ−2)​IdA=(\kappa-1)A_{2}-(\kappa-2)I_{d}. Thus one matrix–vector reply for either matrix determines the corresponding reply for the other. The proof compares ∑icos⁡(n​θi)​⟨x,O​ei⟩2\sum_{i}\cos(n\theta_{i})\langle x,Oe_{i}\rangle^{2} with ‖x‖2\|x\|^{2}, where n=2​q+1n=2q+1. For the output of a qq-query algorithm, the ratio is at most d−Ω⁡(1)d^{-\Omega(1)} in absolute value (Lemma 4.4), whereas under the target the ratio of the means has absolute value e−Θ⁡(n/κ)e^{-\Theta(n/\sqrt{\kappa})} (Lemma 4.5). Hence the test works for nn up to order κ​log⁡d\sqrt{\kappa}\log d, rather than log⁡d\log d as at κ=2\kappa=2, which is the source of the factor κ\sqrt{\kappa} in Theorem 4.1.
