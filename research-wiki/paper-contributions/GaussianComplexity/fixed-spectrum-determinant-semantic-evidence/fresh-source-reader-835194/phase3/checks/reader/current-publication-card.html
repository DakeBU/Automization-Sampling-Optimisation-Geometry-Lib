Lean uses zero-based \(j.\mathrm{val}+1/2\), corresponding to source \(i=j.\mathrm{val}+1\). The determinant is the genuine real finite determinant and the product ranges over every coordinate once. theorem actual_precision_det {d : ℕ} (hd : 2 ≤ d)
    {κ : ℝ} (hκ : 2 ≤ κ)
    (O : Matrix.orthogonalGroup (Fin d) ℝ) :
    let θ : Fin d → ℝ := fun i => ((i.val : ℝ) + 1 / 2) * Real.pi / (d : ℝ)

      (κ + 1) / 2 + (κ - 1) / 2 * Real.cos (θ i)
