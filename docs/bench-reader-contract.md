# Bench reader and evaluation contract

Owner-directed refinement, 2026-11-05. This extends the current Bench Goal; it
does not create another Goal or certify a theorem, model score or evaluation
backend. Canonical questions, families, splits and source reviews are unchanged.

## Navigation and reading depth

- Public name: **Sampling-Optimisation-Geometry-Bench**. Keep existing URLs and
  machine identifiers compatible. SamplingBench, OptimisationBench and
  GeometryBench are three views of the same catalog. Riemannian and other shared
  tasks may cross-list; global counts deduplicate core/family identity.
- Libraries and Benchmarks are peer sidebar sections. Lean Graph and Functor
  Hypergraph have immediate visible links, not names buried in project utilities.
- Landing pages show a small number of visual choices and short captions. Move
  coverage matrices, protocol details and downloads to disclosures or focused
  pages. Search/pagination changes presentation only; every task remains reachable.
- Preserve complete reviewed assumptions, statements, formula proofs, adjacent
  folded exact Lean, source, test and graph links in the task/reader drill-down.
  Never shorten gold answers or alter review/status data for cosmetic reasons.
- Navigation SVGs are original schematic illustrations, not proof certificates.
  Actual formal structure, source-proof overlays and conceptual mechanisms keep
  their distinct evidence meanings in the real graph views.

## What the benchmark must measure

The intended tasks test domain knowledge, mathematical reasoning and Agentic Lean
proof work, not merely familiarity with an interface. NL and Lean views retain
separate levels and scoring. L1–L5 is provisional until empirical calibration.
No easy filler, fabricated research answers or import-distance difficulty.

Readiness must be visible. At this checkpoint, prose run recording requires
mathematical human assessment; native Harbor executes Graph JSON tasks; trusted
Lean references compile. These do not supply a safe arbitrary-submission Lean
evaluator or measured agent performance. The Goal next requires an actually tested
isolated fixed-target Lean environment with faithful target/axiom checks, useful
compiler interaction, resource/network bounds and full failure accounting. An
initial supported submission subset must state its restrictions, not claim full
Lean-language or security coverage. Do not execute model Lean on the host.

Real model runs must record model/version, prompts, retrieval/tools, attempts,
resources, raw responses, grading and failures. Oracle/nop software fixtures are
not model scores. Public-dev families are not held-out tests; answers and verifier
material stay outside prompt-only packages. Keep restricted ATLAS material out.

## Acceptance and maintenance

Build the existing static site; do not introduce a disconnected frontend or
another manually maintained theorem-status list. Collection membership comes
from website/content/bench_navigation.json and the canonical seven-domain catalog.
Counts and links are computed from real records. Preserve source/review bindings.

Check all three collection routes, collection overlap/identity, task search and
pagination, source/graph links, folded metadata, keyboard use and desktop/mobile
screenshots. Verify visible navigation and first-screen density, not just HTML
existence. Reuse the all-core offline formula/Lean/copy/download checks. Maintain
whole-site link/status checks and actual clean CI/deployment/public read-back.
Do not declare the whole website human-ready from three screenshots, or complete
the Bench Goal after one UI or theorem packet.
