      M-kappa transport controls ordinary phase transport

      AutoSamplingTheory.ExampleCases.SmoothedPicardHMC.PhaseMetric.wassersteinDistance_sq_le_phaseWassersteinSq · theorem · Teaching coverage

      Read this first

      Mathematical statement

      Ordinary squared Wasserstein distance is at most six times the M-kappa squared transport value.

      \[W_2^2(\mu,\nu)\le6W_{2,M_\kappa}^2(\mu,\nu).\]

      Proof roadmap

      1. 1Lower-bound the twisted form

        Cauchy--Schwarz and a completed square absorb the cross term without losing kappa.

      2. 2Compare each coupling

        The product max norm is bounded by the Euclidean sum of coordinate squares.

      3. 3Remove near-optimal excess

        Choose a twisted coupling within delta of the infimum and let delta vanish; no optimizer is assumed.

      Full assumptions and notation

      All objects and hypotheses

      • kappa>=1.
      • The two laws are arbitrary measures on phase space.

      Mathematical proof

      1. Lower-bound the twisted form

      Cauchy--Schwarz and a completed square absorb the cross term without losing kappa.

      \[q_\kappa(x,p)\ge\frac16(\|x\|^2+\|p\|^2).\]
      Corresponding Lean step

      Private ordinary_energy_le_phaseQuadraticForm.

      2. Compare each coupling

      The product max norm is bounded by the Euclidean sum of coordinate squares.

      \[\|z-z'\|_{\max}^2\le6q_\kappa(z-z').\]
      Corresponding Lean step

      lintegral_mono and lintegral_const_mul.

      3. Remove near-optimal excess

      Choose a twisted coupling within delta of the infimum and let delta vanish; no optimizer is assumed.

      theorem wassersteinDistance_sq_le_phaseWassersteinSq
    [MeasurableSpace E] [BorelSpace E] [SecondCountableTopology E]
    {kappa : ℝ} (hkappa : 1 ≤ kappa) (mu nu : Measure (E × E)) :
    WassersteinSpace.wassersteinDistance mu nu ^ 2 ≤
      ENNReal.ofReal 6 * phaseWassersteinSq kappa mu nu := by
  rw [WassersteinSpace.wassersteinDistance_sq]
  apply ENNReal.le_of_forall_pos_le_add
  intro epsilon hepsilon hfinite
  let A : ℝ≥0∞ := ENNReal.ofReal 6
  let C : ℝ≥0∞ := phaseWassersteinSq kappa mu nu
  have hA0 : A ≠ 0 := by
    simp [A]
  have hAtop : A ≠ ⊤ := ENNReal.ofReal_ne_top
  have hCtop : C < ⊤ := by
    rcases ENNReal.mul_lt_top_iff.mp hfinite with h | h | h
    · exact h.2
    · exact (hA0 h).elim
    · simpa [C, h]
  let delta : ℝ≥0∞ := (epsilon : ℝ≥0∞) / A
  have hdelta0 : delta ≠ 0 := by
    have hepsilon0 : (epsilon : ℝ≥0∞) ≠ 0 := by exact_mod_cast hepsilon.ne'
    exact (ENNReal.div_pos hepsilon0 hAtop).ne'
  have hstrict : C < C + delta :=
    ENNReal.lt_add_right hCtop.ne hdelta0
  obtain ⟨gamma, hgamma, hgammaCost⟩ :=
    Transport.exists_isCoupling_lintegral_lt_of_transportCost_lt
      (phaseCost (E := E) kappa) mu nu hstrict
  have hphaseMeas : Measurable (phaseCost (E := E) kappa) := by
    unfold phaseCost phaseQuadraticForm
    fun_prop
  calc
    Transport.transportCost (WassersteinSpace.quadraticCost (E := E × E)) mu nu ≤
        ∫⁻ z, WassersteinSpace.quadraticCost (E := E × E) z ∂gamma :=
      Transport.transportCost_le_lintegral_of_isCoupling _ mu nu gamma hgamma
    _ ≤ ∫⁻ z, A * phaseCost (E := E) kappa z ∂gamma := by
      apply lintegral_mono
      intro z
      exact quadraticCost_le_phaseCost hkappa z
    _ = A * ∫⁻ z, phaseCost (E := E) kappa z ∂gamma := by
      exact lintegral_const_mul _ hphaseMeas
    _ ≤ A * (C + delta) := by
      gcongr
    _ = A * C + (epsilon : ℝ≥0∞) := by
      rw [mul_add, show A * delta = (epsilon : ℝ≥0∞) by
        exact ENNReal.mul_div_cancel hA0 hAtop]
    _ = ENNReal.ofReal 6 * phaseWassersteinSq kappa mu nu +
        (epsilon : ℝ≥0∞) := rfl

      Mathematical statement

      The incoming phase energy is integrable and bounded by 2(Mx+Mp)+24r^2.

      \[\mathbb E_\mu[\|X-x_\star\|^2+\|P\|^2]\le2(M_X+M_P)+24r^2.\]

      Proof roadmap

      1. 1Convert the radius

        The uniform metric comparison gives the ordinary radius sqrt(6)|r|.

      2. 2Reuse the compiled coordinate theorem

        The earlier theorem proves both integrability and the ordinary-Wasserstein moment estimate.

      3. 3Normalize the constant

        Expand the square to obtain the explicit final factor.

      Full assumptions and notation

      All objects and hypotheses
