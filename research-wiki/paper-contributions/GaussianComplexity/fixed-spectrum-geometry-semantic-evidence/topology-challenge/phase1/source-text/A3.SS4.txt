C.4 Proof of Lemma 4.6 Proof of Lemma 4.6 . Since $0<\theta_{i}<\pi$ , we have $-1<\cos\theta_{i}<1$ ,
and hence $1<\lambda_{i}<\kappa$ .
As $A=O\Lambda O^{\top}$ with $O$ orthogonal, this gives $I_{d}\preceq A\preceq\kappa I_{d}$ . The potential $V_{A}(x)=\tfrac{1}{2}x^{\top}Ax$ is $C^{\infty}$ with Hessian $\nabla^{2}V_{A}(x)=A$ .
Thus it is $1$ -strongly convex and $\kappa$ -smooth.
Moreover, $V_{A}(0)=0$ and $V_{A}(x)\geq\tfrac{1}{2}\|x\|^{2}>0$ for $x\neq 0$ ,
so $0$ is its unique minimizer. The associated target is $N(0,A^{-1})$ .
Gaussian integration gives $Z_{A}=(2\pi)^{d/2}(\det A)^{-1/2}=(2\pi)^{d/2}(\det\Lambda)^{-1/2}.$ Since $\Lambda$ is fixed, $Z_{A}$ is independent of $O$ and is known whenever the spectrum is public.
∎
