
B.3 Modified $L^{2}$ contraction and proof of the main theorem
We now prove Theorem 3.5 using a discrete modified $L^{2}$ 
argument. For $f\in L_{0}^{2}(\pi_{\eta})$ , write
 $f_{\mathsf{P}}\coloneqq\mathsf{P}f\,,\hskip 20.00003ptf_{\perp}\coloneqq\mathsf{P}^{\perp}f\,.$ 
Here $f_{\mathsf{P}}$ and $f_{\perp}$ are, respectively, the macroscopic
and microscopic parts of $f$ .
Fix $0<\rho\leq 1/2$ . The ordinary $L^{2}$ energy does not by itself give
the required one-step contraction. Indeed, since $\mathsf{U}$ is unitary and
 $\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}$ is a contraction,
 $\displaystyle\|\mathsf{K}f\|^{2}-\|f\|^{2}$ 
 $\displaystyle=(1-\rho)^{2}\,\|\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}f_{\perp}\|^{2}-\|f_{\perp}\|^{2}$ 
 $\displaystyle\leq-\rho\,(2-\rho)\,\|f_{\perp}\|^{2}\leq-\rho\,\|f_{\perp}\|^{2}\,.$ 
(B.18)
This estimate controls $f_{\perp}$ but gives nothing when
 $f=f_{\mathsf{P}}$ .
Reflection moves part of $f_{\mathsf{P}}$ into the
microscopic subspace, but this transfer is not captured by the $L^{2}$ norm,
which is a standard difficulty in hypocoercivity. The modified $L^{2}$ 
method addresses this difficulty by adding a corrector to the Lyapunov
function. The corrector records the transfer from $f_{\mathsf{P}}$ to
 $f_{\perp}$ , allowing the contraction in
(B.18) to also control $f_{\mathsf{P}}$ .
This idea goes back to Dolbeault, Mouhot, and Schmeiser [14]; here we
follow the refinement of Fan, Li, and
Lu [16].
To construct the corrector, we first isolate the part of $f_{\perp}$ that
couples to $f_{\mathsf{P}}$ under reflection. Define
 $f_{\mathsf{V}}\coloneqq\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}f_{\perp}\,.$ 
By (B.16),
 $\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}f_{\perp}=\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{V}}$ , while the component of
 $f_{\perp}$ orthogonal to $\operatorname{ran}(\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}})$ is annihilated by
 $\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}$ . Since $\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}$ is an isometry,
 $\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{V}}=\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}f_{\perp}$ is the orthogonal
projection of $f_{\perp}$ onto $\operatorname{ran}(\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}})$ , and hence
 $\|f_{\mathsf{V}}\|\leq\|f_{\perp}\|$ . We work with $f_{\mathsf{V}}$ , rather
than with this projection itself, because $f_{\mathsf{V}}$ and
 $f_{\mathsf{P}}$ both belong to $\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0}$ and can therefore be
paired in the corrector.
With this notation, let $\omega>0$ be a weight to be chosen below and
define the modified Lyapunov function
 $\mathscr{L}_{\omega}(f)\coloneqq\|f\|^{2}+\omega\mathscr{C}(f_{\mathsf{P}},f_{\mathsf{V}})\,,$ 
(B.19)
where the corrector is
 $\mathscr{C}(f_{\mathsf{P}},f_{\mathsf{V}})\coloneqq\frac{1}{2}\,\bigl(\|f_{\mathsf{P}}\|^{2}-\|f_{\mathsf{V}}\|^{2}\bigr)-\bigl\langle\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}f_{\mathsf{P}},f_{\mathsf{V}}\bigr\rangle\,.$ 
(B.20)
Here $\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}$ is applied only on $\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0}$ , where
 $\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}\succeq\gamma_{\rm gap}I$ , so the corrector is well-defined.
To motivate this choice, recall from (B.7) that
 $\mathsf{K}$ acts in two stages: it first maps
 $f_{\mathsf{P}}+f_{\perp}\longmapsto f_{\mathsf{P}}+(1-\rho)\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}f_{\perp}$ 
and then applies $\mathsf{U}$ . For the moment, set $\rho=0$ and replace
 $\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}$ by $-I_{\mathfrak{H}_{\perp}}$ . The error
 $I+\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}$ introduced by this replacement is controlled by
Proposition B.2. Denote the resulting idealized operator
by
 $\mathsf{K}_{\rm id}\coloneqq\mathsf{U}(\mathsf{P}-\mathsf{P}^{\perp})\,.$ 
Set $g\coloneqq\mathsf{K}_{\rm id}f$ , and define the corresponding components by
 $g_{\mathsf{P}}\coloneqq\mathsf{P}g\,,\hskip 20.00003ptg_{\mathsf{V}}\coloneqq\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\mathsf{P}^{\perp}g\,.$ 
In terms of these components, $\mathsf{K}_{\rm id}$ is represented by the
operator-valued rotation matrix
 $\begin{pmatrix}g_{\mathsf{P}}\\
g_{\mathsf{V}}\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}&-\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}\\
\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}&\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}f_{\mathsf{P}}\\
f_{\mathsf{V}}\end{pmatrix}\,.$ 
Indeed, the first component follows immediately from the block representation
of $\mathsf{U}$ and the polar decomposition
(B.16):
 $\displaystyle g_{\mathsf{P}}$ 
 $\displaystyle=\mathsf{P}\mathsf{U}(f_{\mathsf{P}}-f_{\perp})=\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{P}}-\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}f_{\perp}=\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{P}}-\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}f_{\perp}=\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{P}}-\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{V}}\,.$ 
For the second component, substitute
 $\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}=\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}$ into
 $\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\mathsf{U}_{\perp\perp}=-\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}$ from
(B.5). Since $\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}$ commutes with
 $\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}$ , this gives
 $\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\mathsf{U}_{\perp\perp}=-\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\,.$ 
The ranges of $\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\mathsf{U}_{\perp\perp}$ and
 $\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}$ lie in $\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0}$ , where
 $\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}$ is bounded. Applying $\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}$ to the
displayed identity therefore gives
 $\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\mathsf{U}_{\perp\perp}=-\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}$ . Hence
 $\displaystyle g_{\mathsf{V}}$ 
 $\displaystyle=\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\mathsf{P}^{\perp}\mathsf{U}(f_{\mathsf{P}}-f_{\perp})=\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}(\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{P}}-\mathsf{U}_{\perp\perp}f_{\perp})=\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{P}}+\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}f_{\perp}=\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{P}}+\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{V}}\,.$ 
This proves the claimed matrix formula. Since
 $\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}^{2}+\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{2}=I$ , the block matrix acts as a rotation on
 $\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0}\oplus\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0}$ . Under this rotation,
