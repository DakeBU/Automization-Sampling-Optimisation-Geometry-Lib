    Wasserstein distance transfers a Lipschitz observable's second moment

    AutoSamplingTheory.TechnicalLemmas.Measure.WassersteinLipschitzMoment.of_wassersteinDistance_sq_le · theorem · Teaching coverage

    Read this first

    Mathematical statement

    The observable remains square-integrable under μ, and its μ second moment is bounded by twice its reference second moment plus 2b²r².

    \[W_2^2(\mu,\nu)\le r^2\Longrightarrow \int\|g\|^2d\mu\le 2\int\|g\|^2d\nu+2b^2r^2.\]

    Proof roadmap

    1. 1Choose an almost-optimal coupling

      For every s>r², the definition of transport cost supplies a coupling whose quadratic cost is below s. Optimal-plan existence is unnecessary.

    2. 2Dominate the transported observable

      Lipschitz continuity and the quadratic two-term inequality control the first-coordinate observable by an integrable reference term plus coupling displacement.

    3. 3Use the exact marginals

      The coordinate projections of γ preserve μ and ν, so the two coupling integrals are exactly the desired marginal moments.

    4. 4Remove the excess cost

      The bound holds for every s>r². Applying it at a controlled positive excess and letting that excess be arbitrary gives the closed r² bound.

    Full assumptions and notation

    All objects and hypotheses

    • E is a second-countable Borel normed additive group and F is a normed additive group.
    • g:E→F is b-Lipschitz and its squared norm is integrable under the reference law ν.
    • The squared extended-real Wasserstein distance from μ to ν is at most ofReal(r²).

    Mathematical proof

    1. Choose an almost-optimal coupling

    For every s>r², the definition of transport cost supplies a coupling whose quadratic cost is below s. Optimal-plan existence is unnecessary.

    \[\exists\gamma\in\Gamma(\mu,\nu):\int\|x-y\|^2d\gamma\le s.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    wassersteinDistance_sq and exists_isCoupling_lintegral_lt_of_transportCost_lt.

    2. Dominate the transported observable

    Lipschitz continuity and the quadratic two-term inequality control the first-coordinate observable by an integrable reference term plus coupling displacement.

    \[\|g(x)\|^2\le2\|g(y)\|^2+2b^2\|x-y\|^2.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    The private pointwise bound and Integrable.mono' establish integrability before integral comparison.

    3. Use the exact marginals

    The coordinate projections of γ preserve μ and ν, so the two coupling integrals are exactly the desired marginal moments.

    \[(\mathrm{fst})_\#\gamma=\mu,\quad(\mathrm{snd})_\#\gamma=\nu.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    CouplingQuadraticIntegrability.measurePreserving_fst/snd_of_isCoupling.

    4. Remove the excess cost

    The bound holds for every s>r². Applying it at a controlled positive excess and letting that excess be arbitrary gives the closed r² bound.

    \[\forall\varepsilon>0,\ I_\mu\le2I_\nu+2b^2r^2+\varepsilon.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    le_of_forall_pos_le_add and real algebra close the infimum argument.

    theorem of_wassersteinDistance_sq_le {mu nu : Measure E}
    {g : E → F} {b : ℝ≥0} {r : ℝ} (hg : LipschitzWith b g)
    (hi : Integrable (fun x => ‖g x‖ ^ 2) nu)
    (hw : WassersteinSpace.wassersteinDistance mu nu ^ 2 ≤ ENNReal.ofReal (r ^ 2)) :
    Integrable (fun x => ‖g x‖ ^ 2) mu ∧
      (∫ x, ‖g x‖ ^ 2 ∂mu) ≤
        2 * (∫ x, ‖g x‖ ^ 2 ∂nu) + 2 * (b : ℝ) ^ 2 * r ^ 2 := by
  have hall (s : ℝ) (hs : r ^ 2 < s) :
      Integrable (fun x => ‖g x‖ ^ 2) mu ∧
        (∫ x, ‖g x‖ ^ 2 ∂mu) ≤
          2 * (∫ x, ‖g x‖ ^ 2 ∂nu) + 2 * (b : ℝ) ^ 2 * s := by
    have hs0 : 0 < s := (sq_nonneg r).trans_lt hs
    have hcost : Transport.transportCost
        (WassersteinSpace.quadraticCost (E := E)) mu nu < ENNReal.ofReal s := by
      rw [← WassersteinSpace.wassersteinDistance_sq]
      exact hw.trans_lt ((ENNReal.ofReal_lt_ofReal_iff hs0).2 hs)
    obtain ⟨gamma, hgamma, hgammaCost⟩ :=
      Transport.exists_isCoupling_lintegral_lt_of_transportCost_lt
        (WassersteinSpace.quadraticCost (E := E)) mu nu hcost
    exact of_isCoupling hg hi hgamma hs0.le hgammaCost.le
  refine ⟨(hall (r ^ 2 + 1) (by linarith)).1, ?_⟩
  apply le_of_forall_pos_le_add
  intro epsilon hepsilon
  have hden : 0 < 2 * (b : ℝ) ^ 2 + 1 := by positivity
  have hdelta : 0 < epsilon / (2 * (b : ℝ) ^ 2 + 1) := div_pos hepsilon hden
  have hbound := (hall (r ^ 2 + epsilon / (2 * (b : ℝ) ^ 2 + 1)) (by linarith)).2
  have heq := div_mul_cancel₀ epsilon hden.ne'
  nlinarith
