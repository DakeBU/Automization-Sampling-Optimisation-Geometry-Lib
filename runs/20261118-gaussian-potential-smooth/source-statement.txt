Let E be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, and P a probability on E times R whose first marginal is the standard Gaussian. Let p be any real polynomial and assume only that the label is square integrable. For every probability rho on the unit sphere, V_rho(w)=2 E_P[(integral p(inner(v,X)) d rho(v)-Y) p(inner(w,X))] is smooth on all of E. For each real radius R and derivative order n, a finite nonnegative bound C exists such that the operator norm of D^n V_rho(w) is at most C for every sphere probability rho and every ambient w of norm at most R. The constant is uniform in rho and w, but may depend on the data distribution, polynomial, dimension, radius and derivative order. Order zero is included.
