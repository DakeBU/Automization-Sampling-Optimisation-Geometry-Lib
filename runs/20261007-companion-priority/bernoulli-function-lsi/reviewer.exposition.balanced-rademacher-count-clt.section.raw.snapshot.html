Proof roadmap

  1. 1Build the actual laws, including zero

    The finite carrier has positive cardinal2^N. Scaling counting measure by its reciprocal gives mass1. All finite functions are measurable and Bochner integrable; each normalized sum is pushed forward to a genuine probability law. AtN0 the empty sum is0, so its map law is the unit atom at0, without any fallback.

  2. 2Produce the single-coordinate moments

    Use the actual balanced Bool count law and sigma(true)=1, sigma(false)=-1. The finite integral is the two-value average; hence the coordinate has mean0 and secondmoment1. These moments and measurable sign map are proof ingredients, never target premises.

  3. 3Prove the finite characteristic-function product

    For every configuration, exp(i t sum sigma_j) is the product of exp(i t sigma_j). Sum over all configurations using Fintype.sum_pow; multiplying by2^-N gives the Nth power of the actual single-coordinate characteristic function. This is an authored count-law adapter, not an assumed independence or copied infinite-iid construction.

  4. 4Apply the pinned scalar CLT to the derived moments

    Mathlib characteristic-function CLT applies to this actual measurable balanced coordinate with its internally derived mean0 and secondmoment1. It yields convergence of the same Nth-power expression to exp(-t^2/2), the exact characteristic function of mean0 variance1 Gaussian.

  5. 5Return weak convergence and the actual successor family

    Levy convergence upgrades the pointwise characteristic-function limit of genuine probability measures to weak convergence. Composition with the cofinal n->n+1 sequence gives the sealed positive-index limit. The same family supplies every literal map equality and its zero atom.
