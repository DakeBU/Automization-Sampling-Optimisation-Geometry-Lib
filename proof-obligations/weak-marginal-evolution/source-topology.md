# Proof ingredients for weak marginal evolution

| Source ingredient | Proof dependency |
| --- | --- |
| Almost-sure convergence along continuous paths | Mathlib convergence in distribution from almost-sure convergence |
| Average a measurable test over the actual state law | Mathlib pushforward integral formula |
| Expectation of the signed generator integral | Mathlib product integrability and the signed interval Fubini theorem |
| Continuity of a jointly continuous field integrated against a weakly continuous law | Samplinglib compact weak probability integral |
| Differentiate the local continuous generator primitive | Mathlib interval fundamental theorem of calculus |
| Coefficient measurability on filtration prefixes | Mathlib strong progressiveness and Borel composition |
| Integrability of time sections and sample-time representative | Samplinglib finite horizon time measure and Mathlib finite-measure bounded integrability |
| Signed law balance and absolute continuity | Centered increment theorem, difference of primitive values and a local Lipschitz bound |
| Almost-everywhere derivative at positive real time | Mathlib Lebesgue differentiation and equality with the primitive on a neighborhood of each positive time |

The exact links, assumptions and readable formula arguments appear in the
individual publication items and declaration lessons. Continuous-time sampling
and Riemannian diffusion are intended applications.
