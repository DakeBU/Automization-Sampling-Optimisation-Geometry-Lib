Proof roadmap

  1. 1Keep one actual posterior and its proved density domains

    Reuse the true posterior square-root producer once. It constructs the same measurable p, residual rho, true RGO R and affine standardized r used throughout this theorem. Its positive actual Z, actual probability and qlogq L1 are conclusions, not source inputs.

  2. 2Derive stationary uniqueness from the actual source curvature

    The Hessian lower bound produces strong convexity and hence nonnegative gradient monotonicity. Subtract two stationary equations and take inner product with z-p. Positive eta forces norm(z-p)=0. This derived uniqueness permits future identification of independently constructed stationary witnesses; no witness equality is assumed here.

  3. 3Transfer the genuine entropy domain to the actual tilted law

    Positivity of the already proved actual integral Z rules out a nonintegrable exponential. qlogq L1 gives exp(-rho)logq L1 by multiplying with Z, and the actual tilted integrability equivalence gives logq L1r. Since logq=-rho-logZ everywhere and r is probability, rho is L1r as well.

  4. 4Use the existing canonical KL producer and AE log-ratio adapter

    Apply the shared TiltedKL theorem to the actual Gaussian base with exponents -rho and0; the zero tilt is gamma. The separately compiled log-ratio adapter identifies canonical llr with logq only r-a.e. This transfers L1 and integrals, and proves extended KL is not infinity. It supplies no pointwise RN derivative.

  5. 5Integrate both exact entropy formulas under the same laws

    The actual tilted integral rewrites integral_r logq to integral_gamma qlogq. Its everywhere explicit logarithm and the proved rho/constant L1 domains give -integral_r rho-logZ. The canonical finite KL real value equals both. No original V partition, Gaussian-volume constant or affine Jacobian is added to this relative Gaussian formula.
