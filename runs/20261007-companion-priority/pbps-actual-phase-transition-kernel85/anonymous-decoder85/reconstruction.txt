Fix an arbitrary type E with a normed additive commutative group structure, a real inner-product-space structure, finite dimension over R, and a measurable-space structure that is its Borel structure. Dimension zero is permitted. Fix a function V:E->R, nonnegative real parameters alpha,beta, and a real parameter eta. All these data are arbitrary subject to exactly the following six supplied hypotheses:
(1) 0 < alpha, where alpha is coerced to R.
(2) alpha <= beta in the nonnegative reals.
(3) V is twice continuously Frechet differentiable over R.
(4) For every x,v in E, both
    alpha * ||v||^2 <= (D(DV)(x)[v])[v]
    and (D(DV)(x)[v])[v] <= beta * ||v||^2.
    Here the middle expression is the second Frechet derivative, first applied to v as a direction and then to v as the argument of the resulting linear functional.
(5) 0 < eta.
(6) beta * eta <= 1, with beta coerced to R.
No starting point, trajectory, measurable selection, or kernel is an assumed input.
