    Mathematical statement

    The incoming phase energy is integrable and bounded by 2(Mx+Mp)+24r^2.

    \[\mathbb E_\mu[\|X-x_\star\|^2+\|P\|^2]\le2(M_X+M_P)+24r^2.\]

    Full assumptions and notation

    All objects and hypotheses

    • kappa>=1.
    • Reference position and momentum squares are integrable and bounded by Mx,Mp.
    • The incoming law has M-kappa squared transport radius at most r^2.

    Mathematical proof

    1. Convert the radius

    The uniform metric comparison gives the ordinary radius sqrt(6)|r|.

    \[W_2^2\le6r^2=(\sqrt6\,r)^2.\]

    2. Reuse the compiled coordinate theorem

    The earlier theorem proves both integrability and the ordinary-Wasserstein moment estimate.

    \[I\le2(M_X+M_P)+4(\sqrt6r)^2.\]

    PhaseStateMoment.phase_state_second_moment_of_wasserstein.

    3. Normalize the constant

    Expand the square to obtain the explicit final factor.

    \[4(\sqrt6r)^2=24r^2.\]

      ENNReal.ofReal 6 * phaseWassersteinSq kappa nu reference ≤

        rw [← ENNReal.ofReal_mul (by norm_num : (0 : ℝ) ≤ 6)]
        congr 1
        rw [mul_pow, hsqrt]

    nu reference xstar hX hP hXbound hPbound hordinary
