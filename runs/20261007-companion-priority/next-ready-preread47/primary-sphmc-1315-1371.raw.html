Moreover,

The gradient of ρy\rho_{y} is η\eta-Lipschitz, so (4.6) gives (4.2). Since g^η​(y,⋅)\widehat{g}_{\eta}(y;\cdot) is η\sqrt{\eta}-Lipschitz, the standard Gaussian concentration inequality gives (4.3), and the same observation gives (4.5). Finally, y↦xy+y\mapsto x_{y}^{+} is non-expansive and ∇V\nabla V is 11-Lipschitz, proving (4.4). ∎
