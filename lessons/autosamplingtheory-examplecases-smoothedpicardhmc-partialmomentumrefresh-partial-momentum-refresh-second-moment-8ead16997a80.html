    The actual OU half-refresh and its corrected moment budget

    AutoSamplingTheory.ExampleCases.SmoothedPicardHMC.PartialMomentumRefresh.partial_momentum_refresh_second_moment · theorem · Teaching coverage

    Read this first

    Mathematical statement

    Set a=exp(−h/2), sigma=sqrt(1−exp(−h)), X0=X and P0=a Pinit+sigma Z on the product law of the incoming state and a fresh standard Gaussian. Then X0 and P0 are measurable, their combined squared distance is integrable, and its expectation satisfies the exact identity and corrected budget below.

    \[\mathbb E[\|X_0-x_*\|^2+\|P_0\|^2]=\mathbb E[\|X-x_*\|^2+e^{-h}\|P_{\rm init}\|^2]+(1-e^{-h})d\le M+(1-e^{-h})d.\]

    Proof roadmap

    1. 1Realize the fresh draw by a product law

      A sample is a pair ((X,Pinit),Z) distributed according to nu⊗gamma_d. The first coordinate is the incoming phase state and the second is a genuinely fresh standard Gaussian. Coordinate projections and continuous linear combinations make X0 and P0 measurable.

    2. 2Establish every integrability premise

      Nonnegativity lets us extract the position and momentum square integrability from the combined incoming energy. L2 then gives integrability of Pinit itself. The shared ASTIS theorem gives ‖Z‖² in L1, while Mathlib's Gaussian integrability API separately gives Z in L1; together these make the mixed inner product integrable on the product measure.

    3. 3Use actual independence to kill the cross term

      For every fixed incoming momentum, the Gaussian mean is zero. Fubini is legal because the mixed inner product was proved integrable, hence its expectation under the product law vanishes without a centering assumption on Pinit.

    4. 4Expand the refreshed square

      The coefficients satisfy a²=e^(−h) and sigma²=1−e^(−h). Expanding the Hilbert norm, integrating the four integrable terms, using the zero cross term and E‖Z‖²=d gives the exact identity.

    5. 5Derive the consumer-ready bound

      Because h≥0, 0<e^(−h)≤1. Replacing the damped momentum term by the incoming momentum square and applying the input budget yields M+(1−e^(−h))d. The extra dimension term must remain visible until the later paper-parameter absorption step.

    Full assumptions and notation

    All objects and hypotheses

    • The incoming phase-state law nu on E×E is a probability measure, and the combined square ‖X−x*‖²+‖Pinit‖² is integrable with expectation at most M.
    • E is a complete finite-dimensional real inner-product Borel space and h≥0.
    • Freshness of Z~N(0,I) is represented by the actual product measure nu⊗stdGaussian(E), rather than by assuming the mixed term has expectation zero.

    Mathematical proof

    1. Realize the fresh draw by a product law

    A sample is a pair ((X,Pinit),Z) distributed according to nu⊗gamma_d. The first coordinate is the incoming phase state and the second is a genuinely fresh standard Gaussian. Coordinate projections and continuous linear combinations make X0 and P0 measurable.

    \[\mu=\nu\otimes\gamma_d,\qquad P_0=e^{-h/2}P_{\rm init}+\sqrt{1-e^{-h}}\,Z.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    The public X and P0 lets use product projections; the final fun_prop goals prove their measurability.

    2. Establish every integrability premise

    Nonnegativity lets us extract the position and momentum square integrability from the combined incoming energy. L2 then gives integrability of Pinit itself. The shared ASTIS theorem gives ‖Z‖² in L1, while Mathlib's Gaussian integrability API separately gives Z in L1; together these make the mixed inner product integrable on the product measure.

    \[\|X-x_*\|^2,\|P_{\rm init}\|^2\in L^1(\nu),\quad P_{\rm init}\in L^1(\nu),\quad Z,\|Z\|^2\in L^1(\gamma_d).\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    hposI, hmomI, hmom1, hgaussI, hz1 and Integrable.op_fst_snd justify Fubini and all subsequent integral algebra.

    3. Use actual independence to kill the cross term

    For every fixed incoming momentum, the Gaussian mean is zero. Fubini is legal because the mixed inner product was proved integrable, hence its expectation under the product law vanishes without a centering assumption on Pinit.

    \[\mathbb E_{\nu\otimes\gamma_d}\langle P_{\rm init},Z\rangle=\mathbb E_\nu\langle P_{\rm init},\mathbb E_{\gamma_d}Z\rangle=0.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    integral_strongDual_stdGaussian and integral_prod prove hcross0.

    4. Expand the refreshed square

    The coefficients satisfy a²=e^(−h) and sigma²=1−e^(−h). Expanding the Hilbert norm, integrating the four integrable terms, using the zero cross term and E‖Z‖²=d gives the exact identity.

    \[\|P_0\|^2=e^{-h}\|P_{\rm init}\|^2+2e^{-h/2}\sqrt{1-e^{-h}}\langle P_{\rm init},Z\rangle+(1-e^{-h})\|Z\|^2.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    ha2, hs2, hexpand, hpieces and heq mirror this algebra and product integration.

    5. Derive the consumer-ready bound

    Because h≥0, 0<e^(−h)≤1. Replacing the damped momentum term by the incoming momentum square and applying the input budget yields M+(1−e^(−h))d. The extra dimension term must remain visible until the later paper-parameter absorption step.

    \[\mathbb E[\|X-x_*\|^2+e^{-h}\|P_{\rm init}\|^2]\le M.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    hexp, hweighted and heq prove the final inequality. The focused test passes this result to picard_center_gradient_moment.

    theorem partial_momentum_refresh_second_moment
    (ν : Measure (E × E)) [IsProbabilityMeasure ν]
    (xstar : E) {h M : ℝ} (hh : 0 ≤ h)
    (hstateI : Integrable (fun s : E × E => ‖s.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 + ‖s.2‖ ^ 2) ν)
    (hstate : (∫ s : E × E, ‖s.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 + ‖s.2‖ ^ 2 ∂ν) ≤ M) :
    let a := Real.exp (-h / 2)
    let sigma := Real.sqrt (1 - Real.exp (-h))
    let μ := ν.prod (stdGaussian E)
    let X : (E × E) × E → E := fun w => w.1.1
    let P0 : (E × E) × E → E := fun w => a • w.1.2 + sigma • w.2
    Measurable X ∧ Measurable P0 ∧
      Integrable (fun w => ‖X w - xstar‖ ^ 2 + ‖P0 w‖ ^ 2) μ ∧
      (∫ w, ‖X w - xstar‖ ^ 2 + ‖P0 w‖ ^ 2 ∂μ) =
        (∫ s : E × E, ‖s.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 + Real.exp (-h) * ‖s.2‖ ^ 2 ∂ν) +
          (1 - Real.exp (-h)) * (Module.finrank ℝ E : ℝ) ∧
      (∫ w, ‖X w - xstar‖ ^ 2 + ‖P0 w‖ ^ 2 ∂μ) ≤
        M + (1 - Real.exp (-h)) * (Module.finrank ℝ E : ℝ)

    theorem partial_momentum_refresh_second_moment
    (ν : Measure (E × E)) [IsProbabilityMeasure ν]
    (xstar : E) {h M : ℝ} (hh : 0 ≤ h)
    (hstateI : Integrable (fun s : E × E => ‖s.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 + ‖s.2‖ ^ 2) ν)
    (hstate : (∫ s : E × E, ‖s.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 + ‖s.2‖ ^ 2 ∂ν) ≤ M) :
    let a := Real.exp (-h / 2)
    let sigma := Real.sqrt (1 - Real.exp (-h))
    let μ := ν.prod (stdGaussian E)
    let X : (E × E) × E → E := fun w => w.1.1
    let P0 : (E × E) × E → E := fun w => a • w.1.2 + sigma • w.2
    Measurable X ∧ Measurable P0 ∧
      Integrable (fun w => ‖X w - xstar‖ ^ 2 + ‖P0 w‖ ^ 2) μ ∧
      (∫ w, ‖X w - xstar‖ ^ 2 + ‖P0 w‖ ^ 2 ∂μ) =
        (∫ s : E × E, ‖s.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 + Real.exp (-h) * ‖s.2‖ ^ 2 ∂ν) +
          (1 - Real.exp (-h)) * (Module.finrank ℝ E : ℝ) ∧
      (∫ w, ‖X w - xstar‖ ^ 2 + ‖P0 w‖ ^ 2 ∂μ) ≤
        M + (1 - Real.exp (-h)) * (Module.finrank ℝ E : ℝ) := by
  classical
  dsimp only
  have hexp : Real.exp (-h) ≤ 1 := Real.exp_le_one_iff.mpr (by linarith)
  have hvar : 0 ≤ 1 - Real.exp (-h) := sub_nonneg.mpr hexp
  have ha2 : Real.exp (-h / 2) ^ 2 = Real.exp (-h) := by
    rw [pow_two, ← Real.exp_add]
    congr 1
    ring
  have hs2 : Real.sqrt (1 - Real.exp (-h)) ^ 2 = 1 - Real.exp (-h) :=
    Real.sq_sqrt hvar
  have hposI : Integrable (fun s : E × E => ‖s.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2) ν := by
    apply hstateI.mono' (by fun_prop)
    filter_upwards with s
    rw [Real.norm_eq_abs, abs_of_nonneg (sq_nonneg _)]
    exact le_add_of_nonneg_right (sq_nonneg _)
  have hmomI : Integrable (fun s : E × E => ‖s.2‖ ^ 2) ν := by
    apply hstateI.mono' (by fun_prop)
    filter_upwards with s
    rw [Real.norm_eq_abs, abs_of_nonneg (sq_nonneg _)]
    exact le_add_of_nonneg_left (sq_nonneg _)
  have hmom1 : Integrable (fun s : E × E => s.2) ν :=
    MemLp.integrable (by norm_num : 1 ≤ (2 : ℝ≥0∞))
      ((memLp_two_iff_integrable_sq_norm (by fun_prop)).2 hmomI)
  obtain ⟨hgaussI, hgauss⟩ :=
    integrable_norm_sq_and_integral_stdGaussian (E := E)
  have hz1 : Integrable (fun z : E => z) (stdGaussian E) := IsGaussian.integrable_id
  have hcross : Integrable
      (fun w : (E × E) × E => inner ℝ w.1.2 w.2) (ν.prod (stdGaussian E)) :=
    hmom1.op_fst_snd (by fun_prop)
      ⟨1, by intro x y; simpa using norm_inner_le_norm (𝕜 := ℝ) x y⟩ hz1
  have hcross0 :
      (∫ w : (E × E) × E, inner ℝ w.1.2 w.2 ∂ν.prod (stdGaussian E)) = 0 := by
    rw [integral_prod _ hcross]
    have hz (s : E × E) :
        (∫ z : E, inner ℝ s.2 z ∂stdGaussian E) = 0 :=
      integral_strongDual_stdGaussian (innerSL ℝ s.2)
    simp only [hz, integral_zero]
  have hposP := hposI.comp_fst (stdGaussian E)
  have hmomP := hmomI.comp_fst (stdGaussian E)
  have hgaussP := hgaussI.comp_snd ν
  have hcrossC : Integrable (fun w : (E × E) × E =>
      (2 * Real.exp (-h / 2) * Real.sqrt (1 - Real.exp (-h))) *
        inner ℝ w.1.2 w.2) (ν.prod (stdGaussian E)) := hcross.const_mul _
  have hbase : Integrable (fun w : (E × E) × E =>
      ‖w.1.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 + Real.exp (-h) * ‖w.1.2‖ ^ 2)
      (ν.prod (stdGaussian E)) := hposP.add (hmomP.const_mul _)
  have hbaseCross : Integrable (fun w : (E × E) × E =>
      ‖w.1.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 + Real.exp (-h) * ‖w.1.2‖ ^ 2 +
        (2 * Real.exp (-h / 2) * Real.sqrt (1 - Real.exp (-h))) *
          inner ℝ w.1.2 w.2) (ν.prod (stdGaussian E)) := hbase.add hcrossC
  have hnoiseC : Integrable (fun w : (E × E) × E =>
      (1 - Real.exp (-h)) * ‖w.2‖ ^ 2) (ν.prod (stdGaussian E)) :=
    hgaussP.const_mul _
  have hpieces : Integrable (fun w : (E × E) × E =>
      ‖w.1.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 + Real.exp (-h) * ‖w.1.2‖ ^ 2 +
        (2 * Real.exp (-h / 2) * Real.sqrt (1 - Real.exp (-h))) *
          inner ℝ w.1.2 w.2 +
        (1 - Real.exp (-h)) * ‖w.2‖ ^ 2) (ν.prod (stdGaussian E)) :=
    hbaseCross.add hnoiseC
  have hexpand (w : (E × E) × E) :
      ‖w.1.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 +
          ‖Real.exp (-h / 2) • w.1.2 +
            Real.sqrt (1 - Real.exp (-h)) • w.2‖ ^ 2 =
        ‖w.1.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 + Real.exp (-h) * ‖w.1.2‖ ^ 2 +
          (2 * Real.exp (-h / 2) * Real.sqrt (1 - Real.exp (-h))) *
            inner ℝ w.1.2 w.2 +
          (1 - Real.exp (-h)) * ‖w.2‖ ^ 2 := by
    rw [norm_add_sq_real, norm_smul, norm_smul, real_inner_smul_left,
      real_inner_smul_right]
    rw [Real.norm_eq_abs, Real.norm_eq_abs, abs_of_pos (Real.exp_pos _),
      abs_of_nonneg (Real.sqrt_nonneg _)]
    simp only [mul_pow, ha2, hs2]
    ring
  have houtI : Integrable (fun w : (E × E) × E =>
      ‖w.1.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 +
        ‖Real.exp (-h / 2) • w.1.2 +
          Real.sqrt (1 - Real.exp (-h)) • w.2‖ ^ 2)
      (ν.prod (stdGaussian E)) := by
    simpa only [hexpand] using hpieces
  have heq :
      (∫ w : (E × E) × E,
          ‖w.1.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 +
            ‖Real.exp (-h / 2) • w.1.2 +
              Real.sqrt (1 - Real.exp (-h)) • w.2‖ ^ 2
          ∂ν.prod (stdGaussian E)) =
        (∫ s : E × E,
          ‖s.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 + Real.exp (-h) * ‖s.2‖ ^ 2 ∂ν) +
          (1 - Real.exp (-h)) * (Module.finrank ℝ E : ℝ) := by
    simp_rw [hexpand]
    rw [integral_add hbaseCross hnoiseC,
      integral_add hbase hcrossC,
      integral_const_mul, hcross0, mul_zero, add_zero,
      integral_const_mul, integral_prod _ hgaussP]
    simp only [hgauss, integral_const]
    rw [integral_prod _ hbase]
    simp only [integral_const]
    simp
  have hweighted :
      (∫ s : E × E,
          ‖s.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 + Real.exp (-h) * ‖s.2‖ ^ 2 ∂ν) ≤ M := by
    calc
      _ ≤ ∫ s : E × E, ‖s.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 + ‖s.2‖ ^ 2 ∂ν := by
        apply integral_mono (hposI.add (hmomI.const_mul _)) hstateI
        intro s
        change ‖s.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 + Real.exp (-h) * ‖s.2‖ ^ 2 ≤
          ‖s.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2 + ‖s.2‖ ^ 2
        have hp : Real.exp (-h) * ‖s.2‖ ^ 2 ≤ ‖s.2‖ ^ 2 := by
          simpa only [one_mul] using
            mul_le_mul_of_nonneg_right hexp (sq_nonneg ‖s.2‖)
        exact add_le_add_right hp (‖s.1 - xstar‖ ^ 2)
      _ ≤ M := hstate
  refine ⟨by fun_prop, by fun_prop, houtI, heq, ?_⟩
  rw [heq]
  linarith
