Lemma C.1 (Derivative formula for 𝖴𝖯𝖯\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}).

The extension to H1​(πηY)H^{1}(\pi_{\eta}^{Y}) is a standard approximation argument once we check that the covariance term is continuous in L2L^{2}. By (2.11), the conditional law of XX given Y+=yY_{+}=y has Poincaré constant at most η/(1+α​η)≤η\eta/(1+\alpha\eta)\leq\eta. Hence, for every unit vector a∈ℝda\in\mathbb{R}^{d},

Conditional Cauchy–Schwarz and (B.9) therefore imply

‖Cov⁡(f⁡(Y−),∇V​(X)∣Y+)‖L2≤β​η​‖𝖴⟂𝖯​f‖,\bigl\|\mathrm{Cov}(f(Y_{-}),\nabla V(X)\mid Y_{+})\bigr\|_{L^{2}}\leq\beta\sqrt{\eta}\,\|\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}f\|\,,

and the same bound applies to differences. Conditional expectation is an L2L^{2} contraction, and (B.13) controls the left side. Thus every term in (C.8) is continuous under H1H^{1} approximation.
