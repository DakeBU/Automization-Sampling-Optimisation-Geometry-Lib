notation

c

Phi

S

rate

Lambda

Rec
Rec=(ℝ≥0×(E×E))⊕Unit. A live record is inl(a,z) with a nonnegative time and state; the right summand is a single cemetery record.

Hist
Hist(n) is the type of functions Fin(n+1)→Rec, hence exactly n+1 records. There is no chronological or liveness restriction on an arbitrary Hist(n).

Delta
Δ(n,T) is the subset of real vectors Fin n→ℝ for which the prepended vector (0,t₁,…,tₙ) is strictly increasing, and every tᵢ<T. Thus it is precisely 0<t₁<⋯<tₙ<T when n>0.

Prefix

zAt

qReal

liveTime

d

weight

Psi

U
