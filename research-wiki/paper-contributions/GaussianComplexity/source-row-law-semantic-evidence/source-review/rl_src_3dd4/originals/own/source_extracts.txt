A3.SS2.p10 (lines 3532-3556)
Truncation and its bias. Set $L=2r+3\log(1000d)$ and $R_{i}=\|\gamma_{i}\|^{2}$. Since $\mathbb{E}e^{R_{i}/3}=3^{r/2}$, Markov’s inequality gives, for $x\geq 0$, $\mathbb{P}(R_{i}>2r+3x)\leq\exp\!\left(\frac{r}{2}\log 3-\frac{2r}{3}-x\right)\leq e^{-x},$ where the last inequality uses $(\log 3)/2<2/3$. Consequently, the event $\mathcal{T}=\{R_{i}\leq L\text{ for every }i\}$ has probability at least $1-d/(1000d)=0.999$.
