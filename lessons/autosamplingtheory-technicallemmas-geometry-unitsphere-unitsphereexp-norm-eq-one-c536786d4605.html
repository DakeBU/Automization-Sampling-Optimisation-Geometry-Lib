    Tangent exponential steps remain on the unit sphere

    AutoSamplingTheory.TechnicalLemmas.Geometry.UnitSphere.unitSphereExp_norm_eq_one · theorem · Teaching coverage

    Read this first

    Mathematical statement

    A unit base point and an orthogonal increment produce an exponential-map value with exactly unit norm.

    \[\|w\|=1,\ \langle w,z\rangle=0\Longrightarrow\|\operatorname{Exp}_w(z)\|=1\]

    Proof roadmap

    1. 1Handle zero increment

      At z=0, the exponential formula is w.

    2. 2Use orthogonality

      The cosine and sinc components have zero cross inner product.

    3. 3Apply Pythagoras and trigonometry

      The two squared norms are cos²r and sin²r.

    4. 4Use nonnegativity of norm

      A nonnegative real with square one equals one.

    Full assumptions and notation

    All objects and hypotheses

    • E is a real inner-product space.
    • ‖w‖=1.
    • ⟪w,z⟫=0.
    • z=0 is allowed.

    Mathematical proof

    1. Handle zero increment

    At z=0, the exponential formula is w.

    \[z=0\Rightarrow\operatorname{Exp}_w(z)=w\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    Simplification uses the unit-norm premise.

    2. Use orthogonality

    The cosine and sinc components have zero cross inner product.

    \[\langle\cos(r)w,\operatorname{sinc}(r)z\rangle=0\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    Inner-product scalar rules reduce this to ⟪w,z⟫=0.

    3. Apply Pythagoras and trigonometry

    The two squared norms are cos²r and sin²r.

    \[\|\operatorname{Exp}_w(z)\|^2=\cos^2r+\sin^2r=1\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    norm_add_sq_real, Real.sinc_of_ne_zero, and Real.sin_sq_add_cos_sq prove the identity.

    4. Use nonnegativity of norm

    A nonnegative real with square one equals one.

    \[x\ge0,\ x^2=1\Rightarrow x=1\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    Nonlinear arithmetic closes the final scalar step.

    theorem unitSphereExp_norm_eq_one
    {w z : E}
    (hw : ‖w‖ = 1)
    (htan : ⟪w, z⟫_ℝ = 0) :
    ‖unitSphereExp w z‖ = 1 := by
  by_cases hz : z = 0
  · subst z
    simp [unitSphereExp, hw]
  · have hr : ‖z‖ ≠ 0 := norm_ne_zero_iff.mpr hz
    have hcross :
        ⟪Real.cos ‖z‖ • w, Real.sinc ‖z‖ • z⟫_ℝ = 0 := by
      simp [real_inner_smul_left, real_inner_smul_right, htan]
    have hpyth :
        ‖unitSphereExp w z‖ ^ (2 : ℕ) =
          ‖Real.cos ‖z‖ • w‖ ^ (2 : ℕ) +
          ‖Real.sinc ‖z‖ • z‖ ^ (2 : ℕ) := by
      rw [unitSphereExp, norm_add_sq_real, hcross]
      ring
    have hfirst :
        ‖Real.cos ‖z‖ • w‖ ^ (2 : ℕ) =
          Real.cos ‖z‖ ^ (2 : ℕ) := by
      simp [norm_smul, hw, sq_abs]
    have hsinc :
        Real.sinc ‖z‖ = Real.sin ‖z‖ / ‖z‖ :=
      Real.sinc_of_ne_zero hr
    have hsecond :
        ‖Real.sinc ‖z‖ • z‖ ^ (2 : ℕ) =
          Real.sin ‖z‖ ^ (2 : ℕ) := by
      rw [norm_smul, Real.norm_eq_abs, hsinc]
      have hrpos : 0 < ‖z‖ := norm_pos_iff.mpr hz
      rw [abs_div, abs_of_pos hrpos]
      rw [div_mul_cancel₀ _ hr]
      exact sq_abs (Real.sin ‖z‖)
    have hsq : ‖unitSphereExp w z‖ ^ (2 : ℕ) = 1 := by
      rw [hpyth, hfirst, hsecond]
      nlinarith [Real.sin_sq_add_cos_sq ‖z‖]
    have hnonneg : 0 ≤ ‖unitSphereExp w z‖ := norm_nonneg _
    nlinarith


end
