    scalarSideConditions : List String (explicit)

    Ordered data items

    1. alpha>0 and alpha<=alpha0 are required for alpha^(-1) and for the finite log-mgf witness from the source alpha0-complexity assumption.
    2. dot{s}(t)>0 and dot{t}(s(t))=dot{s}(t)^(-1) are required by the inverse-schedule time-change and coefficient rewrite.
    3. C_LSI(t)>=0 is used only in the residual-exponent drop, not to change the differential inequality or theorem statement.
    4. K(t), a(t), and b(t) need the differentiability/continuity or interval-integrability required by lem:gronwall.
    5. Endpoint identities s(0)=0, S=s(T), and tilde_pi_{s(t)}=pi_t are required before rewriting K(0) and K(T).
    6. rho_s, tilde_pi_s, and pi_t must live on a common state space with enough density and boundary regularity for the KL derivative and DV instantiation.
