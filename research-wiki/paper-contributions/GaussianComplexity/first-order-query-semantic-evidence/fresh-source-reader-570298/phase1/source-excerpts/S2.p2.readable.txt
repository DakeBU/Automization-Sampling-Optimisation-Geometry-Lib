Let O(n):=\{O\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times n}:O^{\top}O=I_{n}\} denote the real orthogonal group, equipped with Haar probability
measure.
It has two components, \{\det O=+1\} , \{\det O=-1\} , and each have Haar probability 1/2 .
For b\in\{-1,+1\} , we write \mu_{n,b} for Haar probability
measure conditioned on \det O=b .
