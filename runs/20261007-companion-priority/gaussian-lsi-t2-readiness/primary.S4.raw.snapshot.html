4 Proof of the W2W_{2} guarantee

In this section, our goal is to prove the W2W_{2} guarantee (Theorem 1.1). However, since we will later need to obtain stronger moment estimates for Theorem 1.2, we will first develop the part of the analysis that is common to both results.

4.2 Local error analysis

The W2W_{2} proof will be based on the local error framework.
