    - Integrating a derivative bound with a backward exponential kernel gives the exact endpoint convolution factor. The quantities `source` and `energy` may have either sign: the theorem assumes the required derivative inequality directly. Algebraically, a nonzero rate is enough for the proof; strict positivity records the intended decay regime.

    theorem endpoint_le_of_deriv_le_exponential_kernel

    (hrate : 0 < rate) (ht : 0 ≤ t)
