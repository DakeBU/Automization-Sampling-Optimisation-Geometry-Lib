

B.1 Operator framework and micro–macro decomposition




Our target distribution is the joint law  [MATH A2.SS1.p1.m1: \pi_{\eta}]  defined in
(2.6), and we set  [MATH A2.SS1.p1.m2: \mathfrak{H}\coloneqq L^{2}(\pi_{\eta})] . Recall from
(3.11) that the ideal Proximal BPS has
transition kernel






 [MATH A2.Ex1.m1: \mathcal{K}\bigl((x,y),\mathrm{d}x^{\prime}\,\mathrm{d}y^{\prime}\bigr)=\delta_{2x-y}(\mathrm{d}y^{\prime})\,\bigl[\rho\,\pi_{\eta}^{X\mid Y=y^{\prime}}(\mathrm{d}x^{\prime})+(1-\rho)\,\mathcal{H}_{y^{\prime}}(x,\mathrm{d}x^{\prime})\bigr]\,,] 



where  [MATH A2.SS1.p1.m3: \mathcal{H}_{y^{\prime}}]  is the conditional half-turn kernel from
Proposition 3.2. Since the algorithm is a discrete-time
chain, its evolution is described by the induced one-step Markov operator
on observables,






 [MATH A2.Ex2.m1: (\mathsf{K}f)(x,y)\coloneqq\int_{\mathbb{R}^{2d}}f(x^{\prime},y^{\prime})\,\mathcal{K}\bigl((x,y),\mathrm{d}x^{\prime}\,\mathrm{d}y^{\prime}\bigr)\,.] 



Thus  [MATH A2.SS1.p1.m4: \mathcal{K}]  acts on probability laws, whereas  [MATH A2.SS1.p1.m5: \mathsf{K}]  is its
induced action on observables. The first term in brackets
in  [MATH A2.SS1.p1.m6: \mathcal{K}]  is the conditional resampling branch. After the
auxiliary coordinate is updated to  [MATH A2.SS1.p1.m7: y^{\prime}] ,
this branch replaces the  [MATH A2.SS1.p1.m8: x] -coordinate by an independent draw from
 [MATH A2.SS1.p1.m9: \pi_{\eta}^{X\mid Y=y^{\prime}}] . The associated conditional expectation operator
is






 [MATH A2.E1.m1: (\mathsf{P}f)(x,y)\coloneqq\int f(x^{\prime},y)\,\pi_{\eta}^{X\mid Y=y}(\mathrm{d}x^{\prime})=\mathbb{E}[f(X,Y)\mid Y=y]\,.] 

(B.1)


In particular,  [MATH A2.SS1.p1.m10: \mathsf{P}f]  depends only on the coordinate  [MATH A2.SS1.p1.m11: y] . The range
and kernel of  [MATH A2.SS1.p1.m12: \mathsf{P}]  are






 [MATH A2.E2.m1: \begin{split}\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}}&\coloneqq\operatorname{ran}(\mathsf{P})=\{f\in\mathfrak{H}:f(x,y)=g(y)\text{ for some }g\in L^{2}(\pi_{\eta}^{Y})\}\,,\\
\mathfrak{H}_{\perp}&\coloneqq\ker(\mathsf{P})=\operatorname{ran}(\mathsf{P}^{\perp})=\{f\in\mathfrak{H}:\mathbb{E}[f(X,Y)\mid Y]=0\}\,.\end{split}] 

(B.2)


Here  [MATH A2.SS1.p1.m13: \mathsf{P}^{\perp}\coloneqq I-\mathsf{P}] , so
 [MATH A2.SS1.p1.m14: \mathfrak{H}=\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}}\oplus\mathfrak{H}_{\perp}]  and
 [MATH A2.SS1.p1.m15: f=\mathsf{P}f+\mathsf{P}^{\perp}f] . In the common terminology of
hypocoercivity [14, 21],  [MATH A2.SS1.p1.m16: \operatorname{ran}(\mathsf{P})]  is the
macroscopic subspace and its orthogonal complement is the
microscopic subspace.
Thus functions in  [MATH A2.SS1.p1.m17: \mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}}]  depend only on the coordinate  [MATH A2.SS1.p1.m18: y] ,
whereas functions in  [MATH A2.SS1.p1.m19: \mathfrak{H}_{\perp}]  have zero mean under
 [MATH A2.SS1.p1.m20: \pi_{\eta}^{X\mid Y=y}]  for  [MATH A2.SS1.p1.m21: \pi_{\eta}^{Y}] -almost every  [MATH A2.SS1.p1.m22: y] .




Relative to the decomposition
 [MATH A2.SS1.p2.m1: \mathfrak{H}=\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}}\oplus\mathfrak{H}_{\perp}] , we write an operator
 [MATH A2.SS1.p2.m2: \mathsf{T}]  on  [MATH A2.SS1.p2.m3: \mathfrak{H}]  in block form as






 [MATH A2.E3.m1: \mathsf{T}=\begin{pmatrix}\mathsf{T}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}&\mathsf{T}_{\mathsf{P}\perp}\\
\mathsf{T}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}&\mathsf{T}_{\perp\perp}\end{pmatrix}\,,\hskip 20.00003pt\begin{aligned} \mathsf{T}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}&\coloneqq\mathsf{P}\mathsf{T}\mathsf{P}\,,&\mathsf{T}_{\mathsf{P}\perp}&\coloneqq\mathsf{P}\mathsf{T}\mathsf{P}^{\perp}\,,\\
\mathsf{T}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}&\coloneqq\mathsf{P}^{\perp}\mathsf{T}\mathsf{P}\,,&\mathsf{T}_{\perp\perp}&\coloneqq\mathsf{P}^{\perp}\mathsf{T}\mathsf{P}^{\perp}\,.\end{aligned}] 

(B.3)


The first subscript specifies the target subspace and the second the source
subspace; thus  [MATH A2.SS1.p2.m4: \mathsf{T}_{ab}:\mathfrak{H}_{b}\to\mathfrak{H}_{a}]  for
 [MATH A2.SS1.p2.m5: a,b\in\{\mathsf{P},\perp\}] .




We first apply this block notation to the reflection map  [MATH A2.SS1.p3.m1: \mathsf{U}]  from
(2.14). We use the same symbol for its pullback on
observables:






 [MATH A2.E4.m1: (\mathsf{U}f)(x,y)\coloneqq f(\mathsf{U}(x,y))=f(x,2x-y)\,.] 

(B.4)


This pullback is self-adjoint and unitary, and hence has block matrix






 [MATH A2.Ex3.m1: \mathsf{U}=\begin{pmatrix}\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}&\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\\
\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}&\mathsf{U}_{\perp\perp}\end{pmatrix}\,.] 



Here  [MATH A2.SS1.p3.m2: \mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}=\mathsf{P}\mathsf{U}\mathsf{P}]  maps  [MATH A2.SS1.p3.m3: \mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}}]  to itself, whereas
 [MATH A2.SS1.p3.m4: \mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}=\mathsf{P}^{\perp}\mathsf{U}\mathsf{P}]  maps  [MATH A2.SS1.p3.m5: \mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}}]  to  [MATH A2.SS1.p3.m6: \mathfrak{H}_{\perp}] .
Since
 [MATH A2.SS1.p3.m7: \mathsf{U}]  is self-adjoint and unitary,  [MATH A2.SS1.p3.m8: \mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}]  is a self-adjoint
contraction on  [MATH A2.SS1.p3.m9: \mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}}] . Writing  [MATH A2.SS1.p3.m10: \mathsf{U}^{2}=I]  in blocks gives






 [MATH A2.E5.m1: \mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}=I-\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}^{2}\,,\hskip 20.00003pt\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\mathsf{U}_{\perp\perp}=-\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\,.] 

(B.5)





We next express the joint half-turn operator  [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m1: \mathsf{H}]  in the same block
decomposition. It is defined in (A.9) and is a
self-adjoint Markov contraction by
Proposition A.2. Since  [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m2: \mathsf{H}]  acts only on the
 [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m3: x] -coordinate, while every
function in  [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m4: \operatorname{ran}(\mathsf{P})]  depends only on  [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m5: y] , we have
 [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m6: \mathsf{H}\mathsf{P}=\mathsf{P}] . Self-adjointness also gives
 [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m7: \mathsf{P}\mathsf{H}=\mathsf{P}] . Set
 [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m8: \mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}\coloneqq\mathsf{P}^{\perp}\mathsf{H}\mathsf{P}^{\perp}:\mathfrak{H}_{\perp}\to\mathfrak{H}_{\perp}] .
The identity  [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m9: \mathsf{P}\mathsf{H}=\mathsf{P}]  makes  [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m10: \mathfrak{H}_{\perp}]  invariant under
 [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m11: \mathsf{H}] , so  [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m12: \mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}]  is its restriction to  [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m13: \mathfrak{H}_{\perp}] . It is
therefore self-adjoint, and
 [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m14: \|\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}g\|=\|\mathsf{H}g\|\leq\|g\|]  for
 [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m15: g\in\mathfrak{H}_{\perp}] . Consequently,






 [MATH A2.E6.m1: \mathsf{H}=\mathsf{P}+\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}\,,\hskip 20.00003pt-I\preceq\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}\preceq I\hskip 10.00002pt\text{on }\mathfrak{H}_{\perp}\,.] 

(B.6)


Relative to  [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m16: \mathfrak{H}=\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}}\oplus\mathfrak{H}_{\perp}] , this decomposition
has the block representation






 [MATH A2.Ex4.m1: \mathsf{H}=\begin{pmatrix}I_{\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}}}&0\\
0&\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}\end{pmatrix}\,.] 



The Markov operator  [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m17: \mathsf{K}]  induced by the transition kernel  [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m18: \mathcal{K}] 
therefore factors as






 [MATH A2.E7.m1: \mathsf{K}=\mathsf{U}\bigl[\rho\mathsf{P}+(1-\rho)\mathsf{H}\bigr]=\mathsf{U}\bigl[\mathsf{P}+(1-\rho)\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}\bigr]\,.] 

(B.7)


Accordingly, its block representation is






 [MATH A2.Ex5.m1: \mathsf{K}=\begin{pmatrix}\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}&\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\\
\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}&\mathsf{U}_{\perp\perp}\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}I_{\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}}}&0\\
0&(1-\rho)\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}\end{pmatrix}\,.] 



The diagonal factor in the block representation of  [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m19: \mathsf{K}]  contracts
 [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m20: \mathfrak{H}_{\perp}]  but leaves  [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m21: \mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}}]  unchanged. For an observable
 [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m22: f\in\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}}] , contraction therefore relies on the
 [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m23: \mathfrak{H}_{\perp}] -component  [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m24: \mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}f]  created by reflection. The next
subsection estimates this component and the subsequent action of
 [MATH A2.SS1.p4.m25: \mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}] .
