B.3 Regularity In this section, we verify that the precision matrices in our construction belong
to \mathcal{A}_{d,\kappa} .
The spectral bounds follow from orthogonality within each block
and are preserved by the block-diagonal construction. Lemma B.8 ( Regularity). For every \bm{O}\in O(n)^{k} , A_{\bm{O}}\in\mathcal{A}_{d,\kappa} .
The potential f_{A_{\bm{O}}} has unique minimizer 0 and minimum value 0 . Proof of Lemma 3.4 . For O\in O(n) and v\in\mathbb{R}^{n} , orthogonality gives \|Ov\|=\|v\| . Hence, by Cauchy–Schwarz, |\langle v,Ov\rangle|\leq\|v\|^{2} .
Since v^{\top}A_{O}v=\frac{\kappa+1}{2}\|v\|^{2}-\frac{\kappa-1}{2}\langle v,Ov\rangle, we obtain \|v\|^{2}\leq v^{\top}A_{O}v\leq\kappa\|v\|^{2} .
Together with A_{O}=A_{O}^{\top} , this proves I_{n}\preceq A_{O}\preceq\kappa I_{n}. (26) Now let x=(x_{1},\ldots,x_{k},z)\in\mathbb{R}^{d} , where x_{j}\in\mathbb{R}^{n} and z\in\mathbb{R}^{d-kn} .
Applying Eq. ( 26 ) to each block yields \displaystyle\|x\|^{2} \displaystyle=\sum_{j=1}^{k}\|x_{j}\|^{2}+\|z\|^{2} \displaystyle\leq\sum_{j=1}^{k}x_{j}^{\top}A_{O_{j}}x_{j}+\|z\|^{2}=x^{\top}A_{\bm{O}}x \displaystyle\leq\kappa\sum_{j=1}^{k}\|x_{j}\|^{2}+\|z\|^{2}\leq\kappa\|x\|^{2}. The last inequality uses \kappa\geq 1 .
When kn=d , the terms involving z are omitted.
Since A_{\bm{O}} is symmetric, these inequalities imply I_{d}\preceq A_{\bm{O}}\preceq\kappa I_{d} , and therefore A_{\bm{O}}\in\mathcal{A}_{d,\kappa} . Finally, f_{A_{\bm{O}}}(0)=0 , whereas for every x\neq 0 , f_{A_{\bm{O}}}(x)=\frac{1}{2}x^{\top}A_{\bm{O}}x\geq\frac{1}{2}\|x\|^{2}>0. Thus 0 is the unique minimizer and the minimum value is 0 .
∎
