    Probability expectations preserve full real matrix positivity

    Read this first

    Mathematical statement

    An integrable, almost-everywhere positive semidefinite real matrix map has a positive semidefinite probability expectation.

    \[f\in L^1(\mu),\quad f(\omega)\succeq0\ \mu\text{-a.e.}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\int f\,d\mu\succeq0.\]

    Full assumptions and notation

    All objects and hypotheses

    • Let \(n\) be a finite index set, possibly empty, and let \((\Omega,\mu)\) be any measurable probability space. The function \(f\) takes values in real \(n\)-by-\(n\) matrices.
    • The explicit authored contracts are \(f\in L^1(\mu)\) and \(f(\omega)\succeq0\) almost everywhere. Integrability includes an a.e. strongly measurable representative and finite extended-norm integral.
    • Here \(A\succeq0\) means both \(A^\top=A\) and \(v^\top A v\ge0\) for every finite vector. Positivity is full matrix PSD, rather than entrywise positivity.
    • The matrix space uses its complete Euclidean operator norm and the integral is Bochner. No caller-supplied closed/convex cone, pointwise measurability, nonempty matrix index or representative certificate is required.
    • This arbitrary-function background statement is authored explicitly. Concrete Gaussian consumers prove its contracts internally; the original Gaussian Source Anchor gains no new premise.

theorem integral_posSemidef
    {n Ω : Type*} [Fintype n] [DecidableEq n] [MeasurableSpace Ω]
    {μ : Measure Ω} [IsProbabilityMeasure μ]
    (f : Ω → Matrix n n ℝ) (hf : Integrable f μ)
    (hpos : ∀ᵐ ω ∂μ, Matrix.PosSemidef (f ω)) :
    Matrix.PosSemidef (∫ ω, f ω ∂μ)
:= by
  classical
  let M := Matrix n n ℝ
    2. Prove the cone closed in the declared topology

    Hermitian matrices form the equalizer of continuous star and identity. For every fixed finite vector, its quadratic evaluation is continuous and the nonnegative half-line is closed. Intersect these closed conditions; no cone-geometry certificate is a public input.

    \[\mathcal P=\{A:A^\top=A\}\cap\bigcap_v\{A:v^\top A v\ge0\}.\]
    Corresponding Lean step
      have PSD_closed : IsClosed {A : M | A.PosSemidef} := by
    have hermitian_closed : IsClosed {A : M | A.IsHermitian} := by
      change IsClosed {A : M | star A = A}
      exact isClosed_eq continuous_star continuous_id
    have quadratic_closed (v : n → ℝ) :
        IsClosed {A : M | 0 ≤ dotProduct (star v) (Matrix.mulVec A v)} := by
      apply isClosed_le continuous_const
      change Continuous (fun A : M => ∑ a, star (v a) * ∑ b, A a b * v b)
      fun_prop
    convert hermitian_closed.inter (isClosed_iInter quadratic_closed) using 1
    ext A
    simp [Matrix.posSemidef_iff_dotProduct_mulVec]
    3. Use convexity and the canonical probability integral theorem

    Nonnegative scalar combinations of full PSD matrices remain PSD, proving convexity. Apply the existing closed-convex integral theorem to the actual a.e. membership and integrability hypotheses. The proof does not strengthen a.e. positivity to pointwise positivity.

    \[a,b\ge0,\ a+b=1,\ A,B\in\mathcal P\Longrightarrow aA+bB\in\mathcal P,\qquad\int f\,d\mu\in\mathcal P.\]
    Corresponding Lean step
      have PSD_convex : Convex ℝ {A : M | A.PosSemidef} := by
    intro A hA B hB a b ha hb hab
    exact (hA.smul ha).add (hB.smul hb)
  exact PSD_convex.integral_mem PSD_closed hpos hf
