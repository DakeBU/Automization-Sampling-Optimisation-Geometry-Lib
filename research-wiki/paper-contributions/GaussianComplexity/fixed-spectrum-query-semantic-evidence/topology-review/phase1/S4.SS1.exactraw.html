4.1 The Hard Family

We use a construction similar to that in [22, Section 9.2], with a cosine eigenvalue grid (Chebyshev nodes) and a random orthogonal rotation, while extending the spectral interval from [1,2][1,2] to [1,κ][1,\kappa]. For d≥2d\geq 2 and κ≥2\kappa\geq 2, define

The spectrum is deterministic and may be supplied to the algorithm. In particular, the Gaussian normalizing constant is known. The unknown component is the random eigenbasis represented by OO.

Remark 4.2 (Comparison with κ=2\kappa=2 case).

At κ=2\kappa=2, the spectrum in Eq. (11) recovers the original construction in [22].
