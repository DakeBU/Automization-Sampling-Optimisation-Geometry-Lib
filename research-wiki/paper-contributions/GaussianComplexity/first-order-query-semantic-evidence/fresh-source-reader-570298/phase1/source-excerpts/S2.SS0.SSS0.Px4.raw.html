Sampling accuracy and query complexity.

For a fixed input AA, let ρAAlg\rho_{A}^{\mathrm{Alg}} denote the law of the algorithm’s output, with all internal randomness included. For 0<ε<10<\varepsilon<1, define

QG​(d,κ,ε):=inf{T∈ℤ≥0:∃Alg​ with fixed query budget ​Tsuch that ​supA∈𝒜d,κTV⁡(ρAAlg,πA)≤ε}.Q_{G}(d,\kappa,\varepsilon):=\inf\left\{T\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}:\begin{array}[]{l}\exists\,\mathrm{Alg}\text{ with fixed query budget }T\\[2.0pt] \text{such that }\displaystyle\sup_{A\in\mathcal{A}_{d,\kappa}}\operatorname{TV}\bigl(\rho_{A}^{\mathrm{Alg}},\pi_{A}\bigr)\leq\varepsilon\end{array}\right\}. (2)

The accuracy requirement is uniform over inputs and concerns the output law after averaging over the algorithm’s seed. Throughout the paper, query budgets are deterministic caps on the number of queries in each run, rather than bounds on the expected number of queries.
