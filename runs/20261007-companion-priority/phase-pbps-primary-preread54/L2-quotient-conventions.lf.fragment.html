Definition D.1 (Hilbert space notation).

Let ζ\zeta be a probability measure. The real Hilbert space L2​(ζ)L^{2}(\zeta) consists of equivalence classes of measurable functions ff such that ∫f2​𝑑ζ<∞\int f^{2}\,\mathrm{d}\zeta<\infty. Its inner product and norm are

⟨f,g⟩L2​(ζ)≔∫f​g​𝑑ζ,‖f‖L2​(ζ)≔⟨f,f⟩L2​(ζ)1/2.\langle f,g\rangle_{L^{2}(\zeta)}\coloneqq\int fg\,\mathrm{d}\zeta\,,\hskip 20.00003pt\|f\|_{L^{2}(\zeta)}\coloneqq\langle f,f\rangle_{L^{2}(\zeta)}^{1/2}\,. (D.1)

We write

L02​(ζ)≔{f∈L2​(ζ):∫f​𝑑ζ=0}.L_{0}^{2}(\zeta)\coloneqq\Bigl\{f\in L^{2}(\zeta):\int f\,\mathrm{d}\zeta=0\Bigr\}\,. (D.2)

When ζ=πη\zeta=\pi_{\eta}, we abbreviate the inner product and norm by ⟨f,g⟩\langle f,g\rangle and ‖f‖\|f\|.

Two functions equal ζ\zeta-almost everywhere represent the same vector. A closed linear subspace ℳ⊆L2​(ζ)\mathcal{M}\subseteq L^{2}(\zeta) has orthogonal complement ℳ⟂≔{g:⟨f,g⟩=0​ for all ​f∈ℳ}\mathcal{M}^{\perp}\coloneqq\{g:\langle f,g\rangle=0\text{ for all }f\in\mathcal{M}\}. Its orthogonal projection is the unique bounded operator 𝖯ℳ\mathsf{P}_{\mathcal{M}} for which 𝖯ℳ​f∈ℳ\mathsf{P}_{\mathcal{M}}f\in\mathcal{M} and f−𝖯ℳ​f∈ℳ⟂f-\mathsf{P}_{\mathcal{M}}f\in\mathcal{M}^{\perp}.

All operators in the convergence analysis are bounded linear operators on the indicated Hilbert spaces unless explicitly stated otherwise.
