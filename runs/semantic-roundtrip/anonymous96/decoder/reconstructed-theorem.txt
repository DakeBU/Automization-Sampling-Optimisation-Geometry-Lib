SOURCE-BLIND RECONSTRUCTION OF THE ANONYMOUS PROPOSITION

Ambient objects and assumptions.
For every type E equipped with a normed additive commutative group structure and a real inner-product space structure, assume E is finite-dimensional over R and has a measurable structure equal to its Borel sigma-algebra. Dimension zero is included. Let V:E→R, α,β∈R≥0, and η∈R. Assume all six displayed named hypotheses:
(1) α>0 as a real number;
(2) α≤β as nonnegative reals;
(3) V is twice continuously differentiable over R;
(4) for EVERY x,v∈E,
    α||v||² ≤ D²V(x)[v,v] AND D²V(x)[v,v] ≤ β||v||²;
(5) η>0;
(6) βη≤1.
D²V(x)[v,v] is the Frechet derivative of the Frechet-derivative-valued map, evaluated successively on v and v. These are global bounds at all x in all directions v. No third-derivative hypothesis, randomness, choice of initial law, or restriction to a subset of E occurs. α,β are retained assumptions even though neither is used in the explicit formulas below.

All literal local definitions.
Let g=∇V denote the real inner-product gradient. For y,r,x,n,p∈E define
 c(y,r)=y−ηg(r),
 h(r,x)=g(x)−g(r),
 B(n,p)=p−[2<p,n>/||n||²]n.
The arguments n,p of B are arbitrary vectors; no normalization or nonzero condition on n is imposed. Real division is total with division by zero equal to zero. Thus B(0,p)=p exactly. For z=(x,p)∈E×E define
 S_r(x,p)=(x,B(h(r,x),p)),
 R(x,p)=(x,−p).
For y,r∈E and EVERY real u, positive, zero or negative, define
 Φ_(y,r,u)(x,p)
 = (c(y,r)+cos(u)(x−c(y,r))+sqrt(η)sin(u)p,
    −sin(u)/sqrt(η)(x−c(y,r))+cos(u)p).
The square root is the nonnegative real square root; η>0 makes sqrt(η)>0. Scalar juxtaposition in these vector expressions is real scalar multiplication. The trigonometric argument is u itself, and the first and second coordinate coefficients are exactly sqrt(η)sin(u) and −sin(u)/sqrt(η).
Define the real-valued rate
 λ_r(x,p)=sqrt(η) max(0,<p,h(r,x)>).
This is a finite nonnegative real by its definition. Define
 ell = (volume on E) ⊗ (volume on E)
on E×E, where volume is the canonical Hilbert-space Lebesgue measure and ⊗ is product measure. This is a phase-volume measure, not a probability or a density weighted by V.

Complete conclusion: thirteen simultaneous clauses.
Under the assumptions, the following single conjunction holds:
(A) The map (y,r,u,z)↦Φ_(y,r,u)(z) is jointly measurable from ((E×E)×(R×(E×E))) to E×E with the stated product measurable structures. The displayed parenthesization is the formal type (E×E)×R×(E×E), with products right-associated.
(B) The map (r,z)↦S_r(z) is jointly measurable.
(C) The map (r,z)↦λ_r(z) is jointly measurable.
(D) For EVERY y,r∈E and EVERY u∈R, Φ_(y,r,u) is MeasurePreserving from ell to ell: it is measurable and (Φ_(y,r,u))_*ell=ell.
(E) For EVERY r∈E, S_r is MeasurePreserving from ell to ell: it is measurable and (S_r)_*ell=ell.
(F) R is MeasurePreserving from ell to ell: it is measurable and R_*ell=ell.
(G) For EVERY y,r∈E,u∈R,z∈E×E, BOTH inverse identities hold pointwise:
    Φ_(y,r,−u)(Φ_(y,r,u)(z))=z,
    Φ_(y,r,u)(Φ_(y,r,−u)(z))=z.
(H) For EVERY r∈E,z∈E×E, S_r(S_r(z))=z.
(I) For EVERY z∈E×E, R(R(z))=z.
(J) For EVERY y,r∈E,u∈R,z∈E×E,
    R(Φ_(y,r,u)(R(z)))=Φ_(y,r,−u)(z).
    This is reversal conjugating time u to time −u for this explicit flow.
(K) For EVERY r∈E,z∈E×E,
    R(S_r(z))=S_r(R(z)).
(L) For EVERY r∈E,z∈E×E,
    λ_r(R(S_r(z)))=λ_r(z).
    Rate preservation is asserted for the COMPOSITION R after S_r; the statement does not separately assert λ_r∘R=λ_r or λ_r∘S_r=λ_r.
(M) For EVERY r∈E and EVERY real-valued function F:E×E→R, if F is Bochner integrable with respect to ell, then BOTH
    z↦F(S_r(z)) is Bochner integrable with respect to ell,
    ∫_(E×E) F(S_r(z)) d ell(z)=∫_(E×E) F(z) d ell(z).
    These are real Bochner integrals. The test F may be signed and need not be bounded, continuous, smooth or supplied with a pointwise Measurable proof. Integrable includes almost-everywhere strong measurability and absolute integrability. That full integrability premise is required; the assertion is not for every merely measurable function.

Quantifier order and senses.
The outer E/structures,V,α,β,η and all six assumptions precede the local definitions and conjunction. No witness function or existential object is chosen. y,r,u,z in the individual clauses are universally quantified exactly where displayed. In the final clause the order is ∀r,∀F,Integrable F ell→(Integrable(F∘S_r) ell AND equality of integrals). y,u are not arguments of this final reflection test and do not occur in it. The three joint measurability assertions cover parameters as well as state. The measure-preserving assertions quantify fixed y,r,u or r and compare the same ell on source and target; they are not a joint-volume assertion for parameter-state space.
All algebraic identities are literal equalities on EVERY state, including h(r,x)=0. They are not almost-everywhere statements. Measure preservation is equality of pushforward measures together with measurability, not a pointwise Jacobian formula or merely a formal determinant computation. No almost-everywhere quantifier appears as an explicit clause; the only AE content mentioned in the test class is the standard meaning of Bochner Integrable. In particular no pointwise representative regularity for F is silently added.
The conclusions give no joint continuity or differentiability claim for S_r, no Jacobian assertion, no measure preservation for B as a separately stated theorem, no flow group-composition law for arbitrary unequal times beyond the displayed inverse identities, and no rate-integrability assertion. Some properties may be derivable from the formulas or existing clauses, but they are not additional claimed conjuncts. No event process, paths, event counts, waiting times, finite horizon, sigma-algebra on a function space, invariant weighted law, stationary stochastic process, or detailed-balance identity for a transition kernel is present in the proposition. There is therefore no fixed-time versus all-time AE ambiguity or infinite-wait case in its scope.

Degeneracies and explicit boundaries.
Zero normal vector: if h(r,x)=0 then B(h(r,x),p)=p for every p. Consequently S_r(x,p)=(x,p) and λ_r(x,p)=0. All involution, commutation, rate and integration conclusions include these states with no exception. The reflection formula never requires division by a nonzero norm as a premise. In particular x=r gives this case.
Zero momentum: B(n,0)=0, S_r(x,0)=(x,0), R(x,0)=(x,0) and λ_r(x,0)=0. Such a state need not be fixed by the flow: if x differs from c(y,r), the flow may produce nonzero momentum. The state (c(y,r),0) is fixed by the displayed flow for all real u, directly from the formula.
Orthogonality: if <p,h(r,x)>=0 then B(h(r,x),p)=p and λ_r(x,p)=0, whether or not h(r,x) vanishes. No positive-rate premise occurs. When that inner product is nonzero, preservation in (L) is for the combined transformation R∘S_r, as written; it is not preservation under either factor alone.
Zero time: u=0 is allowed; Φ_(y,r,0) is the identity by its literal formula. Both inverse identities and time-reversal identity remain meaningful. Negative real times are allowed and are essential to their exact statement. There is no upper time bound or step count. η=0 is excluded by η>0, and α=0 is excluded; β>0 follows from α≤β and α>0. The case βη=1 is allowed because that inequality is non-strict.
Zero dimension: E and E×E each have one point. Gradients and all vectors are zero, the Hessian inequalities in v are vacuous, and all maps Φ,S,R are the unique identity map. The scalar assumptions α>0,α≤β,η>0,βη≤1 remain. λ is zero. Canonical zero-dimensional Hilbert Lebesgue volume has singleton mass one, hence ell also has singleton mass one. Every real-valued F on this singleton is integrable, and the final equality reduces to equality of its single real value.
Positive dimension: phase volume ell has infinite total mass. Thus a nonzero constant test function is not integrable and does not qualify for (M); boundedness by itself is insufficient. The zero test function always qualifies. Unbounded functions qualify whenever they are Bochner integrable. The theorem does not supply probability normalization.
There is no zero-horizon, zero-event or infinite-wait definition here: these concepts are outside the displayed proposition. All definitions are total on their displayed domains and the test class remains exactly Integrable F ell.

Constant dependencies.
The maps depend on V through g and h and on η through c and sqrt(η); Φ additionally uses y,r,u, while S and λ use r, and R is independent of those parameters. α,β occur only in assumptions and the admissible-step constraint βη≤1. ell depends only on the real inner-product space and canonical volumes. All numeric constants are exact: coefficient 2 in B; sign − in R and inverse times; rate sqrt(η); trigonometric frequency one. There is no hidden dimension lower bound, normalization constant, probabilistic error level, asymptotic rate or unspecified constant.
