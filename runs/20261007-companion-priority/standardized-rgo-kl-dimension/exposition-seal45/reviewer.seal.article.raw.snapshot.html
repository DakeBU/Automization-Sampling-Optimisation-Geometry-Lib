Mathematical statement

Let E be a finite-dimensional complete real Hilbert space with its Borel structure, including rank zero, and S a measurable parameter space. Let V be globally C2 with kappa>=1 and kappa^-1 norm(v)^2<=D²V(x)[v,v]<=norm(v)^2 for every x,v. For measurable eta/y with every eta(s)>0, there exists a measurable unique stationary proximal point p. Define the actual normalized volume tilt mu, its actual quadratic RGO reweighting R, its actual affine standardization r about that same p, the displayed smooth residual rho, and gamma=stdGaussian E. For every s, r(s) is a probability, absolutely continuous with respect to gamma, canonical KL is finite, gradient rho(s) is L2(r(s)), and canonical KL(r(s)||gamma).toReal<=eta(s)^2*real(finrank E)/2. No step-size upper bound, caller witness, law, coherence, domain or desired-bound certificate is assumed.

\[D_{\mathrm{KL}}(r_s\Vert\gamma)\le\frac{\eta_s^2 d}{2},\qquad d=\dim_{\mathbb R}E,\quad\gamma=\mathrm{stdGaussian}(E).\]

Proof roadmap

  1. 1Produce the actual unique-prox law

    The actual canonical KL/Fisher producer supplies measurable p, stationarity and uniqueness. The displayed volume tilt, quadratic reweighting and affine pushforward are the source posterior; probability, absolute continuity, finite canonical KL and the score L2 domain are actual outputs.

  2. 2Identify the numerical-Fisher witness internally

    The position/Fisher producer initially returns another stationary p prime. Uniqueness of p gives equality pointwise; Lean Core function extensionality gives equality of the families. Rewriting its entire output transports rho, Q, r, gradients and integration laws. The caller supplies no coherence condition.

  3. 3Reuse the actual numerical Fisher estimate

    The same-law position/Fisher parent already proves the actual second moment at most d and the score-energy bound at most eta squared times that moment. Its final output is the numerical Fisher bound below. This proof calls that genuine producer and does not repeat its moment or gradient-growth proof.

  4. 4Retain canonical KL and its true domains

    At the identical posterior and residual potential, the canonical KL/Fisher parent supplies the half-Fisher inequality together with actual probability, absolute continuity, finite canonical KL and score integrability. It is the finite canonical ENNReal KL real value, rather than a chosen density entropy or totalized infinite value.

  5. 5Compose with the exact real coefficient

    Multiply the actual numerical Fisher inequality by nonnegative one half and use order transitivity. Exact real arithmetic gives eta squared d divided by two. No division by d, eta or energy occurs, so zero-dimensional and zero-energy cases are retained.

  6. 6Exercise actual posterior consumers

    Actual tests specialize eta=2, the positive unbounded measurable family eta(s)=s²+1 with y(s)=s v, and the zero-dimensional constant potential. The last test uses the real finrank zero upper bound and canonical KL nonnegativity to prove the actual finite KL real value is zero. T2 and the W2/FIRST step remain separate.
