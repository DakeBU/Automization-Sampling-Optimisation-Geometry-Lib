global weighted Bochner boundary after the genuine local resolvent route.
The current compact localization proves ordinary volume L2 and weak equations;
it does not put chi*u into the original weighted closed-gradient domain.

Proposed source-specific route, requiring C2 W and bounded Hessian:
1. Derive |gradient W(x)| <= |gradient W(0)| + beta*|x| from the actual
   Hessian bound, reusing the compiled Hessian/secant background if compatible.
2. Construct actual scaled smooth cutoffs chi_R, plateau1 on B_R, support
   B_(2R), with derivative bounds C1/R and C2/R^2.
3. Prove the actual weak-generator commutator for L=Delta-gradient W dot grad:
   L(chi_R*u)=chi_R*Lu+2 inner(gradient chi_R,G)
      +u*(Delta chi_R-inner(gradient W,gradient chi_R)).
   Here Lu=epsilon*u-f is actual weighted L2 from the original resolvent.
4. On the derivative shell R<=|x|<=2R the drift coefficient is bounded by
   C1*(|gradient W(0)|/R+2*beta). Weighted L2 tails of u and G should therefore
   suffice for commutator convergence, without assuming |x|*u is weighted L2.
5. After real local H2 is proved, approximate the compact cutoff solution by
   actual smooth compact mollifications. On each fixed compact set, positive
   continuous weight and bounded drift should transfer volume convergence to
   the required weighted gradient and generator graph norms.
6. Apply the compiled compact Bochner identity to those actual approximants;
   prove local Hessian convergence/Fatou and original-operator identification
   before any global Hessian bound, coercivity or core admission.

mollifier convergence in the needed norms, weighted tails and original closed
operator identification remain obligations. A smooth-core assumption cannot
replace them. Reflected and unreflected RGO Hessian/gradient/Dirichlet scales
remain distinct. No epsilon-zero limit, Poincare, BL, measurable fiber choice,
