B.3 Modified  [A2.SS3.m1: L^{2}]  contraction and proof of the main theorem
We now prove Theorem 3.5 using a discrete modified  [A2.SS3.p1.m1: L^{2}]
argument. For  [A2.SS3.p1.m2: f\in L_{0}^{2}(\pi_{\eta})] , write
[A2.Ex6.m1: f_{\mathsf{P}}\coloneqq\mathsf{P}f\,,\hskip 20.00003ptf_{\perp}\coloneqq\mathsf{P}^{\perp}f\,.]
Here  [A2.SS3.p1.m3: f_{\mathsf{P}}]  and  [A2.SS3.p1.m4: f_{\perp}]  are, respectively, the macroscopic
and microscopic parts of  [A2.SS3.p1.m5: f] .
Fix  [A2.SS3.p2.m1: 0<\rho\leq 1/2] . The ordinary  [A2.SS3.p2.m2: L^{2}]  energy does not by itself give
the required one-step contraction. Indeed, since  [A2.SS3.p2.m3: \mathsf{U}]  is unitary and
[A2.SS3.p2.m4: \mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}]  is a contraction,
[A2.Ex7.m1: \displaystyle\|\mathsf{K}f\|^{2}-\|f\|^{2}]
[A2.Ex7.m2: \displaystyle=(1-\rho)^{2}\,\|\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}f_{\perp}\|^{2}-\|f_{\perp}\|^{2}]
[A2.E18.m1: \displaystyle\leq-\rho\,(2-\rho)\,\|f_{\perp}\|^{2}\leq-\rho\,\|f_{\perp}\|^{2}\,.]
(B.18)
This estimate controls  [A2.SS3.p2.m5: f_{\perp}]  but gives nothing when
[A2.SS3.p2.m6: f=f_{\mathsf{P}}] .
Reflection moves part of  [A2.SS3.p2.m7: f_{\mathsf{P}}]  into the
microscopic subspace, but this transfer is not captured by the  [A2.SS3.p2.m8: L^{2}]  norm,
which is a standard difficulty in hypocoercivity. The modified  [A2.SS3.p2.m9: L^{2}]
method addresses this difficulty by adding a corrector to the Lyapunov
function. The corrector records the transfer from  [A2.SS3.p2.m10: f_{\mathsf{P}}]  to
[A2.SS3.p2.m11: f_{\perp}] , allowing the contraction in
(B.18) to also control  [A2.SS3.p2.m12: f_{\mathsf{P}}] .
This idea goes back to Dolbeault, Mouhot, and Schmeiser [14]; here we
follow the refinement of Fan, Li, and
Lu [16].
To construct the corrector, we first isolate the part of  [A2.SS3.p3.m1: f_{\perp}]  that
couples to  [A2.SS3.p3.m2: f_{\mathsf{P}}]  under reflection. Define
[A2.Ex8.m1: f_{\mathsf{V}}\coloneqq\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}f_{\perp}\,.]
By (B.16),
[A2.SS3.p3.m3: \mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}f_{\perp}=\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{V}}] , while the component of
[A2.SS3.p3.m4: f_{\perp}]  orthogonal to  [A2.SS3.p3.m5: \operatorname{ran}(\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}})]  is annihilated by
[A2.SS3.p3.m6: \mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}] . Since  [A2.SS3.p3.m7: \mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}]  is an isometry,
[A2.SS3.p3.m8: \mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{V}}=\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}f_{\perp}]  is the orthogonal
projection of  [A2.SS3.p3.m9: f_{\perp}]  onto  [A2.SS3.p3.m10: \operatorname{ran}(\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}})] , and hence
[A2.SS3.p3.m11: \|f_{\mathsf{V}}\|\leq\|f_{\perp}\|] . We work with  [A2.SS3.p3.m12: f_{\mathsf{V}}] , rather
than with this projection itself, because  [A2.SS3.p3.m13: f_{\mathsf{V}}]  and
[A2.SS3.p3.m14: f_{\mathsf{P}}]  both belong to  [A2.SS3.p3.m15: \mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0}]  and can therefore be
paired in the corrector.
With this notation, let  [A2.SS3.p4.m1: \omega>0]  be a weight to be chosen below and
define the modified Lyapunov function
[A2.E19.m1: \mathscr{L}_{\omega}(f)\coloneqq\|f\|^{2}+\omega\mathscr{C}(f_{\mathsf{P}},f_{\mathsf{V}})\,,]
(B.19)
where the corrector is
[A2.E20.m1: \mathscr{C}(f_{\mathsf{P}},f_{\mathsf{V}})\coloneqq\frac{1}{2}\,\bigl(\|f_{\mathsf{P}}\|^{2}-\|f_{\mathsf{V}}\|^{2}\bigr)-\bigl\langle\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}f_{\mathsf{P}},f_{\mathsf{V}}\bigr\rangle\,.]
(B.20)
Here  [A2.SS3.p4.m2: \mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}]  is applied only on  [A2.SS3.p4.m3: \mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0}] , where
[A2.SS3.p4.m4: \mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}\succeq\gamma_{\rm gap}I] , so the corrector is well-defined.
To motivate this choice, recall from (B.7) that
[A2.SS3.p5.m1: \mathsf{K}]  acts in two stages: it first maps
[A2.Ex9.m1: f_{\mathsf{P}}+f_{\perp}\longmapsto f_{\mathsf{P}}+(1-\rho)\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}f_{\perp}]
and then applies  [A2.SS3.p5.m2: \mathsf{U}] . For the moment, set  [A2.SS3.p5.m3: \rho=0]  and replace
[A2.SS3.p5.m4: \mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}]  by  [A2.SS3.p5.m5: -I_{\mathfrak{H}_{\perp}}] . The error
[A2.SS3.p5.m6: I+\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}]  introduced by this replacement is controlled by
Proposition B.2. Denote the resulting idealized operator
by
[A2.Ex10.m1: \mathsf{K}_{\rm id}\coloneqq\mathsf{U}(\mathsf{P}-\mathsf{P}^{\perp})\,.]
Set  [A2.SS3.p5.m7: g\coloneqq\mathsf{K}_{\rm id}f] , and define the corresponding components by
[A2.Ex11.m1: g_{\mathsf{P}}\coloneqq\mathsf{P}g\,,\hskip 20.00003ptg_{\mathsf{V}}\coloneqq\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\mathsf{P}^{\perp}g\,.]
In terms of these components,  [A2.SS3.p5.m8: \mathsf{K}_{\rm id}]  is represented by the
operator-valued rotation matrix
[A2.Ex12.m1: \begin{pmatrix}g_{\mathsf{P}}\\
g_{\mathsf{V}}\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}&-\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}\\
\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}&\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}f_{\mathsf{P}}\\
f_{\mathsf{V}}\end{pmatrix}\,.]
Indeed, the first component follows immediately from the block representation
of  [A2.SS3.p5.m9: \mathsf{U}]  and the polar decomposition
(B.16):
[A2.Ex13.m1: \displaystyle g_{\mathsf{P}}]
[A2.Ex13.m2: \displaystyle=\mathsf{P}\mathsf{U}(f_{\mathsf{P}}-f_{\perp})=\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{P}}-\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}f_{\perp}=\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{P}}-\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}f_{\perp}=\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{P}}-\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{V}}\,.]
For the second component, substitute
[A2.SS3.p5.m10: \mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}=\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}]  into
[A2.SS3.p5.m11: \mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\mathsf{U}_{\perp\perp}=-\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}]  from
(B.5). Since  [A2.SS3.p5.m12: \mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}]  commutes with
[A2.SS3.p5.m13: \mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}] , this gives
[A2.Ex14.m1: \mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\mathsf{U}_{\perp\perp}=-\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\,.]
The ranges of  [A2.SS3.p5.m14: \mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\mathsf{U}_{\perp\perp}]  and
[A2.SS3.p5.m15: \mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}]  lie in  [A2.SS3.p5.m16: \mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0}] , where
[A2.SS3.p5.m17: \mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}]  is bounded. Applying  [A2.SS3.p5.m18: \mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}]  to the
displayed identity therefore gives
[A2.SS3.p5.m19: \mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\mathsf{U}_{\perp\perp}=-\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}] . Hence
[A2.Ex15.m1: \displaystyle g_{\mathsf{V}}]
[A2.Ex15.m2: \displaystyle=\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}\mathsf{P}^{\perp}\mathsf{U}(f_{\mathsf{P}}-f_{\perp})=\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}(\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{P}}-\mathsf{U}_{\perp\perp}f_{\perp})=\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{P}}+\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}f_{\perp}=\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{P}}+\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{V}}\,.]
This proves the claimed matrix formula. Since
[A2.SS3.p5.m20: \mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}^{2}+\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{2}=I] , the block matrix acts as a rotation on
[A2.SS3.p5.m21: \mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0}\oplus\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0}] . Under this rotation,
direct substitution into (B.20) gives
[A2.Ex16.m1: \displaystyle\mathscr{C}(g_{\mathsf{P}},g_{\mathsf{V}})-\mathscr{C}(f_{\mathsf{P}},f_{\mathsf{V}})]
[A2.Ex17.m1: \displaystyle\hskip 10.00002pt=\mathscr{C}(\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{P}}-\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{V}}\,,\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{P}}+\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{V}})-\mathscr{C}(f_{\mathsf{P}},f_{\mathsf{V}})]
[A2.E21.m1: \displaystyle\hskip 10.00002pt=-\|f_{\mathsf{P}}\|^{2}+\|f_{\mathsf{V}}\|^{2}\,.]
(B.21)
For  [A2.SS3.p5.m22: 0<\omega<\rho] , combining the leading terms from
(B.18) and
(B.21), and using
[A2.SS3.p5.m23: \|f_{\mathsf{V}}\|\leq\|f_{\perp}\|] , gives
[A2.Ex18.m1: \displaystyle-\rho\,\|f_{\perp}\|^{2}+\omega\,\bigl(-\|f_{\mathsf{P}}\|^{2}+\|f_{\mathsf{V}}\|^{2}\bigr)\leq-\omega\,\|f_{\mathsf{P}}\|^{2}-(\rho-\omega)\,\|f_{\perp}\|^{2}\,.]
Thus the leading terms control both components. This calculation makes
explicit the role of the corrector in the modified  [A2.SS3.p5.m24: L^{2}]  hypocoercivity
argument; see also Fan, Li, and Lu [16].
We now turn from the idealized calculation to the contraction proof. The
modified  [A2.SS3.p6.m1: L^{2}]  argument has two steps: we first show that the Lyapunov
function is equivalent to the squared  [A2.SS3.p6.m2: L^{2}]  norm and then establish its
one-step decay.
Lemma B.3 (Equivalence with the squared  [A2.Thmtheorem3.m1: L^{2}]  norm).
If  [A2.Thmtheorem3.p1.m1: 0<\omega\leq\gamma_{\rm gap}] , then every
[A2.Thmtheorem3.p1.m2: f\in L_{0}^{2}(\pi_{\eta})]  satisfies
[A2.E22.m1: \frac{1}{2}\,\|f\|^{2}\leq\mathscr{L}_{\omega}(f)\leq\frac{3}{2}\,\|f\|^{2}\,.]
(B.22)
Proof.
The operators  [A2.SS3.p7.m1: \mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}]  and  [A2.SS3.p7.m2: \mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}]  commute, so
[A2.SS3.p7.m3: \mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}]  is self-adjoint on
[A2.SS3.p7.m4: \mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0}] . Moreover,
[A2.Ex19.m1: I+(\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1})^{2}=I+\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}^{2}\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-2}=\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-2}\,,]
where the last identity uses  [A2.SS3.p7.m5: \mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}^{2}+\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{2}=I] .
By (B.20),
[A2.Ex20.m1: \displaystyle 2\mathscr{C}(f_{\mathsf{P}},f_{\mathsf{V}})]
[A2.Ex20.m2: \displaystyle=\Bigl\langle\begin{pmatrix}f_{\mathsf{P}}\\
f_{\mathsf{V}}\end{pmatrix}\,,\begin{pmatrix}I&-\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}\\
-\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}&-I\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}f_{\mathsf{P}}\\
f_{\mathsf{V}}\end{pmatrix}\Bigr\rangle\,.]
The square of the block operator in this display is
[A2.Ex21.m1: \begin{pmatrix}I&-\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}\\
-\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}&-I\end{pmatrix}^{\!2}=\begin{pmatrix}\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-2}&0\\
0&\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-2}\end{pmatrix}\,.]
Since the block operator is self-adjoint and
[A2.SS3.p7.m6: \mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}\succeq\gamma_{\rm gap}I] ,
[A2.Ex22.m1: \Bigl\|\begin{pmatrix}I&-\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}\\
-\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}&-I\end{pmatrix}\Bigr\|=\|\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}^{-1}\|\leq\frac{1}{\gamma_{\rm gap}}\,.]
Applying this operator norm bound to the preceding quadratic form gives
[A2.E23.m1: |\mathscr{C}(f_{\mathsf{P}},f_{\mathsf{V}})|\leq\frac{1}{2\gamma_{\rm gap}}\,\bigl(\|f_{\mathsf{P}}\|^{2}+\|f_{\mathsf{V}}\|^{2}\bigr)\leq\frac{1}{2\gamma_{\rm gap}}\,\|f\|^{2}\,.]
(B.23)
Here the second inequality uses  [A2.SS3.p7.m7: \|f_{\mathsf{P}}\|^{2}+\|f_{\mathsf{V}}\|^{2}\leq\|f_{\mathsf{P}}\|^{2}+\|f_{\perp}\|^{2}=\|f\|^{2}] .
Finally, (B.19),
(B.23), and  [A2.SS3.p7.m8: \omega\leq\gamma_{\rm gap}]  imply
[A2.E24.m1: \displaystyle\bigl|\mathscr{L}_{\omega}(f)-\|f\|^{2}\bigr|]
[A2.E24.m2: \displaystyle=\omega\,|\mathscr{C}(f_{\mathsf{P}},f_{\mathsf{V}})|\leq\frac{\omega}{2\gamma_{\rm gap}}\,\|f\|^{2}\leq\frac{1}{2}\,\|f\|^{2}\,.\qed]
(B.24)
Lemma B.4 (One-step decay of the Lyapunov function).
There exist universal constants  [A2.Thmtheorem4.p1.m1: c_{\rm hyp},c_{0},c>0]  such that the
following holds. Set
[A2.E25.m1: \Lambda_{\rho}\coloneqq\log(1/\gamma_{\rm gap})+\Bigl(\frac{\rho}{\gamma_{\rm gap}}\Bigr)^{2}\,,\hskip 20.00003pt\omega\coloneqq\frac{c_{\rm hyp}\rho}{\Lambda_{\rho}}\,.]
(B.25)
If  [A2.Thmtheorem4.p1.m2: \beta\eta\leq c_{0}] , then every
[A2.Thmtheorem4.p1.m3: f\in L_{0}^{2}(\pi_{\eta})]  satisfies
[A2.E26.m1: \mathscr{L}_{\omega}(\mathsf{K}f)\leq\Bigl(1-\frac{c\rho}{\Lambda_{\rho}}\Bigr)\,\mathscr{L}_{\omega}(f)\,.]
(B.26)
Proof.
