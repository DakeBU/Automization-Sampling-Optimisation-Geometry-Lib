Recommended next mathematical edge

On the SAME centered macroscopic real Hilbert space H_P,0, let A0 be the produced centered restriction of U_PP, let Gamma0 be the produced restriction of Gamma_P, and let Inv be the SAME bounded two-sided inverse of Gamma0. Define

  C(u,v) = (||u||^2 - ||v||^2)/2 - inner_R(A0(Inv u),v).

The shortest new source-backed target is, for EVERY u,v,r in that SAME centered space,

  C(u + Gamma0 r, v - A0 r) - C(u,v)
    = inner_R(u,Inv r) + ||r||^2/2.

The coefficients, signs, order A0(Inv u), and occurrence of Inv on r are exact. The target is the unnumbered algebra between B27 and B28, printed as A2.Ex28 through A2.Ex34. It is not a new assertion about an arbitrary inverse on the full, uncentered macroscopic space. It adds neither smoothness nor surjectivity nor strict-endpoint requirements. It has no rho, omega or new smallness condition of its own.
