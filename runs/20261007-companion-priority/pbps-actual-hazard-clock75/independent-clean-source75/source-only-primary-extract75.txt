$\beta$-smoothness of $V$,
$\|h_{\widetilde{x}}(x)\|=\|\nabla V(x)-\nabla V(\widetilde{x})\|\leq\beta\,\|x-\widetilde{x}\|\,.$
Drawing the reference point from the same localized conditional law keeps
the residual gradient small and reduces the expected number of bounces per
run of the half-turn process to order $\beta\eta\sqrt{d}$; see
Proposition 3.3.
Having specified the dynamics, it remains to choose the integration
horizon. For fixed $(y,\widetilde{x})$, the deterministic part of
(3.5) is harmonic motion about its equilibrium
position $c_{y,\widetilde{x}}$. In the absence of bounces, its position at
time $t$ satisfies
$x_{t}-c_{y,\widetilde{x}}=(x_{0}-c_{y,\widetilde{x}})\cos t+\sqrt{\eta}\,p_{0}\sin t\,.$
(3.6)
As discussed earlier, we choose the integration horizon $t=\pi$, corresponding to one
half-period. This is the first non-zero harmonic time at which the
position $x_{\pi}$ no longer depends on the initial momentum $p_{0}$.
Without bounces, the resulting map is
$(x_{\pi},p_{\pi})=(2c_{y,\widetilde{x}}-x_{0},-p_{0})\,.$
(3.7)
Thus, the position is reflected through the equilibrium position
$c_{y,\widetilde{x}}$ and retains its dependence on $x_{0}$. Bounce

fixed horizon $t=\pi$ retains the memory-preserving structure of the
half-turn process.

Proposition 3.1 (Well-posedness and stationarity of the conditional phase space process).
For every fixed $y,\widetilde{x}\in\mathbb{R}^{d}$ and initial phase space point
$(x_{0},p_{0})$, the event-driven dynamics in Algorithm 1, with

Proof of the well-posedness assertion in
Proposition 3.1.
Fix $y,\widetilde{x}\in\mathbb{R}^{d}$. In the notation of
(3.4), the bounce rate
(3.9) reads
$\lambda_{\widetilde{x}}(x,p)=\sqrt{\eta}\,[p^{\top}h_{\widetilde{x}}(x)]_{+}\,.$
The deterministic harmonic flow, namely the solution
(4.5) of (3.8), and
the bounce map are
$\displaystyle\Phi_{t}^{y,\widetilde{x}}(x,p)$
$\displaystyle\coloneqq\bigl(c_{y,\widetilde{x}}+(x-c_{y,\widetilde{x}})\cos t+\sqrt{\eta}\,p\sin t,\;-\eta^{-1/2}(x-c_{y,\widetilde{x}})\sin t+p\cos t\bigr)\,,$
$\displaystyle\mathsf{S}_{\widetilde{x}}(x,p)$
$\displaystyle\coloneqq(x,R_{h_{\widetilde{x}}(x)}p)\,,\hskip 20.00003ptR_{0}\coloneqq I\,.$
The bounce map is an involution. Although there is a possible discontinuity at $h_{\widetilde{x}}(x)=0$, this creates no ambiguity
because the event rate vanishes there.
Let $(E_{n})_{n\geq 1}$ be independent $\operatorname{Exp}(1)$
random variables. Starting from
$\zeta_{0}\coloneqq(x_{0},p_{0})$ at time $T_{0}\coloneqq 0$, define recursively
$\displaystyle S_{n+1}$
$\displaystyle\coloneqq\inf\Bigl\{u\geq 0:\int_{0}^{u}\lambda_{\widetilde{x}}(\Phi_{s}^{y,\widetilde{x}}(\zeta_{T_{n}}))\,\mathrm{d}s\geq E_{n+1}\Bigr\}\,,$
(A.1)
$\displaystyle T_{n+1}$
$\displaystyle\coloneqq T_{n}+S_{n+1}\,,\hskip 20.00003pt\zeta_{T_{n+1}}\coloneqq\mathsf{S}_{\widetilde{x}}\bigl(\Phi_{S_{n+1}}^{y,\widetilde{x}}(\zeta_{T_{n}})\bigr)\,,$
(A.2)
when $S_{n+1}<\infty$. If the set is empty, set
$T_{n+1}=S_{n+1}=\infty$ and stop the recursion. Between jumps, set
$\zeta_{T_{n}+t}=\Phi_{t}^{y,\widetilde{x}}(\zeta_{T_{n}})$ for
$0\leq t<S_{n+1}$.
To prove non-explosion, define the shifted harmonic energy
$H_{y,\widetilde{x}}(x,p)\coloneqq\frac{1}{2}\,\bigl\{\eta^{-1}\,\|x-c_{y,\widetilde{x}}\|^{2}+\|p\|^{2}\bigr\}\,.$
The flow and each bounce preserve this energy. Hence, with
$\mathcal{E}\coloneqq H_{y,\widetilde{x}}(x_{0},p_{0})$, every state produced by
the recursion satisfies
$\|p\|\leq\sqrt{2\mathcal{E}}\,,\hskip 20.00003pt\|x-c_{y,\widetilde{x}}\|\leq\sqrt{2\eta\mathcal{E}}\,.$
Because $\nabla V$ is $\beta$-Lipschitz,
$\lambda_{\widetilde{x}}(x,p)\leq\bar{\lambda}_{\mathcal{E}}(y,\widetilde{x})$ throughout the path, where
$\bar{\lambda}_{\mathcal{E}}(y,\widetilde{x})\coloneqq\sqrt{\eta}\,\beta\sqrt{2\mathcal{E}}\,\bigl\{\sqrt{2\eta\mathcal{E}}+\|c_{y,\widetilde{x}}-\widetilde{x}\|\bigr\}\,.$
Hence,
$\int_{0}^{u}\lambda_{\widetilde{x}}(\Phi_{s}^{y,\widetilde{x}}(\zeta_{T_{n}}))\,\mathrm{d}s\leq\bar{\lambda}_{\mathcal{E}}(y,\widetilde{x})\,u\,.$
If $\bar{\lambda}_{\mathcal{E}}(y,\widetilde{x})=0$, the event rate
vanishes and there are no jumps. Otherwise,
(A.1) implies, for every finite waiting time,
$S_{n+1}\geq\frac{E_{n+1}}{\bar{\lambda}_{\mathcal{E}}(y,\widetilde{x})}\,.$
If the recursion does not terminate, it follows that
$T_{n}\geq\frac{\sum_{j=1}^{n}E_{j}}{\bar{\lambda}_{\mathcal{E}}(y,\widetilde{x})}\longrightarrow\infty$
almost surely by the strong law of large numbers. Thus, the jump times
cannot accumulate, and the recursive construction defines a unique
process for all $t\geq 0$. Finally, the
memoryless property of the exponential clocks gives the time-homogeneous
Markov property. This is precisely the integrated hazard construction
of Davis [12, Section 2].
