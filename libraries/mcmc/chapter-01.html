    This page establishes a stable source route and truth boundary; it does not claim a completed formalization.

    Separate the sampler from its target and its estimator.

    \[\pi P=\pi,\qquad \widehat I_n=\frac1n\sum_{k=1}^n f(X_k)\]

    Share measurable kernels, invariance, covariance and integrability. The RKHS kernel of §1.5 is not a Markov transition kernel. Pull §1.3 forward; stochastic calculus is only a prerequisite for the diffusion branch. A Markov law contract is not an existence theorem for a process or semigroup.
