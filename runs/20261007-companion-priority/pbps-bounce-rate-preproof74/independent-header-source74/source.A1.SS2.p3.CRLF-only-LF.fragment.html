Since ∇V\nabla V is continuous, the harmonic flow, event rate, and reflection map are jointly Borel; the convention R0=IR_{0}=I ensures this even where the reflection normal vanishes. The integrated hazard is jointly Borel, with continuous, nondecreasing dependence on time, so its threshold-crossing times are Borel, as can be checked using rational times. Thus the recursion (A.1)–(A.2) makes each jump time and post-jump state Borel in (y,x0,p0,x~,(Ej)j≥1)(y,x_{0},p_{0},\widetilde{x},(E_{j})_{j\geq 1}). By non-explosion, the terminal state at time π\pi is the almost-sure limit of the Borel terminal states obtained by allowing at most nn jumps. Setting the limit to zero wherever it does not exist gives a Borel version.
