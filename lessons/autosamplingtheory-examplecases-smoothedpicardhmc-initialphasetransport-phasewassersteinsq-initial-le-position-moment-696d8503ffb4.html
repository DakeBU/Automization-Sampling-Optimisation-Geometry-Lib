    Couple the initial and reference phase laws with identical momentum

    AutoSamplingTheory.ExampleCases.SmoothedPicardHMC.InitialPhaseTransport.phaseWassersteinSq_initial_le_position_moment · theorem · Teaching coverage

    Read this first

    Mathematical statement

    The actual initial phase law at xref and any product reference law with standard-Gaussian momentum admit a coupling that uses the same momentum on both sides. Its M-kappa transportation cost is at most the reference law's position second moment about xref.

    \[W_{2,M_\kappa}^{2}(\delta_{x_{\rm ref}}\otimes\gamma,\,\pi\otimes\gamma)\le\int\|x_{\rm ref}-y\|^2\,\pi(dy),\qquad\kappa\ge1,\quad\gamma=\mathsf N(0,I).\]

    Proof roadmap

    1. 1Build the actual coupling

      Draw Y from pi and P independently from the standard Gaussian; pair the phase states (xref,P) and (Y,P). The first and second marginals are exactly the required phase laws.

    2. 2Cancel the momentum displacement

      For a paired sample the momentum difference is zero. The paper's twisted quadratic form therefore reduces to its position coefficient, which is at most one when kappa is at least one.

    3. 3Bound the Kantorovich infimum

      Any admissible coupling upper-bounds the optimal transport cost. Measurability permits pushforward integration, and the finite position moment converts the nonnegative Lebesgue integral back to the ordinary expectation.

    Full assumptions and notation

    All objects and hypotheses

    • E is a finite-dimensional complete real inner-product Borel space.
    • The target position law pi is a probability measure with an integrable squared distance from xref.
    • The paper phase parameter kappa is at least one; its M_kappa quadratic form is used without changing normalization.
    • Both phase laws use the same standard-Gaussian momentum marginal.

    Mathematical proof

    1. Build the actual coupling

    Draw Y from pi and P independently from the standard Gaussian; pair the phase states (xref,P) and (Y,P). The first and second marginals are exactly the required phase laws.

    \[\Gamma=\operatorname{Law}((x_{\rm ref},P),(Y,P)),\quad\Gamma_1=\delta_{x_{\rm ref}}\otimes\gamma,\quad\Gamma_2=\pi\otimes\gamma.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    identicalMomentumCoupling; Measure.fst_map_prodMk, Measure.snd_map_prodMk and Measure.dirac_prod certify both marginals.

    2. Cancel the momentum displacement

    For a paired sample the momentum difference is zero. The paper's twisted quadratic form therefore reduces to its position coefficient, which is at most one when kappa is at least one.

    \[\|(x_{\rm ref},P)-(Y,P)\|_{M_\kappa}^2=\left(\frac1{2\kappa}+\frac12\right)\|x_{\rm ref}-Y\|^2\le\|x_{\rm ref}-Y\|^2.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    phaseCost_identical_momentum_le unfolds the exact phase cost and proves the coefficient inequality.

    3. Bound the Kantorovich infimum

    Any admissible coupling upper-bounds the optimal transport cost. Measurability permits pushforward integration, and the finite position moment converts the nonnegative Lebesgue integral back to the ordinary expectation.

    \[\inf_{\Lambda\in\mathsf{Cpl}(\mu_0,\Pi)}\int c_{M_\kappa}\,d\Lambda\le\int c_{M_\kappa}\,d\Gamma\le\mathbb E_\pi\|x_{\rm ref}-Y\|^2.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    Transport.transportCost_le_lintegral_of_isCoupling, lintegral_map, lintegral_mono and ofReal_integral_eq_lintegral_ofReal.

    theorem phaseWassersteinSq_initial_le_position_moment
    (π : Measure E) [IsProbabilityMeasure π] (xref : E)
    {κ : ℝ} (hκ : 1 ≤ κ)
    (hpos : Integrable (fun y : E => ‖xref - y‖ ^ 2) π) :
    PhaseMetric.phaseWassersteinSq κ
      ((Measure.dirac xref).prod (stdGaussian E))
      (π.prod (stdGaussian E)) ≤
        ENNReal.ofReal (∫ y : E, ‖xref - y‖ ^ 2 ∂π) := by
  let γ := identicalMomentumCoupling π xref
  have hγ := identicalMomentumCoupling_isCoupling π xref
  have hcost := Transport.transportCost_le_lintegral_of_isCoupling
    (PhaseMetric.phaseCost (E := E) κ) _ _ γ hγ
  change PhaseMetric.phaseWassersteinSq κ _ _ ≤ _
  refine hcost.trans ?_
  have hmeas : Measurable (PhaseMetric.phaseCost (E := E) κ) := by
    unfold PhaseMetric.phaseCost
    change Measurable (fun z : (E × E) × (E × E) =>
      ENNReal.ofReal ((1 / (2 * κ) + 1 / 2) * ‖(z.1 - z.2).1‖ ^ 2 +
        inner ℝ (z.1 - z.2).1 (z.1 - z.2).2 + ‖(z.1 - z.2).2‖ ^ 2))
    fun_prop
  rw [show γ = (π.prod (stdGaussian E)).map (fun z => ((xref, z.2), z)) by rfl,
    lintegral_map hmeas (by fun_prop)]
  have hbound : (∫⁻ z : E × E,
      PhaseMetric.phaseCost κ ((xref, z.2), z) ∂π.prod (stdGaussian E)) ≤
      ∫⁻ z : E × E, ENNReal.ofReal (‖xref - z.1‖ ^ 2) ∂π.prod (stdGaussian E) := by
    apply lintegral_mono
    intro z
    exact phaseCost_identical_momentum_le hκ xref z.1 z.2
  refine hbound.trans_eq ?_
  rw [← ofReal_integral_eq_lintegral_ofReal
    (hpos.comp_fst (stdGaussian E))
    (Filter.Eventually.of_forall fun z => sq_nonneg _)]
  congr 1
  rw [integral_prod _ (hpos.comp_fst (stdGaussian E))]
  simp

end AutoSamplingTheory.ExampleCases.SmoothedPicardHMC.InitialPhaseTransport
