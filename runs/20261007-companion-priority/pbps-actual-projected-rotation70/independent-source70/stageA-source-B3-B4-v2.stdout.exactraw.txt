B.3 Modified 
MATH A2.SS3.m1 RAW[801333,801544) L^{2}
 contraction and proof of the main theorem

We now prove Theorem 3.5 using a discrete modified 
MATH A2.SS3.p1.m1 RAW[802056,802270) L^{2}

argument. For

, write

Here

MATH A2.SS3.p1.m4 RAW[804081,804305) f_{\perp}

and microscopic parts of 
MATH A2.SS3.p1.m5 RAW[804366,804549) f

Fix 
MATH A2.SS3.p2.m1 RAW[804638,804945) 0<\rho\leq 1/2

MATH A2.SS3.p2.m2 RAW[804960,805174) L^{2}

the required one-step contraction. Indeed, since 
MATH A2.SS3.p2.m3 RAW[805255,805459) \mathsf{U}
 is unitary and

MATH A2.Ex7.m1 RAW[806057,806606) \displaystyle\|\mathsf{K}f\|^{2}-\|f\|^{2}

MATH A2.Ex7.m2 RAW[806659,807652) \displaystyle=(1-\rho)^{2}\,\|\mathsf{H}_{\perp\perp}f_{\perp}\|^{2}-\|f_{\perp}\|^{2}

MATH A2.E18.m1 RAW[807941,808962) \displaystyle\leq-\rho\,(2-\rho)\,\|f_{\perp}\|^{2}\leq-\rho\,\|f_{\perp}\|^{2}\,.

(B.18)

This estimate controls 
MATH A2.SS3.p2.m5 RAW[809259,809483) f_{\perp}

MATH A2.SS3.p2.m6 RAW[809507,809779) f=f_{\mathsf{P}}

Reflection moves part of

microscopic subspace, but this transfer is not captured by the 
MATH A2.SS3.p2.m8 RAW[810114,810328) L^{2}

which is a standard difficulty in hypocoercivity. The modified 
MATH A2.SS3.p2.m9 RAW[810398,810612) L^{2}

method addresses this difficulty by adding a corrector to the Lyapunov
function. The corrector records the transfer from 
MATH A2.SS3.p2.m10 RAW[810734,810970) f_{\mathsf{P}}
 to

MATH A2.SS3.p2.m11 RAW[810974,811199) f_{\perp}
, allowing the contraction in
(B.18) to also control 
MATH A2.SS3.p2.m12 RAW[811585,811821) f_{\mathsf{P}}

This idea goes back to Dolbeault, Mouhot, and Schmeiser [14]; here we
follow the refinement of Fan, Li, and
Lu [16].

To construct the corrector, we first isolate the part of 
MATH A2.SS3.p3.m1 RAW[812280,812504) f_{\perp}
 that
couples to 
MATH A2.SS3.p3.m2 RAW[812521,812756) f_{\mathsf{P}}

MATH A2.Ex8.m1 RAW[813002,813589) f_{\mathsf{V}}\coloneqq\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}f_{\perp}\,.

By (B.16),

MATH A2.SS3.p3.m3 RAW[814045,814780) \mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}f_{\perp}=\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{V}}

MATH A2.SS3.p3.m4 RAW[814805,815029) f_{\perp}

MATH A2.SS3.p3.m5 RAW[815044,815500) \operatorname{ran}(\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}})

MATH A2.SS3.p3.m6 RAW[815519,815857) \mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}
. Since 
MATH A2.SS3.p3.m7 RAW[815865,816177) \mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}
 is an isometry,

MATH A2.SS3.p3.m8 RAW[816194,817096) \mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}f_{\mathsf{V}}=\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}^{*}f_{\perp}

projection of 
MATH A2.SS3.p3.m9 RAW[817129,817353) f_{\perp}

MATH A2.SS3.p3.m10 RAW[817359,817816) \operatorname{ran}(\mathsf{V}_{\perp\mathsf{P}})

MATH A2.SS3.p3.m11 RAW[817828,818308) \|f_{\mathsf{V}}\|\leq\|f_{\perp}\|

MATH A2.SS3.p3.m12 RAW[818323,818559) f_{\mathsf{V}}

than with this projection itself, because 
MATH A2.SS3.p3.m13 RAW[818610,818846) f_{\mathsf{V}}

MATH A2.SS3.p3.m14 RAW[818851,819087) f_{\mathsf{P}}

With this notation, let
