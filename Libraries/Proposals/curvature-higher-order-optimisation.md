# Proposal: Curvature-aware higher-order optimisation route target

- **Source:** *The Blessing and Curse of Curvature in Higher-Order Optimization: Horospherical versus Geodesic Convexity*
- **arXiv:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06719
- **Samplinglib status:** frontier route target / proposal
- **Primary routes:** `optimisation`, `riemannian-optimization`
- **Sampling role:** shared geometric and optimisation infrastructure; this proposal does **not** claim that the paper itself proves a sampling theorem.
- **Functor Hypergraph status:** conceptual candidate only; no Lean-certified functor or transport is claimed.

## Why this belongs in Samplinglib

The paper isolates a reusable geometric mechanism that sits exactly at the Optimisation–Riemannian Optimisation boundary and can be shared by future non-Euclidean sampling developments. Its main structural distinction is between strong geodesic convexity and strong horospherical convexity on Hadamard / hyperbolic geometry. The same negative-curvature divergence then creates opposite information-theoretic effects: horospherical support produces fast localization, while geodesic convexity alone permits exponentially many hidden minimizer directions.

For Samplinglib, the target is therefore not a one-off theorem import. The target is the underlying Lean graph: manifold calculus, global support objects, higher-order smoothness/Taylor control, curvature localization, and oracle lower-bound infrastructure should be reusable across optimisation, Riemannian optimisation, and later curved-state-space sampling proofs.

## Dependency-first Lean target spine

1. **Hadamard geometry floor**
   - exponential / logarithm maps;
   - unique minimizing geodesics;
   - parallel transport;
   - covariant derivatives and tensor operator norms;
   - squared-distance convexity and curvature-comparison lemmas.

2. **Horospherical geometry layer**
   - geodesic rays;
   - Busemann functions;
   - horospheres and horoballs;
   - hyperbolic distance identities needed for localization.

3. **Convex-support layer**
   - strong horospherical convexity;
   - strong geodesic convexity;
   - `strong h-convex => strong g-convex`;
   - Busemann minorant from strong h-convexity;
   - the induced gradient-defined horoball containing a minimizer.

4. **Higher-order model layer**
   - Lipschitz `p`-th covariant derivative;
   - Riemannian Taylor remainder;
   - Taylor-gradient model;
   - approximate regularized / proximal step;
   - relative-error matching through the dimensionless quantity `lambda * L_p * rho^(p-1)`.

5. **Acceleration layer**
   - intrinsic estimate-sequence / potential abstraction;
   - Busemann lower supports as the curved analogue of Euclidean affine lower supports;
   - growth of estimate weights `A_N` at the higher-order accelerated exponent;
   - strong-convexity restart and local superlinear phase.

6. **Negative-curvature blessing branch**
   - supporting horoball intersection geometry;
   - radius contraction in hyperbolic space;
   - localization from radius `R` to curvature scale `O(1/kappa)` in `O(log(1+kappa R))` queries;
   - replacement of `Q_p(R) = L_p R^(p-1)/mu` by the curvature-localized effective condition parameter.

7. **Negative-curvature curse branch**
   - exponential hyperbolic packing;
   - local smooth `p`-jet interpolation with controlled Hessian / higher derivative;
   - resisting-oracle transcript construction;
   - higher-order geodesically-convex oracle lower bound.

## Shared route ownership

### Optimisation route

Reuse / generalize:

- estimate sequences and acceleration invariants;
- strong-convexity restart;
- proximal / regularized subproblem abstractions;
- higher-order oracle models;
- deterministic resisting-oracle lower-bound infrastructure.

The key abstraction to expose is not “Euclidean linearity” itself, but:

> local high-quality step + global lower-support aggregation + coupling invariant.

This lets Euclidean affine minorants and Riemannian Busemann minorants become two realizations of one proof role rather than duplicated proofs.

### Riemannian Optimisation route

Reuse / extend:

- Hadamard manifold interfaces;
- geodesic convexity and strong convexity;
- covariant higher derivatives and Taylor expansions;
- Busemann / horoball geometry;
- curvature-dependent distortion and localization.

The paper should therefore sit as a frontier target downstream of the Riemannian textbook geometry spine, especially the general-manifold and geodesic-convexity material, without pretending that the textbook itself already contains these higher-order results.

### Samplinglib bridge

Expose only shared foundations that are genuinely reusable:

- global support abstractions for convex energies;
- manifold Taylor / smoothness infrastructure;
- curvature comparison and localization;
- metric/proximal energy primitives;
- oracle-transcript and information lower-bound abstractions.

A direct optimisation-to-sampling theorem must be added separately if and when proved. This route target must not manufacture such an implication.

## Functor Hypergraph proposals

### Candidate A — affine support to Busemann support

- **Source role:** Euclidean affine minorant / halfspace support.
- **Target role:** Busemann minorant / horoball support on Hadamard geometry.
- **Preserved proof role:** global lower support carrying first-order information into an estimate-sequence argument.
- **Failure boundary:** geodesic convexity alone does not automatically provide the Busemann support used by the h-convex proof.
- **Certification:** candidate correspondence only; identity/composition laws and Lean adapters are not yet certified.

Conceptual dictionary:

`hyperplane <-> horosphere`, `halfspace <-> horoball`, `affine function <-> Busemann function`, `affine minorant <-> Busemann minorant`.

### Candidate B — one curvature cause, two complexity branches

Common geometric node:

`negative-curvature divergence`

Blessing branch:

`negative-curvature divergence -> sharper horoball intersection -> curvature-scale localization -> smaller effective higher-order condition parameter`.

Curse branch:

`negative-curvature divergence -> exponential hyperbolic packing -> local p-jet hiding -> resisting oracle -> higher-order g-convex lower bound`.

This proposal is intentionally a **bifurcation hyperedge**: the same geometric cause has opposite algorithmic consequences depending on whether the function class supplies global horospherical support.
