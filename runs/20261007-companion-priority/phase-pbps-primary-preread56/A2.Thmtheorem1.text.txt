Lemma B.1 (Uniform macroscopic coercivity) . Assume \beta\eta\leq 1 , and set \rho_{\rm mac}\coloneqq\frac{1-\alpha\eta}{1+\alpha\eta}\,,\hskip 20.00003pt\gamma_{\rm gap}\coloneqq\sqrt{1-\rho_{\rm mac}^{2}}=\frac{2\sqrt{\alpha\eta}}{1+\alpha\eta}\,. (B.12) Note that \gamma_{\rm gap}\geq\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{\alpha\eta} . Then the following hold. (1) For every f\in L^{2}(\pi_{\eta}^{Y}) , \mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}f\in H^{1}(\pi_{\eta}^{Y}) and 4\eta\,\|\nabla\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}f\|^{2}\leq\|f\|^{2}-\|\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}f\|^{2}=\|\mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}f\|^{2}=\|\mathsf{U}_{\perp\mathsf{P}}f\|^{2}\,. (B.13) (2) Consider the mean-zero macroscopic subspace \mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0}\coloneqq\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P}}\cap L_{0}^{2}(\pi_{\eta}) . Then, it holds that \|\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\|\leq\rho_{\rm mac}\hskip 10.00002pt\text{and}\hskip 10.00002pt\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}\succeq-\frac{1}{2}\,I\hskip 20.00003pt\text{on }\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0}\,. (B.14) Consequently, \mathsf{\Gamma}_{\mathsf{P}}=(I-\mathsf{U}_{\mathsf{P}\mathsf{P}}^{2})^{1/2}\succeq\gamma_{\rm gap}I\hskip 20.00003pt\text{on }\mathfrak{H}_{\mathsf{P},0}\,. (B.15)
