    Signed derivative budgets on the unit interval

    AutoSamplingTheory.TechnicalLemmas.Analysis.FinitePowerSeries.abs_iteratedDeriv_finitePowerSeries_le_mass · theorem · Teaching coverage

    Read this first

    Mathematical statement

    On |u|≤1, every iterated derivative is bounded by its weighted absolute coefficient mass.

    \[|u|\le1\Longrightarrow |P^{(r)}(u)|\le M_r\]

    Proof roadmap

    1. 1Insert the exact derivative

      Rewrite the actual derivative as the descending-factorial finite sum.

    2. 2Apply the triangle inequality

      Discard cancellation and split absolute values across products.

    3. 3Use |u|≤1

      Every remaining power is at most one.

    Full assumptions and notation

    All objects and hypotheses

    • u lies in [-1,1].
    • The derivative order is arbitrary.
    • Coefficients may have arbitrary signs.

    Mathematical proof

    1. Insert the exact derivative

    Rewrite the actual derivative as the descending-factorial finite sum.

    \[P^{(r)}(u)=\sum_j j^{\underline r}a_j u^{j-r}\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    Use iteratedDeriv_finitePowerSeries.

    2. Apply the triangle inequality

    Discard cancellation and split absolute values across products.

    \[|P^{(r)}(u)|\le\sum_j j^{\underline r}|a_j||u|^{j-r}\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    Finset.abs_sum_le_sum_abs proves the finite-sum bound.

    3. Use |u|≤1

    Every remaining power is at most one.

    \[|u|^{j-r}\le1\Longrightarrow |P^{(r)}(u)|\le M_r\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    pow_le_one₀ completes the comparison.

    theorem abs_iteratedDeriv_finitePowerSeries_le_mass
    (order : ℕ) (support : Finset ℕ) (a : ℕ → ℝ) {u : ℝ}
    (hu : |u| ≤ 1) :
    |iteratedDeriv order (finitePowerSeries support a) u| ≤
      finitePowerSeriesDerivativeMass order support a := by
  rw [iteratedDeriv_finitePowerSeries]
  unfold finitePowerSeriesDerivative finitePowerSeriesDerivativeMass
  calc
    |∑ j ∈ support,
        (j.descFactorial order : ℝ) * a j * u ^ (j - order)| ≤
        ∑ j ∈ support,
          |(j.descFactorial order : ℝ) * a j * u ^ (j - order)| :=
      Finset.abs_sum_le_sum_abs _ _
    _ = ∑ j ∈ support,
        (j.descFactorial order : ℝ) * |a j| * |u| ^ (j - order) := by
      apply Finset.sum_congr rfl
      intro j hj
      rw [abs_mul, abs_mul, abs_pow,
        abs_of_nonneg (show 0 ≤ (j.descFactorial order : ℝ) by positivity)]
    _ ≤ ∑ j ∈ support, (j.descFactorial order : ℝ) * |a j| := by
      apply Finset.sum_le_sum
      intro j hj
      exact mul_le_of_le_one_right
        (mul_nonneg (by positivity) (abs_nonneg (a j)))
        (pow_le_one₀ (abs_nonneg u) hu)
