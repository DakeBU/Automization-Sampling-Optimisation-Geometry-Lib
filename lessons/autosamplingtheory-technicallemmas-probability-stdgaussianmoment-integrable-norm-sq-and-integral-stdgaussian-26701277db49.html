    The squared norm of a standard Gaussian has mean d

    AutoSamplingTheory.TechnicalLemmas.Probability.StdGaussianMoment.integrable_norm_sq_and_integral_stdGaussian · theorem · Teaching coverage

    Read this first

    Mathematical statement

    If Z is standard Gaussian in E, then its squared norm is Bochner-integrable and its expectation is the real dimension of E.

    \[\mathbb E\|Z\|^2=\dim_{\mathbb R}E.\]

    Proof roadmap

    1. 1Obtain genuine L2 integrability

      Mathlib proves that the identity random variable belongs to L2 under the standard Gaussian law. The L2-to-integrable-square equivalence therefore gives integrability of the squared norm before any coordinate sum is exchanged with the integral.

    2. 2Compute every directional variance

      For an orthonormal basis vector e_i, the covariance identity and zero Gaussian mean give E<e_i,Z>²=1. Each scalar square is dominated by ‖Z‖², so it is integrable.

    3. 3Apply finite-dimensional Parseval

      Parseval writes the squared norm as the finite sum of directional squares. Since every summand is integrable, the integral commutes with the finite sum and each term contributes one.

    Full assumptions and notation

    All objects and hypotheses

    • E is a complete finite-dimensional real inner-product space with its Borel sigma algebra.
    • The probability law is Mathlib's actual standard Gaussian stdGaussian E; no abstract covariance hypothesis replaces it.

    Mathematical proof

    1. Obtain genuine L2 integrability

    Mathlib proves that the identity random variable belongs to L2 under the standard Gaussian law. The L2-to-integrable-square equivalence therefore gives integrability of the squared norm before any coordinate sum is exchanged with the integral.

    \[Z\in L^2(\gamma_E)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \|Z\|^2\in L^1(\gamma_E).\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    IsGaussian.memLp_two_id and memLp_two_iff_integrable_sq_norm produce hi.

    2. Compute every directional variance

    For an orthonormal basis vector e_i, the covariance identity and zero Gaussian mean give E<e_i,Z>²=1. Each scalar square is dominated by ‖Z‖², so it is integrable.

    \[\mathbb E\langle e_i,Z\rangle^2=1,\qquad |\langle e_i,Z\rangle|^2\le\|Z\|^2.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    covarianceBilin_apply, covarianceBilin_stdGaussian and integral_id_stdGaussian prove hdir; norm_inner_le_norm supplies hL1.

    3. Apply finite-dimensional Parseval

    Parseval writes the squared norm as the finite sum of directional squares. Since every summand is integrable, the integral commutes with the finite sum and each term contributes one.

    \[\mathbb E\|Z\|^2=\sum_{i=1}^{d}\mathbb E\langle e_i,Z\rangle^2=\sum_{i=1}^{d}1=d.\]
    Corresponding Lean step

    stdOrthonormalBasis.sum_sq_norm_inner_right and integral_finsetSum close the calculation.

    theorem integrable_norm_sq_and_integral_stdGaussian
    {E : Type*} [NormedAddCommGroup E] [InnerProductSpace ℝ E]
    [CompleteSpace E] [FiniteDimensional ℝ E] [MeasurableSpace E] [BorelSpace E] :
    Integrable (fun x : E => ‖x‖ ^ 2) (stdGaussian E) ∧
      (∫ x : E, ‖x‖ ^ 2 ∂stdGaussian E) = Module.finrank ℝ E := by
  classical
  have hi : Integrable (fun x : E => ‖x‖ ^ 2) (stdGaussian E) :=
    (memLp_two_iff_integrable_sq_norm (by fun_prop)).1 IsGaussian.memLp_two_id
  let b := stdOrthonormalBasis ℝ E
  have hdir (i) : (∫ x : E, (inner ℝ (b i) x) ^ 2 ∂stdGaussian E) = 1 := by
    have hh := covarianceBilin_apply (μ := stdGaussian E)
      IsGaussian.memLp_two_id (b i) (b i)
    rw [covarianceBilin_stdGaussian] at hh
    change inner ℝ (b i) (b i) = _ at hh
    simpa [integral_id_stdGaussian, real_inner_self_eq_norm_sq,
      b.orthonormal.norm_eq_one, pow_two] using hh.symm
  have hL1 (i) : Integrable (fun x : E => (inner ℝ (b i) x) ^ 2)
      (stdGaussian E) := by
    apply hi.mono' (by fun_prop)
    filter_upwards with x
    rw [Real.norm_eq_abs, abs_of_nonneg (sq_nonneg _)]
    have hb := norm_inner_le_norm (𝕜 := ℝ) (b i) x
    rw [b.orthonormal.norm_eq_one, one_mul] at hb
    have hsq := (sq_le_sq₀ (norm_nonneg (inner ℝ (b i) x)) (norm_nonneg x)).2 hb
    simpa [Real.norm_eq_abs, sq_abs] using hsq
  refine ⟨hi, ?_⟩
  calc
    (∫ x : E, ‖x‖ ^ 2 ∂stdGaussian E) =
        ∫ x : E, ∑ i, (inner ℝ (b i) x) ^ 2 ∂stdGaussian E := by
      apply integral_congr_ae
      filter_upwards with x
      simpa [Real.norm_eq_abs, sq_abs] using (b.sum_sq_norm_inner_right x).symm
    _ = ∑ i, ∫ x : E, (inner ℝ (b i) x) ^ 2 ∂stdGaussian E :=
      integral_finsetSum _ (fun i _ => hL1 i)
    _ = Module.finrank ℝ E := by simp [hdir]
